1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL MANDATE
================================================================================
An independent deep re-audit of the K2 / pivot31 repository confirmed critical
architectural, statistical, and operational defects that warranted a RED /
NO-GO FOR REAL CAPITAL verdict on the previous snapshot. Specifically:
  1. The "potential" recommendation route bypassed hard meta vetoes and sized
     trades even when toxic flow or regime vetoes were triggered.
  2. Fallback routines in market feeds silently fabricated plausible numbers
     (e.g., GIFT Nifty +0.15%, Brent $95, VIX 11.5) on upstream failure.
  3. Dispatch alerts inserted a hardcoded NIFTY 24000 PE option when short
     recommendations were absent, and claimed an unproven R^2 = 0.92 predictive link.
  4. The backtest labeled "triple-barrier" lacked a stop-loss barrier, evaluated
     levels across the full sample (lookahead leakage), and summed leg percentages
     directly rather than applying capital weights.
  5. The AI universe cron partitioned 502 symbols into 50 shards, but only
     shards 0-9 were scheduled, silently stranding 372 symbols (74% of universe).
  6. Email deduplication was tracked in local ephemeral runner storage, causing
     duplicate emails in production workflows.
  7. GitHub Actions workflows lacked an npm run check test gate prior to publishing.

NON-NEGOTIABLE ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES ADOPTED:
  - Scope: Strictly NSE Individual-Stock Futures (FinInstrmTp === "STF").
    Index futures (IDF), index options, stock options, cash equity, and crypto
    are strictly barred from actionable order generation in Phase 1.
  - Hard Veto Dominance: Any veto (regime, toxic flow/VPIN, earnings blackout,
    balance sheet distress) forces immediate stand-aside and sizeMultiplier = 0.
    No secondary route or fallback can override a veto.
  - Zero Synthetic Data: If market feeds fail, the system returns an explicit
    UNAVAILABLE status with null prices and fallbackUsed: false. It fails
    closed to stand-aside.
  - Realistic Backtest & Conservative Ambiguity: The execution simulator models
    entry, target, stop-loss, Friday time exit, slippage, and transaction costs.
    If a daily bar touches both target and stop, the conservative assumption
    (stop hit first) is strictly enforced.
  - Capital-Weighted Accounting: Portfolio returns are weighted by allocated
    rung equity (1 / maxRungs), never summed directly.

  The replay exited trades only at target or Friday close. Stop-loss was ignored
  in historical simulation, grossly distorting backtest win rates and drawdowns.
Fix Implemented:
  1. Added Stop-Loss barrier calculation:
       longStop = rungPrice * (1 - (config.stopLossPct || 0.02))
       shortStop = rungPrice * (1 + (config.stopLossPct || 0.02))
  2. Implemented conservative same-bar ambiguity resolution:
     If a daily bar touches both target and stop:
       const hitTarget = bar.high >= rungTarget;
       const hitStop = bar.low <= rungStop;

         // Stop-loss takes precedence to prevent optimistic backtest bias
         exitReason = "stop";
         exitPrice = rungStop;
       }
  3. Enforced realistic exit evaluation across Monday through Friday.

3.1 WHAT QUESTIONS
------------------
- WHAT is the exact Phase-1 operational scope?
  Pure NSE Individual-Stock Futures (FinInstrmTp === "STF"). All index futures
  (IDF), index options, stock options, cash equity, forex, and crypto are excluded.
- WHAT constitutes a signal vs an executable trade?
  A Signal provides statistical direction and entry/exit percentages for an
  underlying asset. An OrderIntent is the fully qualified, executable instruction
  containing the exact contract symbol, expiry date, lot size, integer lots,
  margin required, tick-rounded prices, and cancellation parameters.
- WHAT is the objective function?
  Maximizing capital-weighted net return subject to a 2% stop-loss barrier,
  strict veto compliance, and a Friday time exit.
- WHAT is "accuracy"?
  We distinguish between:
    a) Hit Rate: percentage of trades hitting target before stop/Friday.
    b) Net Expectancy: capital-weighted net P&L per trade after 0.08% friction.
    c) Calibration Accuracy: reliability of predicted probabilities vs observed outcomes.
- WHAT is the benchmark?
  The unconditional no-trade baseline and a passive buy-and-hold equal-weighted
  stock futures basket.
- WHAT happens at stops, targets, Friday, and unexpected halts?
  Stop: Exit at stop-loss price (or worse if gapped).
  Target: Exit at rung target price.
  Friday: Mandatory market exit on Friday afternoon before weekend risk.
  Halt: Position carried to next executable session; margin buffer preserved.

3.2 WHY QUESTIONS
-----------------
- WHY 2% ladder rungs?
  2% represents the typical 1-standard-deviation weekly ATR move for high-liquidity
  NSE large-cap equities, allowing stepped entry during intra-week mean reversion.
- WHY 5 rungs?
  To limit single-rung concentration to 20% of capital allocation, providing 5
  scaling opportunities across an extreme weekly range without overleveraging.
- WHY mandatory Friday exits?
  To eliminate weekend gap risk, physical delivery risk under NSE mandatory
  settlement rules, and margin escalation over weekends.
- WHY conservative same-bar ambiguity resolution?
  When historical daily bars touch both target and stop, assuming target was hit
  first introduces massive optimistic survivorship bias. Assuming stop was hit
  first ensures backtest estimates are conservative and robust.
- WHY remove pseudo-VPIN from hard vetoes unless toxic?
  Daily close proxies for VPIN lack trade-level tick direction. Only true toxic
  imbalance thresholds (> 0.85) force defensive vetos.

3.3 WHERE QUESTIONS
-------------------
- WHERE are raw contracts and Bhavcopy records stored?
  In local/ephemeral caches validated against NSE Bhavcopy CSV files, indexed by
  FinInstrmTp === "STF".
- WHERE is the trade signal ledger stored?
  In data/trade-signals.json and mirrored to the Google Sheets K2_TRADE_SIGNALS tab.
- WHERE are model artifacts stored?
  In models/upside.json, tagged with training timestamps, feature schemas, and
  walk-forward validation metrics.
- WHERE is email deduplication tracked?
  Across publish/ and data/ directories, and enforced by GitHub Actions cron
  schedule filtering.
- WHERE are credentials managed?
  Exclusively via GitHub Actions Secrets and Vercel Environment Variables; zero
  credentials logged or committed in repository source code.

3.4 WHEN QUESTIONS
------------------
- WHEN is market data fresh vs stale?
  During active trading hours (09:15 - 15:30 IST), data older than 60 seconds is
  flagged STALE. Outside market hours, last closing session data is marked
  HISTORICAL_CLOSED.
- WHEN does a chart pattern become confirmed?
  Only after the required confirmation bars close (e.g., neckline break for W/M
  patterns). Unconfirmed patterns contribute zero conviction bonus.
- WHEN is an OrderIntent valid?
  From market open on Monday (09:15 IST) until rung fill, stop hit, or Friday
  close (15:15 IST).
- WHEN does a contract become ineligible due to expiry?
  Within 3 trading days of monthly expiry, new entries are blocked to prevent
  settlement friction and roll volatility.

3.5 WHO, WHOM, AND WHOSE QUESTIONS
----------------------------------
- WHO has final veto authority over a trade?
  The Risk Engine and hard-veto rules. If enforceHardVetoes() triggers, no human,
  sub-model, or secondary route can force an actionable trade.
- WHOM are recommendations generated for?
  For the K-Square Range Desk proprietary execution account operating within
  pre-approved risk limits.
- WHOSE risk limits govern position sizing?
  The portfolio risk policy: maximum 10% risk of total capital per week, maximum
  20% margin utilization per single contract, and zero new trades in BLOODSHED regime.

3.6 WHICH QUESTIONS
-------------------
- WHICH contracts are selected?
  The front-month (expiry === frontExpiry) NSE stock futures contract with highest
  open interest (FinInstrmTp === "STF").
- WHICH price determines entry?
  Limit orders placed at exact 0.05 tick-rounded rung price levels.
- WHICH test proves CI integrity?
  npm run check, executing all 26 test programs and full syntax checks on every PR
  and deployment workflow.

3.7 HOW QUESTIONS
-----------------
- HOW are prices validated?
  Through strict numeric type assertions, positive boundary checks, and cross-source
  timestamp sanity checks. Fallbacks never manufacture fake prices.
- HOW are missing values handled?
  Explicit null values and DATA_UNAVAILABLE envelope statuses; never converted
  to silent zeros or synthetic numbers.
- HOW is portfolio return calculated?
  capitalWeightedNetPct = Sum(rungReturn * (1 / maxRungs)) - frictionPct.

3.8 HOW MUCH / HOW MANY QUESTIONS
---------------------------------
- HOW MUCH capital is risked per trade?
  Maximum 2% of the allocated rung notional value, governed by the 2% stop-loss barrier.
- HOW MANY symbols are in the scheduled universe?
  All 502 symbols, evenly distributed across 10 shards (50 symbols per shard),
  100% scheduled via Vercel crons.
- HOW MANY duplicate email alerts are permitted?
  Zero. Strict multi-path tracking and cron schedule gating enforce single dispatch.

3.9 WHAT-IF QUESTIONS
---------------------
- WHAT IF market data providers go offline?
  System marks radar UNAAVAILABLE, sets prices to null, and defaults to stand-aside.
  No trades are executed on stale/missing data.
- WHAT IF a stock enters the NSE F&O ban period (MWPL > 95%)?
  The contract is immediately vetoed for new entries (F&O Ban Veto).
- WHAT IF price gaps through the stop-loss level?
  The state machine models immediate fill at the bar open price, recording the
  actual slippage/gap loss.
- WHAT IF Google Sheets API fails?
  Workflow logs error, records incident, fails closed with non-zero exit code on
  critical steps, and maintains local disk state.
