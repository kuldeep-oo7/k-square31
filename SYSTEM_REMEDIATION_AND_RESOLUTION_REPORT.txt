3.1 COMPLETE REMOVAL OF FORCED FRIDAY LIQUIDATION

Forced Friday liquidation was an arbitrary calendar constraint that closed winning

In api/_lib/ai-engine.js and scripts/test-ai-engine.js:

  - Zero exit orders are submitted; position quantity is 100% preserved over the weekend.
  - Market risk continues naturally across Saturday and Sunday.

3.2 THE 3-PRICE ACCOUNTING STRUCTURE
------------------------------------
Every open futures contract position is governed by three distinct prices:
1. originalEntryPrice:
   - The actual executed purchase price (including tick slippage).
   - Immutable across the lifetime of the position.
   - Used for lifetime economic P&L and tax calculation.
2. previousSettlementPrice:
   - Official daily mark-to-market settlement price from the previous session (NSE Clearing).
   - Used for daily MTM margin accounting.
3. weeklyReferencePrice:
   - The market opening price of the first tradable session of the new week (Monday 09:15 AM).
   - Used exclusively for weekly strategy performance reset.
   - Does NOT create an order, fill, or fictitious transaction.

3.3 WEEKEND PRICE GAP P&L ATTRIBUTION & RECONCILIATION
------------------------------------------------------
Weekend gap is explicitly isolated and retained:
  weekendGapPnl = (mondayOpen - fridaySettlement) * quantity

Weekly P&L:
  weeklyPnl = (currentPrice - weeklyReferencePrice) * quantity

Reconciliation Invariant:
  lifetimeGrossPnl = priorWeekPnl + weekendGapPnl + weeklyPnl

Example:
  Original Entry: 100, Friday Close: 104, Monday Open: 103, Current Monday Price: 106, Qty: 100.
  - Prior Week P&L  : (104 - 100) * 100 = +400
  - Weekend Gap P&L : (103 - 104) * 100 = -100
  - Current Week P&L: (106 - 103) * 100 = +300
  - Lifetime Gross  : +400 + (-100) + 300 = +600 (Exact match to (106 - 100) * 100)

5. COMPLETE WH-QUESTION RESOLUTION REGISTER
================================================================================
- WHAT is traded? Pure NSE Individual-Stock Futures (FinInstrmTp === "STF").
- WHAT happens on Friday? Position is marked to market; NO exit order is submitted.
- WHAT happens on Monday? Monday open becomes weeklyReferencePrice; weekly reporting
  resets; lifetime entry and cumulative equity remain unchanged.
- WHY was Friday forced exit removed? To prevent unnecessary churn, tax friction,
  and premature liquidation of viable multi-week trend positions.
- WHERE are contract notes stored? In data/shadow-ledger.json and publish/shadow-report.json.
- WHEN does a position exit? Only upon reaching Target Price, Stop-Loss Price,
  or during the mandatory 3-day pre-expiry contract roll window.
- WHO authorizes trades? Only structural ladder criteria passing hard risk vetoes;
  weak ML models cannot unilaterally trigger trades.
- HOW is friction accounted for? Real Indian regulatory fees (STT, GST, turnover,
  stamp duty, brokerage) plus tick slippage deducted from every exit.
