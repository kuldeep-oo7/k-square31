1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================================
The K² Range Desk is an algorithmic, quantitative derivatives trading decision
and risk management system built specifically for the National Stock Exchange of
India (NSE) Stock Futures market (216 active equity futures contracts).

Core Design Philosophy:
1. Pure Trading Execution (Not Buy-and-Hold Investing):
   The desk identifies short-to-medium term structural mispricings, order flow
   imbalances, and volatility anomalies in leveraged stock futures contracts.
2. Derivatives-First Architecture:
   All entry orders, targets, trailing defenses, margins, and lot sizing operate
   on official NSE F&O contracts with explicit expiry dates and contract lot sizes,
   rather than on cash equity shares.
3. Strict Evidence Gating:
   No indicator, candlestick label, or machine learning score is allowed to allocate
   capital unless verified out-of-sample, protected by hard risk vetoes, and backed
   by contemporaneous market microstructure (VPIN, Open Interest, Basis Carry).

6. ADVANCED QUANTITATIVE, DERIVATIVE & PHYSICS EQUATION ENGINE
================================================================================
The desk implements 10 advanced quantitative, institutional, and statistical formulas:

1. Black-Scholes-Merton PDE & 5 Greeks (`api/_lib/advanced-quant.js`):
   $$d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}}, \quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T}$$
   Outputs: Theoretical Price, Delta ($\Delta$), Gamma ($\Gamma$), Vega ($\nu$), Theta ($\Theta$), and Rho ($\rho$).

2. Hurst Exponent ($H$) via Rescaled Range ($R/S$) Analysis:
   $$E[R(n)/S(n)] = C \cdot n^H$$
   $H > 0.55$: Persistent / Trending; $H \approx 0.50$: Random Walk; $H < 0.45$: Mean-Reverting.

3. Kaufman Efficiency Ratio (ER):
   $$ER = \frac{|\text{Price}_t - \text{Price}_{t-n}|}{\sum_{i=1}^n |\text{Price}_i - \text{Price}_{i-1}|}$$
   Filters out choppy, directionless market noise.

4. Monday-Anchored Volume-Weighted Average Price (AVWAP):
   $$\text{AVWAP} = \frac{\sum (P_{\text{typical}} \times V)}{\sum V}$$
   Anchored strictly to Monday's 09:15 AM opening bar.

5. Institutional Open Interest (OI) Matrix:
   - Price $\uparrow$ + OI $\uparrow$ $\to$ **Long Buildup** (+15% conviction boost)
   - Price $\uparrow$ + OI $\downarrow$ $\to$ **Short Covering** (+5% conviction boost)
   - Price $\downarrow$ + OI $\uparrow$ $\to$ **Short Buildup** (Hard Veto on longs)
