1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & CURRENT MATURITY STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The K² Range Desk is a quantitative research, trading decision, and risk
management platform engineered for the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE)
Derivatives (F&O) universe (216 equities).

Current Maturity Level:
• Research Platform                     : ACTIVE & FUNCTIONAL
• Systematic Decision & Risk Desk       : ACTIVE (Gated by Multi-Layer Vetoes)
• Automated Execution / Algo Order Route: DISABLED (Manual Execution via GTT)
• Cross-Asset Generic Screener          : REJECTED (Tailored specifically for NSE F&O)

Governing Engineering Standard:
A mathematical formula or technical hypothesis enters production ONLY when:
  1. It possesses an underlying economic and microstructure rationale.
  2. It is implemented with exact numerical guards (no NaNs, no division by zero).
  3. It is verified in a chronological, leak-free framework (no lookahead bias).
  4. It enforces an uncompromising hard veto when risk or volatility fails.

3. CLOSED DEFECTS & REMEDIATION LOG (CRITICAL AUDIT FIXES APPLIED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Following an exhaustive independent code audit, all flagged defects have been
systematically resolved in the codebase:

[FIX 1] Generated Portal Inline Script Syntax Error:
  • Location: `scripts/build-portal.js` (line 557) & `site/index.html`.
  • Defect: A malformed `num()` helper in template string caused `missing ) after argument list`.
  • Remediation: Fixed `num()` syntax and added an AST-level inline script validator into
    `scripts/verify-portal.js`. CI will now refuse to publish if any embedded script fails.

[FIX 2] Meta-Labeling Hard Veto Leak:
  • Location: `api/_lib/recommendation.js` (line 328).
  • Defect: When `metaGate.decision === "VETO"`, `clamp(..., 0.25, 1.0)` forced a 25% size.
  • Remediation: Rewrote recommendation gate to enforce an immediate `stand-aside` on VETO
    and guaranteed `sizeMultiplier = 0` when rejected.

[FIX 3] Missing BSM Rho Greek & NaN Sensitivity:
  • Location: `api/_lib/advanced-quant.js` (`blackScholesMerton`).
  • Defect: Rho Greek was missing, and invalid/NaN time or volatility produced NaNs.
  • Remediation: Implemented analytical Rho ($\rho = \pm K \cdot T \cdot e^{-rT} \cdot N(\pm d2)$),
    added default bounded fallbacks for $T$ and $\sigma$, and added unit tests in `test-advanced-quant.js`.

[FIX 4] Trade Signal Ledger Deduplication Bug:
  • Location: `scripts/weekly-plan.js` (line 172) & `api/_lib/trade-tracker.js`.
  • Defect: Deduplication checked `rec.signalId`, but records were stored with `id`, causing
    `undefined === undefined` and blocking subsequent weekly records.
  • Remediation: Aligned `id` and `signalId` across modules and implemented composite deduplication.

[FIX 5] Daily Alert Dispatcher Hardcoding & Duplicate Spams:
  • Location: `scripts/dispatch-daily-alert.js` & `.github/workflows/portal.yml`.
  • Defect: Hardcoded stock names/prices, and workflow cron ran 16 times a day without session deduplication.
  • Remediation: Fully dynamic data ingestion from `gm-positions.json` (real holdings & trailing stops)
    and `plan-full.json` (real ladder entries), coupled with an automated IST session deduplicator.

[FIX 6] Quarantining Degraded ML Model:
  • Location: `models/upside.json` & `api/_lib/upside.js`.
  • Defect: Folds 3 and 4 showed negative Information Coefficient (IC < 0), yet model was marked skilled.
  • Remediation: Set `skilled: false` and quarantined the artifact. The system now safely
    bypasses the decayed model and relies on the validated weekly cycle.

[FIX 7] Portal Crypto PBKDF2 Iteration Standard:
  • Location: `api/_lib/portal-crypto.js` (line 15).
  • Defect: 210,000 iterations was below modern OWASP recommendations.
  • Remediation: Raised to 600,000 iterations (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 standard).

6. THE 10 ADVANCED QUANTITATIVE, PHYSICS & MICROSTRUCTURE FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) PDE & 5 Greeks:
    $C(S, t) = S \cdot N(d1) - K \cdot e^{-rT} \cdot N(d2)$
    $P(S, t) = K \cdot e^{-rT} \cdot N(-d2) - S \cdot N(-d1)$
    Greeks computed:
      • Delta ($\Delta$): Price sensitivity ($\partial V / \partial S$)
      • Gamma ($\Gamma$): Delta curvature ($\partial^2 V / \partial S^2$)
      • Vega ($\nu$): Volatility sensitivity ($\partial V / \partial \sigma$)
      • Theta ($\Theta$): Time decay per calendar day ($\partial V / \partial t$)
      • Rho ($\rho$): Interest rate sensitivity ($\partial V / \partial r$)
    Guarded against non-finite values and $T \to 0$.

[2] Hurst Exponent ($H$) via Rescaled Range (R/S) Analysis:
    $(R/S)_n = c \cdot n^H$
    • $H > 0.55$: Persistent trend (favorable for breakouts)
    • $H = 0.50$: Pure Brownian noise (stand aside)
    • $H < 0.45$: Anti-persistent / Mean-reverting (favorable for dip-buying ladders)

[3] Kaufman Adaptive Efficiency Ratio ($ER$):
    $ER = \frac{|\text{Price}_t - \text{Price}_{t-n}|}{\sum_{i=1}^n |\text{Price}_i - \text{Price}_{i-1}|}$
    Measures directional speed versus market friction. $ER > 0.40$ confirms clean trend flow.

[4] Fractional Differentiation ($d \approx 0.45$):
    $(1 - B)^d = \sum_{k=0}^\infty (-1)^k \binom{d}{k} B^k$
    Achieves mathematical stationarity while preserving $>55\%$ long-memory autocorrelation.

[5] VPIN (Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading):
    $\text{VPIN} = \frac{\sum_{\tau=1}^N |V_\tau^B - V_\tau^S|}{N \cdot V}$
    Microstructure order-flow toxicity proxy. $\text{VPIN} \ge 0.65$ triggers a hard toxic dump veto.

[6] Meta-Labeling Secondary AI Gate:
    Evaluates probability $P(\text{Win})$ given primary conviction, Hurst regime, ER, and VPIN.
    $P(\text{Win}) < 0.50 \implies \text{VETO} \implies \text{Size} = 0$.

[7] Half-Kelly Criterion & Merton Fraction:
    $f^* = \frac{p \cdot b - q}{b}, \quad \text{Applied Fraction} = 0.5 \cdot f^*$
    Bounded strictly between $5\%$ and $35\%$ of allocatable capital.

[8] Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) Mean Reversion:
    $dX_t = \theta (\mu - X_t) dt + \sigma dW_t, \quad t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln(2)}{\theta}$
    Calculates expected reversion half-life for range-bound assets.

[9] Anchored Volume-Weighted Average Price (AVWAP):
    $\text{AVWAP} = \frac{\sum (\text{TypicalPrice}_i \cdot \text{Volume}_i)}{\sum \text{Volume}_i}$
    Anchored to Monday 09:15 AM opening bar as weekly institutional benchmark.

[10] F&O Open Interest Buildup Classifier:
    Categorizes contracts into Long Buildup, Short Covering, Short Buildup, and Long Unwinding.


7. RISK CONTROLS, VETO GATES & GLOBAL MACRO THREAT SHIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The desk operates a 3-tier defense matrix:

Tier 1: Global Macro Shock Shield (`api/_lib/market-regime.js`)
• Evaluates Composite Global Macro Vulnerability Index (GMVI):
  - Normal    : Standard execution authorized.
  - Elevated  : Shallow buys (L1/L2) tightened; short setups boosted.
  - Bloodbath : GIFT Nifty gap < -1.5% OR VIX spike > 20% OR Crude spike > 5%.
    Action: Cancels all shallow buy entries; triggers Nifty put option crash hedge.

Tier 2: Microstructure & Technical Veto Gates (`api/_lib/recommendation.js`)
• Vetoes applied prior to capital release:
  1. 12-Month Trend Counter-Trade Veto: Reversing against severe trend blocked.
  2. VPIN Flow Toxicity Veto: Institutional dumping ($\text{VPIN} \ge 0.65$) blocked.
  3. Meta-Labeling Veto: Expected win probability $P < 0.50$ sets size to $0\%$.
  4. Balance Sheet Veto: High leverage / insolvency risk excluded.

Tier 3: Execution Discipline & 0.8% Buy Cap
• GTT Split: 50% placed at Entry Price, 50% placed at Max Buy Cap ($\text{Entry} \times 1.008$).
• Prevents retail FOMO chasing on morning gap-ups.

  - Tab `K2_ARCHIVE` logs immutable time-series data without overwriting prior records.
