1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The K² Range Desk is an institutional-grade, systematic quantitative trading
platform engineered specifically for the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE)
Derivatives (F&O) universe (216 stocks).

The system addresses three critical flaws common to retail and algorithmic trading:
  A. The Monday Anchor Principle: Rather than chasing daily noisy intraday
     signals, the desk fixes its structural price ladders, buy rungs (L1-L5),
     stop-losses, and profit targets anchored to Monday's opening session fair
     value.
  B. The Trillion Dollar Equation & Advanced Quant Gate: Every single setup must
     pass a 10-layer quantitative hurdle including Black-Scholes-Merton PDE,
     Hurst Exponent persistence (H > 0.55), Kaufman Efficiency (ER > 0.50),
     Fractional Differentiation stationarity, and VPIN toxicity rejection.
  C. Dynamic Daily Threat Shield: Real-time correlation monitoring of 9 global
     macro assets (GIFT Nifty, Brent Crude, US S&P/Nasdaq Futures, DXY, US 10Y
     Yield, and VIX) to protect open positions against overnight war shocks.

4. THE 10 ADVANCED QUANTITATIVE, PHYSICS & PROBABILITY FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) PDE & Greeks:
    - Formula:
        C(S, t) = S * N(d1) - K * e^(-r*T) * N(d2)
        d1 = [ln(S/K) + (r + σ²/2)T] / (σ * √T)
        d2 = d1 - σ * √T
    - Greeks: Delta (Δ), Gamma (Γ), Vega (ν), Theta (Θ), Rho (ρ).
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> calculateBsmOption()
    - Safety: Guards against T -> 0 and σ -> 0 with Math.max() lower bounds.

[2] Hurst Exponent (H) via Rescaled Range (R/S) Analysis:
    - Formula: (R/S)_n = c * n^H
    - Interpretation:
        • H > 0.55 : Persistent / Trending time-series (Strong momentum)
        • H = 0.50 : Random walk / Brownian noise (Reject trade)
        • H < 0.45 : Mean-reverting / Choppy regime
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> calculateHurstExponent()

[3] Kaufman Adaptive Efficiency Ratio (ER):
    - Formula: ER = |Price_t - Price_{t-n}| / Σ |Price_i - Price_{i-1}|
    - Range: 0.0 (Pure noise/friction) to 1.0 (Pure directional efficiency).
    - Filter: Setups require ER > 0.50 to clear the noise hurdle.
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> calculateKaufmanER()

[4] Fractional Differentiation (Preserving Memory while Ensuring Stationarity):
    - Formula: (1 - B)^d = Σ_{k=0}^∞ w_k * B^k, where w_k = (-1)^k * (d choose k)
    - Optimal Parameter: d ≈ 0.45
    - Property: Removes non-stationarity without obliterating multi-year memory
      (retains 55%+ autocorrelation memory).
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> fractionalDifferentiation()

[5] VPIN (Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading):
    - Formula: VPIN = (Σ |V_τ^B - V_τ^S|) / (N * V)
    - Thresholds: VPIN < 0.30 (Normal), VPIN > 0.70 (Toxic institutional dumping).
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> calculateVpin()

[6] Meta-Labeling Secondary AI Gate:
    - Method: Secondary logistic sigmoid classifier evaluating primary model
      trade conviction, Hurst persistence, ER efficiency, and VPIN toxicity.
    - Output: Win probability P(Win). Vetoes false breakout signals (P < 0.55)
      and scales position tickets (multiplier clamped between 0.25 and 1.0).
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> metaLabelConfidence()

[7] Kelly Criterion & Merton's Capital Fraction:
    - Formula: f* = (p * b - q) / b
    - Desk Rule: Safe Half-Kelly fraction (0.5 * f*), strictly bounded between
      5% and 35% of allocatable equity to prevent Gambler's Ruin.
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> calculateKellyFraction()

[8] Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) Mean Reversion Process:
    - SDE: dX_t = θ * (μ - X_t) * dt + σ * dW_t
    - Half-life: t_{1/2} = ln(2) / θ
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> calculateOrnsteinUhlenbeck()

[9] Anchored Volume-Weighted Average Price (AVWAP):
    - Formula: AVWAP = Σ (TypicalPrice_i * Volume_i) / Σ Volume_i
    - Anchor: Monday 09:15 AM IST opening bar. Serves as weekly fair-value pivot.
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> calculateAnchoredVwap()

[10] F&O Open Interest Buildup Classifier:
    - Logic:
        • Price Up & OI Up     --> LONG BUILDUP (Bullish continuation)
        • Price Up & OI Down   --> SHORT COVERING (Temporary rally)
        • Price Down & OI Up   --> SHORT BUILDUP (Aggressive shorting)
        • Price Down & OI Down --> LONG UNWINDING (Bull liquidation)
    - Code: api/_lib/advanced-quant.js -> classifyOiBuildup()


5. RISK MANAGEMENT, GLOBAL MACRO SHIELD & VPIN TOXICITY GATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The desk implements a 3-tier hierarchical defense system:

Tier 1: The Global Macro Shock Shield (api/_lib/market-regime.js)
  - Computes the Composite Global Macro Vulnerability Index (GMVI):
    • Severe Bloodbath Alert: GIFT Nifty gap < -1.5% OR VIX spike > 20% OR
      Brent crude spike > 5%.
    • Action: All shallow buy entries (L1/L2) are dynamically vetoed. The engine
      shifts execution solely to deep capitulation discounts (L4/L5 at -4% to -6%)
      and boosts short setups (SUPREMEIND) by +35%.

Tier 2: Intraday Order Flow Toxicity (VPIN Gate)
  - If a stock experiences a massive institutional block dump mid-week, VPIN
    surges above 0.70, triggering an automatic toxic dumping veto.

Tier 3: The 0.8% Buy Cap & 2-Order GTT Split
  - Prevents chasing gap-ups. Max Buy Cap = Entry Price * 1.008.
  - Traders place 50% capital at Entry Price and 50% at Max Buy Cap, ensuring
    favorable fills on shallow morning dips without buying at market highs.

9. AUDITOR WH-QUESTION CHECKLIST & EDGE-CASE SAFETY PROTOCOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Does the system suffer from lookahead bias?
A1: No. In scripts/test-weekly-cycle.js and api/_lib/weekly-cycle.js, every
    simulation uses triple-barrier labeling where bars are replayed chronologically.
    No same-bar exits or Friday afternoon fills are permitted.

Q2: What happens if volatility drops to zero or prices are flat?
A2: Protected. BSM formulas clamp volatility to Math.max(0.01, sigma). Hurst
    exponent and Kaufman ER check for varianceSum > 0 and totalVolatility > 0
    before running divisions, preventing NaN or division-by-zero crashes.

Q3: Can a single high-conviction trade over-allocate capital?
A3: No. Sizing uses Half-Kelly bounded strictly to a maximum 35% ticket cap.
    Additionally, api/_lib/recommendation.js clamps the overall ticket multiplier
    within [0.25, 1.0].

Q4: Can an overnight market crash wipe out open trades?
A4: No. The Global Macro Threat Radar monitors GIFT Nifty and US futures at 8:30 AM
    IST. In a bloodbath, it issues an immediate morning alert instructing traders
    to tighten stops to Breakeven (₹0 risk) or buy Nifty Put Option hedges.
