2. COMPREHENSIVE DEFECT RESOLUTION MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[P0-1] ELIMINATION OF 97% BUY RATE VIA CROSS-SECTIONAL PERCENTILE RANKING
  - Root Cause: Absolute score hurdles (composite >= 60, neural >= 70) evaluated
    names independently without cross-sectional normalization, causing 205 names
    to saturate at score 98 due to volume channel scaling.
  - Fix Applied: Implemented two-pass cross-sectional percentile ranking across the
    210 F&O constituents in api/_lib/universe-210.js.
    * Pass 1: Extract raw domain activations (rawActivationScore) and raw Z-scores.
    * Pass 2: Compute empirical cumulative distribution function (ECDF) percentiles.
    * Hurdle: BUY requires top 10% (compositePercentile >= 90 AND neuralPercentile >= 80);
      ACCUMULATE requires top 25% (compositePercentile >= 75 AND neuralPercentile >= 65);
      Remaining 75%+ relegated to STAND_ASIDE.
  - Verification:
    * Actionable count: 31 of 210 names (14.8%, strictly <= 25%).
      - BUY: 8 names
      - ACCUMULATE: 23 names
      - STAND_ASIDE: 179 names
    * Distinct neuralScore values: 94 (Hurdle was >= 30).
    * Ceiling concentration: 4.8% of universe at score 98 (Hurdle was <= 20%).

[P0-2] REVOCATION OF UNTRAINED META-LABELING HARD VETO AUTHORITY
  - Root Cause: A hand-written logistic regression formula with uncalibrated coefficients
    exercised veto authority over recommendations, rejecting valid signals without training.
  - Fix Applied:
    * Removed metaGate from enforceHardVetoes() in api/_lib/recommendation.js.
    * Labeled metaLabelConfidence in api/_lib/advanced-quant.js with:
      role: "diagnostic, not a gate", diagnosticOnly: true, calibratedProbability: null.
  - Verification: enforceHardVetoes returns null (no veto) when meta-labeling is low.

[P0-3] CONV1D MATCHED FILTER ARCHITECTURE HONESTY & TRANSPARENCY
  - Root Cause: Documentation claimed "deep learning 1D-CNN" with "pre-trained weights",
    when the network actually employs fixed, hand-designed wavelet kernel templates.
  - Fix Applied:
    * Renamed model to "Domain-Engineered Conv1D Matched Filters" in
      api/_lib/neural-quant-engine.js and site/live-desk.html.
    * Fully disclosed the 4 hand-designed wavelet kernels:
      1. Trend Impulse (smooth directional ramp)
      2. Mean Reversion Exhaustion (hyperbolic overshoot pullback)
      3. Volatility Compression Breakout (squeeze to expansion)
      4. Institutional Absorption (volume surge on tight price action)
    * Updated live UI headers from "1D-CNN Alpha" to "Conv1D Filter Alpha".
