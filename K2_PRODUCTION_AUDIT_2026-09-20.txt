PART 3 — A FIFTH OF THE SCORE WAS TWO CONSTANTS
================================================================================

The composite published these weights:

    neural .35   candle .15   chart .10   hurst .15   vpin .10   volBand .15

Measured across all 210 names, two of those terms take the SAME VALUE for every
single symbol:

    chart  = 50 for all 210    no symbol reports a dominant pattern
    vpin   = 50 for all 210    VPIN is not implemented and returns null,
                               and null < 0.25 is false

A constant adds ten points to every score and ranks nothing. Twenty per cent of
the stated weighting was decoration. This is the defect this repository keeps
producing in new clothes: a number describing the system that does not describe
the system.

The formula also BINARISED continuous evidence:

    ((hurst > 0.55 ? 85 : 50) * 0.15)

Hurst carried 160 distinct values across the universe and was collapsed onto two
of them. So 0.549 and 0.843 scored identically, while 0.549 and 0.551 scored 35
points apart — a hard boundary driven through the middle of a noisy estimate.
Volatility regime had the same shape: one favoured bucket, everything else
flattened together.

FIXED. Dead terms carry no weight instead of a hidden constant. Hurst maps
continuously over [0.35, 0.75]. Regime keeps its ordering. Weights are now
neural .35, candle .15, hurst .25, garchRegime .25, and the two inactive
components are declared by name with the reason, so nothing can silently claim
to contribute again.

THIS IS A DEFECT FIX, NOT A VALIDATED IMPROVEMENT. Removing dead weight does not
create an edge. See Part 6.

PART 3A — THE OPTIONS WERE PRICED AGAINST THE WRONG INSTRUMENT
================================================================================

The largest correctness defect found in this system to date, and it produced no
error, no crash and no failing test.

THE DESK PRICED EVERY OPTION AGAINST THE CASH EQUITY INSTEAD OF THE MARKET'S
FORWARD.

A precision note, cross-checked against NSE rather than assumed: single stock
options in India are PHYSICALLY SETTLED into shares (mandatory since October
2019), so they are NOT options on the future. The reason the future matters is
different and more general: Black-Scholes prices off the FORWARD, and for an F&O
name the market publishes its forward as the futures price. An assumed
cost-of-carry from spot does not reproduce it -- for APOLLOHOSP, spot at r 6.5%
and q 0 implies a forward of 8961 while the market's own forward is 8870, a
91-point gap made of dividends, borrow cost and futures basis.

MEASURED ON APOLLOHOSP, 2026-09-20. Cash share 8944.38. Future 8865. A gap of
0.90%.

    strike   premium    IV on the cash share    IV on the future
      8800    127.45    would not solve at all           13.04%
      8900     75.95                    6.02%            13.74%
      9000     40.35                    9.24%            14.03%
      9100     20.50                   10.96%            14.57%
      9200     11.45                   12.70%            15.70%

At the money the error is 7.72 volatility points. On the cash share the smile
ramps 6% to 13% across four strikes, which is not a shape any real option market
makes; on the future it is flat and ordinary, and the in-the-money strike that
would not solve at all solves cleanly.

THE MARKET HAD BEEN TELLING US THE RIGHT NUMBER. Put-call parity recovers the
forward with no model at all, F = K + (C - P)e^(rT), and three different strikes
agreed: 8869.44, 8871.19, 8870.10, against a cash share of 8944.38. The options
themselves knew. Nobody asked them.

ACROSS THE UNIVERSE the cash share is off by a median of 0.77% and up to 3.07%,
and THE SIGN FLIPS PER NAME — COFORGE -2.50%, RELIANCE +1.22%, TATAPOWER -1.79%.
So it was not a constant bias that cancels in a ranking. It distorted every
symbol differently and therefore distorted the ranking BETWEEN them.

HOW IT SHOWED UP, AND WHY IT WAS NEARLY MISSED. The demo run for the user put
five names at the top and ALL FIVE came back CHEAP. Across the whole universe
the verdicts read CHEAP 154 against EXPENSIVE 122 — implied BELOW realized,
which is the reverse of how option markets behave and the reason option sellers
win on average. Every individual number looked plausible. Only the DISTRIBUTION
gave it away.

    THE GENERAL LESSON: when a verdict is lopsided across an entire universe,
    suspect the input, not the market.

FIXED, AND THE DATA IS NOW LIVE. The Angel instrument master was filtered to
equities and options and discarded futures entirely, which is why the desk had
been reaching for the end-of-day bhavcopy for a forward. It now captures 1,305
futures contracts and quotes them live in batches of 50.

    LIVE_BROKER_FUTURE         208 of 210 cards
    EXCHANGE_SETTLED_FUTURE    the 2 indices, which have no stock future
    CASH_SPOT_NO_FUTURE        last resort, labelled

Verdicts now read RICH 98, FAIR 160, CHEAP 102, EXPENSIVE 60 — implied above
realized, as it should be — with implied volatility spanning 13.6% to 85%,
median 23.5%, instead of bottoming out at 6%.

THREE SMALLER INPUT ERRORS FOUND IN THE SAME PASS:

  - The realized-volatility window did not match the option's tenor. A 20-day
    BACKWARD estimate was compared with a 9-day FORWARD implied volatility and

  - Implied volatility was solved at the wrong time. A settled premium struck on

PART 3C — PHYSICAL SETTLEMENT: THE RISK THAT DWARFS THE TRADE
================================================================================

Found by cross-checking NSE's own rules rather than reasoning from the code, and
it is the largest single-trade risk on this desk.

SINCE OCTOBER 2019, IN-THE-MONEY SINGLE STOCK OPTIONS IN INDIA ARE SETTLED BY
DELIVERY OF SHARES, NOT CASH. Index options remain cash settled.

An in-the-money long call carried into expiry is therefore not a claim on a
profit. It is an obligation to pay for the entire lot.

    APOLLOHOSP 8900/9000 spread
      net debit paid                    Rs     4,450
      delivery obligation if carried    Rs 11,12,500      250x the trade

A short leg is worse still: an obligation to DELIVER shares that may not be
held, which brings auction and penalty risk on top.

The desk already said "be flat 2 days before expiry" but justified it only as a
gamma convention, which understates it by two orders of magnitude. Every spread
card now carries the settlement obligation in rupees, and the time stop is
labelled CONVENTION_PLUS_SETTLEMENT_RULE, because the gamma half is a preference
and the settlement half is an exchange rule.

FOR THE USER, PLAINLY: never hold an in-the-money stock option into expiry
unless you intend to take or make delivery and have the cash to do it.

PART 3B — THE CANDLESTICK ENGINE HAD THE SIGN INVERTED
================================================================================

Read as a Japanese technician would read it, not as a programmer.

A long lower shadow is a HAMMER after a decline and a HANGING MAN after a rally.
The same geometry, opposite meanings, told apart ONLY by what came before.
Likewise a long upper shadow is a SHOOTING STAR after a rally and an INVERTED
HAMMER after a decline. This is not a refinement of the patterns. It is their
definition.

The engine matched on shape alone, with no prior trend anywhere in the file.

MEASURED ACROSS 210 SYMBOLS:

    SHOOTING_STAR detections, labelled BEARISH        23
      ...of which followed a DOWNTREND                22   <-- Inverted Hammers,
                                                            classically BULLISH
      ...of which followed an UPTREND                  1   <-- genuine

    HAMMER detections, labelled BULLISH                3
      ...of which followed an UPTREND                  1   <-- Hanging Man,
                                                            classically BEARISH

The sign was wrong on 96% of the second most common pattern the engine emits.
That matters because candlesticks carry 15% of the composite weight and are one
of only two components with continuous resolution, so an error there moves the
ranking directly.

FIXED. The prior trend is measured over the conventional five-session look-back
and the pattern is named and biased accordingly. Where the prior trend is flat
or unknown the pattern is reported as a rejection with a NEUTRAL bias and
reduced reliability rather than guessing a direction — a pattern read without
its context is worth less than one read with it, and should say so. The same 210
symbols now report 22 INVERTED_HAMMER (bullish) and 1 SHOOTING_STAR (bearish).

PART 3D — WHAT THE SECOND AUDIT FOUND, AFTER THE FIRST WAS WRITTEN
================================================================================

Each of these was found by a different method, and that is the point: none of
them would have surfaced from reading the code.

3D.1  REALIZED VOLATILITY WAS BLIND TO THE OVERNIGHT GAP
      Parkinson uses only the intraday high-low range, so it cannot see the move
      from yesterday's close to today's open -- and on Indian equities that gap
      is a large share of the actual movement. Across all 210 symbols on a
      20-day window it came in BELOW Yang-Zhang on 184 of them (88%), by a
      median of 8.8%.

      That biases every verdict, because this desk divides implied by realized:
      understating the denominator by 8.8% inflates the ratio by about 9.6% and
      tilts the universe toward EXPENSIVE.

      CONFIRMED BY SIMULATION AGAINST A KNOWN VOLATILITY, which is the test that
      does not depend on anyone's expectations:

          true vol   gap share   Yang-Zhang   Parkinson
            20%         0%         17.6%       17.8%
            20%        40%         18.6%       13.8%    31% too low
            30%        60%         28.6%       16.8%    44% too low

      Yang-Zhang sums the overnight move, the open-to-close move and
      Rogers-Satchell for the intraday range, making it gap-aware AND
      drift-independent. It is now the primary estimator.

3D.2  THE CHANCE OF REACHING THE TARGET WAS OPTIMISTIC
      P(touch) used the reflection principle as 2*N(-b/(sigma*sqrt(T))). That is
      the result for driftless ARITHMETIC Brownian motion. A price is geometric,
      and a martingale price carries log-drift of -sigma^2/2 -- so the old form
      quietly assumed the stock drifts UP at +sigma^2/2 and promised a better
      chance of reaching target than the mathematics supports. On a 30-day,
      25-vol, 5% barrier it read 49.60% where the truth is 48.40%.

      VERIFIED TWO WAYS. Analytically, forcing mu to zero reproduces the old
      formula exactly (49.6039 against 49.6039), so the general form contains
      the special case. Numerically, Monte Carlo converges onto the new one as
      the barrier is monitored more finely: 44.91% at 100 steps, 46.91% at 500,
      47.77% at 2,000, 48.17% at 8,000, against a closed form of 48.40%. The gap
      closes as one over root steps, the signature of discretisation in the
      SIMULATION rather than error in the formula.

      A NEAR MISS WORTH RECORDING. The first Monte Carlo returned 7.1% and then
      0.0% at high step counts, which is impossible. The cause was a linear
      congruential generator cycling under about a billion draws -- the same
      PRNG class that produced a variance of 1.013 in the covariance tests
      earlier in this project. Trusting it would have "disproved" a formula that
      was right. A verification is only as sound as its random numbers.

3D.3  NIFTY IMPLIED VOLATILITY WAS 8% AGAINST AN INDIA VIX OF 12.4
      Caught by comparing against a number already on the user's own screen.
      NIFTY OPTIONS EXPIRE WEEKLY -- the front contract was 22-Sep -- while the
      code used the monthly expiry, roughly doubling the tenor and pushing
      implied volatility down by about a factor of root two. Corrected to the
      listed expiry: 1.7 days, IV 13.1%, which is where a short-dated weekly
      belongs relative to a 30-day composite.

      NSE's published methodology confirms the comparison is sound: India VIX is
      annualised, 30-day, reverse Black-Scholes from out-of-the-money quotes --
      the same construction, so the two are comparable, though VIX is a
      composite across strikes and no single contract should match it exactly.

3D.4  THE INDEX CARDS HAD NO EXITS AND NO ODDS
      NIFTY and BANKNIFTY are projected on their own code path, which was never
      updated when exits and odds were added. The desk was saying BUY NIFTY
      23350 CE at Rs 88.05 with NO SELL PRICE AND NO STOP -- the exact complaint
      that began this work, surviving on the two most prominent cards on the
      page. Both now carry the full block, and odds from the index's own bars
      (Yahoo publishes them as ^NSEI and ^NSEBANK, which match the spots the
      desk already displayed).

3D.5  NIFTY WEEKLIES HAVE NO MATCHING FUTURE
      Index futures are MONTHLY; NIFTY options are WEEKLY. A 22-Sep option has
      no 22-Sep future, so the underlying waterfall fell through to the cash
      index -- repeating the error of Part 3A on the instrument where the basis
      is largest. Put-call parity supplies the answer with no model at all:
      F = K + (C-P)e^(rT), taken as the median across near-the-money pairs. It
      cross-validates where both exist (APOLLOHOSP parity 8870.15 against a
      quoted future of 8865) and now sits above the cash fallback.

3D.6  THREE FAULTS VISIBLE ONLY ON THE DEPLOYED SITE
      - The bar ingest ran ONCE per container. runCycle only advances its cursor
        and reset() was called from nowhere, so a symbol whose fetch failed on
        that single pass stayed synthetic for the life of the process. On Render
        159 of 210 failed, and 306 of 423 LIVE CARDS were served with no
        probability, no implied volatility and no verdict. Nothing errored.
      - The instrument master outlived the code reading it. Adding futures did
        nothing in production for a full deploy cycle because the container kept
        serving a cache written before futures existed. Now schema-versioned --
        and a stale cache DEGRADES rather than reading as absent, because the
        first version of that fix would have left the desk with no option chains
        at all when the 35 MB refresh failed to complete.
      - pricingBasis reached the call leg twice and the put leg never.

3D.7  DEAD INTEGRATIONS REMOVED
      Dhan feed, TradingView quotes and GM-readonly are deleted: the account has
      no Dhan, TradingView's public API cannot serve options at all (verified by
      test, HTTP 400), and the GM path was ordered removed. UPSTOX is kept but
      MARKED UNSUPPORTED with the reason in its status -- it sends no exchange
      feed time, so under this desk's own freshness contract its quotes can
      never be REALTIME and can never reach a card, whatever credentials it is
      given. Reporting only NOT_CONFIGURED invited somebody to waste an evening
      on it.

      NOTE: the Dhan ONE-TAP ORDER LINK on the cards is untouched. It is a deep
      link into the user's broker app and has nothing to do with the data feed.

PART 6 — THE THING THAT CAN HARM YOU MOST
================================================================================

Not a crash. Not a wrong price. Those are fixed and tested.

THE SIGNAL HAS NO MEASURED EDGE, AND EVERYTHING ELSE BEING EXCELLENT MAKES THAT
MORE DANGEROUS, NOT LESS.

    Pre-registered out-of-sample test: 112 symbols never touched in
    development, 8,893 fills, +0.0422% per fill, t = 1.25 against a
    1.6648 hurdle. FAILED.

    By year: 2023 +0.1358% (the peak), 2026 -0.1752% at t = -2.10.
    Negative, and significant, in the most recent year.

    0 of 24 ladder geometries survived validation.

    P(profit) across all 423 option cards: 23% to 44.6%, median 35%.
    NOT ONE CARD IS ABOVE 45%.

That last line deserves reading twice. Every card on this desk, priced from real
books with exact mathematics, has the odds against it. That is not a flaw in the
desk — it is the honest arithmetic of buying options, where you must clear the
strike AND the premium AND the time decay.

The risk is psychological, and it is specific: a system that prices correctly,
refuses wide spreads, states probabilities, and shows a clean audit trail LOOKS
authoritative. The quality of the machinery is easily mistaken for evidence
about the selection. It is not. The machinery tells you exactly what a contract
is worth. It does not tell you that buying it makes money.

WHAT TO DO ABOUT IT: the paper ledger now records every signal at the price
shown, marks it against observed prices, and reports what it actually earned. It
also stamps each signal with the P(profit) and cheap/expensive verdict it
carried AT ISSUE — so in a month you can ask not only "did it win" but "were the
probabilities calibrated", which is the better question. Let it run. Trade
nothing on the signal until it has an answer.
