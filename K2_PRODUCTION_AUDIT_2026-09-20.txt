2.1 THE EARNINGS VETO HAD NEVER FIRED, FOR ANY NAME, EVER

    const earningsVeto = fund?.events?.insideTradingWeek === true;

    Correct code. But .private/cache/fundamentals/ did not exist, so
    getCachedFundamentals returned null for all 210 symbols and the expression
    was undefined every time.

    WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN IT LOOKS. Buying premium into an earnings
    announcement is the one setup where being RIGHT ABOUT DIRECTION STILL LOSES.
    Implied volatility is bid up before the event and collapses the moment the
    number is out. The stock can move your way and the option still falls,
    because you paid for volatility that has just been extinguished. A desk that
    recommends a long call the day before results, with no veto, is handing you
    the worst trade in options.

    FIXED: the engine now fetches fundamentals at boot and every twelve hours,
    paced in batches of eight. Verified live: 210 of 210 fetched, 0 failures.
    The veto is armed. It fires on nothing this week, which is the correct
    answer for this week, not a broken one.

PART 3 — A FIFTH OF THE SCORE WAS TWO CONSTANTS
================================================================================

The composite published these weights:

    neural .35   candle .15   chart .10   hurst .15   vpin .10   volBand .15

Measured across all 210 names, two of those terms take the SAME VALUE for every
single symbol:

    chart  = 50 for all 210    no symbol reports a dominant pattern
    vpin   = 50 for all 210    VPIN is not implemented and returns null,
                               and null < 0.25 is false

A constant adds ten points to every score and ranks nothing. Twenty per cent of
the stated weighting was decoration. This is the defect this repository keeps
producing in new clothes: a number describing the system that does not describe
the system.

The formula also BINARISED continuous evidence:

    ((hurst > 0.55 ? 85 : 50) * 0.15)

Hurst carried 160 distinct values across the universe and was collapsed onto two
of them. So 0.549 and 0.843 scored identically, while 0.549 and 0.551 scored 35
points apart — a hard boundary driven through the middle of a noisy estimate.
Volatility regime had the same shape: one favoured bucket, everything else
flattened together.

FIXED. Dead terms carry no weight instead of a hidden constant. Hurst maps
continuously over [0.35, 0.75]. Regime keeps its ordering. Weights are now
neural .35, candle .15, hurst .25, garchRegime .25, and the two inactive
components are declared by name with the reason, so nothing can silently claim
to contribute again.

THIS IS A DEFECT FIX, NOT A VALIDATED IMPROVEMENT. Removing dead weight does not
create an edge. See Part 6.

PART 6 — THE THING THAT CAN HARM YOU MOST
================================================================================

Not a crash. Not a wrong price. Those are fixed and tested.

THE SIGNAL HAS NO MEASURED EDGE, AND EVERYTHING ELSE BEING EXCELLENT MAKES THAT
MORE DANGEROUS, NOT LESS.

    Pre-registered out-of-sample test: 112 symbols never touched in
    development, 8,893 fills, +0.0422% per fill, t = 1.25 against a
    1.6648 hurdle. FAILED.

    By year: 2023 +0.1358% (the peak), 2026 -0.1752% at t = -2.10.
    Negative, and significant, in the most recent year.

    0 of 24 ladder geometries survived validation.

    P(profit) across all 423 option cards: 23% to 44.6%, median 35%.
    NOT ONE CARD IS ABOVE 45%.

That last line deserves reading twice. Every card on this desk, priced from real
books with exact mathematics, has the odds against it. That is not a flaw in the
desk — it is the honest arithmetic of buying options, where you must clear the
strike AND the premium AND the time decay.

The risk is psychological, and it is specific: a system that prices correctly,
refuses wide spreads, states probabilities, and shows a clean audit trail LOOKS
authoritative. The quality of the machinery is easily mistaken for evidence
about the selection. It is not. The machinery tells you exactly what a contract
is worth. It does not tell you that buying it makes money.

WHAT TO DO ABOUT IT: the paper ledger now records every signal at the price
shown, marks it against observed prices, and reports what it actually earned. It
also stamps each signal with the P(profit) and cheap/expensive verdict it
carried AT ISSUE — so in a month you can ask not only "did it win" but "were the
probabilities calibrated", which is the better question. Let it run. Trade
nothing on the signal until it has an answer.
