The K² ladder

Rolling rangeR = 31-day high − 31-day low
MidpointM = (high + low) ÷ 2

Buy 1–3 descend from the midpoint toward the low. Sell 1–3 rise toward the high. Levels 4–5 extend beyond the observed range by max(10% × R, 0.75 × ATR₁₄), capped at 20% of R.

ATR measures recent daily movement. This makes wider-moving stocks use wider steps while keeping the result bounded and comparable.

Example: low ₹100 · high ₹125Midpoint ₹112.50 · range ₹25Buy 1–3: ₹108.75 · ₹105 · ₹101.25Buy 4–5 and Sell 4–5 extend outside ₹100–₹125 using ATR.
