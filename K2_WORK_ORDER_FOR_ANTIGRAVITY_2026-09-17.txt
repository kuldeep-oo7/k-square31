P0-1  THE CONVICTION HURDLE ADMITS 97% OF THE UNIVERSE
  Measured now, at commit 5327abb:
      BUY or ACCUMULATE : 204 of 210
      status            : 190 APEX_BUY_SIGNAL, 14 ACCUMULATE,
                          5 SEBI_FO_BAN, 1 STAND_ASIDE
      neuralScore       : min 65, median 98, max 98, 117 of 210 at the ceiling
      compositeScore    : min 54, median 76, max 87, hurdle is 68

  The architecture guide promised "11 BUY, 35 ACCUMULATE, 164 STAND_ASIDE". The
  desk delivers 204 actionable. A screen that says buy everything is not a
  screen, and it is worse than no screen because it looks like one.

  Two separate causes, both need fixing:

  (a) neuralScore is still saturated. The sigmoid pins at 1 for more than half
      the universe. I fixed one cause -- volume entered the convolution around
      1.0 while returns sat in [-0.2,0.2], adding a constant of roughly +4.2 to
      every logit -- and centring it moved the ceiling count from 205 to 117.
      The rest is not a scaling bug, it is that the logit terms were chosen by
      hand and never calibrated.

  (b) compositeScore has a median of 76 against a hurdle of 68, so the hurdle
      sits below the middle of its own distribution.

  FIX. Make both scores CROSS-SECTIONAL rather than absolute. On each refresh,
  rank every name against the universe that day and express the score as a
  percentile. A hurdle then has a defined meaning: "top 15% of the F&O universe
  today" is a statement; "score above 68" is not, because nothing ties 68 to
  anything. This is standard practice and it is not hand-tuning, because the
  transformation carries no free parameters.

  DO NOT fix this by editing the kernel weights or the logit coefficients until
  the histogram looks nicer. That is manufacturing discrimination rather than
  measuring it, and I will reject it.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - No more than 25% of the universe is BUY or ACCUMULATE on a normal day.
    - neuralScore has at least 30 distinct values across 210 names and no more
      than 20% at the maximum.
    - The published hurdle is stated as a percentile, and the code comment says
      what fraction of the universe it is designed to admit.
    - scripts/test-no-fabricated-values.js section 6 still passes unmodified.

P0-2  THE UNTRAINED FORMULA THAT CAN KILL ANY TRADE
  api/_lib/advanced-quant.js:448 metaLabelConfidence is a hand-written function
  of hurst, Kaufman ER, VPIN and trend state. recommendation.js feeds it into
  enforceHardVetoes and returns early on a veto, so it holds absolute kill
  authority over every candidate.

  Lopez de Prado's meta-labelling is a TRAINED SECONDARY CLASSIFIER fitted on
  the primary model's own outcomes. This is not that. It is a guess with veto
  power, and nobody has measured whether its vetoes are right.

  FIX. Either fit it on realised outcomes with a purged, embargoed split and
  report its out-of-sample precision and recall, or strip the veto and let it be
  a displayed diagnostic. Both are acceptable. Leaving an unfitted formula with
  kill authority is not.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - Either a training script exists, is run in CI, and reports out-of-sample
      metrics on data the fit never saw; or enforceHardVetoes no longer consults
      metaLabelConfidence and the UI labels it "diagnostic, not a gate".

P0-3  THE 1D-CNN IS NOT TRAINED AND IS DESCRIBED AS IF IT WERE
  neural-quant-engine.js calls its four kernels "Pre-trained Conv1D Kernels".
  They are hand-written round numbers with intent comments. There is no training
  data, loss function, gradient step or validation anywhere in the file.

  FIX. Pick one and be honest about it:
    (a) Actually train them. Label bars with forward returns, fit the kernels,
        hold out a period the fit never saw, report the out-of-sample result.
    (b) Rename them. "Four hand-designed matched filters" is accurate and is a
        legitimate technique. Remove "pre-trained", "deep learning" and "neural"
        from the user-facing copy if nothing was learned.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - Either a fit procedure with an out-of-sample score, or no occurrence of
      "trained", "deep learning" or "neural network" in code comments or UI copy
      describing these kernels.

P1 — CORRECTNESS
================================================================================

P1-1  NOTHING HAS EVER BEEN BACKTESTED
  The composite score, the conviction hurdle, the 2.5% target and the 1.5% stop
  have never been tested against anything. There is no walk-forward, no
  out-of-sample split, no benchmark and no transaction costs on the Apex path.

  You already have the apparatus and it is good: api/_lib/panel.js and
  scripts/panel-study.js do purged calendar splits, cluster-robust standard
  errors, an equal-weighted buy-and-hold control and a selection-adjusted hurdle
  (Bailey and Lopez de Prado expected maximum). It has never been pointed at the
  Apex engine.

  Be warned what it found when pointed at the older weekly ladder: zero of 24
  geometries survived validation and all 24 validation cells were negative. The
  Apex scores have not yet had to meet that standard. Expect the possibility
  that they do not, and report it if so.

  FIX. Run the Apex signal through panel-study. Report per-fill net return after
  the real dated statutory costs already in shadow-broker.js, against the
  buy-and-hold control, on a split the parameters never saw.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - A committed report with development / validation / final-test columns, the
      benchmark beside them, and the verdict field from panelVerdict.
    - Wired into npm scripts so it can be re-run.

P1-2  SURVIVORSHIP BIAS IN THE UNIVERSE
  The 210-name list is today's F&O membership applied backwards. Names are in
  the sample because they qualify NOW, so every historical statistic is
  optimistic by an unmeasured amount. Measured earlier on the Nifty 500 panel:
  the internal universe showed +105.9% median over a window where the index
  returned +59.1%.

  FIX. Point-in-time constituent history, or at minimum quantify the bias by
  comparing the universe's own return to the index over the same window and
  publishing the gap alongside every backtest number.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - Any backtest output carries the index return for the same window beside it.

P1-3  CASH BARS FOR A FUTURES AND OPTIONS DESK
  Bars come from NSE equity cash. The desk trades futures and options. Basis,
  roll cost, the expiry calendar and the futures margin are all absent.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - Either futures bars are ingested, or every futures figure states that it
      is derived from cash and carries the basis assumption explicitly.

P1-4  NO LIQUIDITY OR CAPACITY MODEL
  No ADV filter, no open-interest filter, no bid-ask spread, no capacity curve.
  There is no answer to "how much size can this take before it stops working",
  which is the first question any desk asks. The Amihud ratio is computed but
  does not gate.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - A name below a stated ADV or OI threshold cannot be actionable.
    - Each recommendation carries an estimated capacity in rupees.

P1-5  KELLY IS COMPUTED AND THROWN AWAY
  recommendation.js:289 computes calculateKellyFraction. Line 294 then sizes off
  conviction instead: clamp(0.20 + 0.20 * clamp(conviction/70), 0.10, 0.40).
  A computed-and-ignored number is how a system starts believing things it has
  not done. Either size with it, or delete it.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - Kelly appears in the sizing path, or not in the codebase.

P1-6  OPTIONS ARE THEORETICAL, NOT TRADEABLE
  Pricing is correct BSM but there is one flat volatility per name. Missing:
    - implied volatility surface and smile, so ATM and OTM share one sigma
    - American early exercise (Indian stock options are American)
    - SPAN and exposure margin
    - bid-ask and depth, so no slippage or executability
  Everything is labelled THEORETICAL_BSM_NOT_A_TRADED_QUOTE, which is honest.
  Keep that label until a real chain is wired.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - The label is not removed until a real option chain with bid/ask is the
      source; an IV surface replaces the single sigma.

P2 — MISSING CAPABILITY
================================================================================

P2-1  NO EARNINGS OR CORPORATE-ACTION BLACKOUT BEYOND THE CURRENT WEEK FLAG
  earningsVeto covers insideTradingWeek only. Add dividend, split, bonus, buyback
  and board-meeting dates, and adjust bars for corporate actions in the desk path
  the way the research path already does.

P2-2  NO MULTI-TIMEFRAME CONFIRMATION
  Candlestick and chart patterns are detected on one timeframe. A pattern that
  disagrees with the higher timeframe is usually noise.

P2-3  NO VOLUME PROFILE, VWAP ANCHORING OR VALUE AREA
  Volume is used as a raw series only. Volume-at-price structure is where most
  institutional execution logic lives.

P2-4  NO REGIME CONDITIONING BEYOND THE MACRO SHIELD
  The shield is binary. A trend model and a mean-reversion model should not be
  weighted identically in every regime. Consider a simple, measurable regime
  label (realised vol tercile plus index trend state) applied out of sample.

P2-5  NO CROSS-SECTIONAL RESIDUALISATION OR BETA HEDGE
  This is the one untested structural idea with a real argument behind it. On
  the older ladder, long geometries improved as the stop widened while short
  geometries worsened -- the same drift term with opposite signs, which says the
  rule was measuring market direction rather than anything about the entry.
  Strip the market factor from each name, trade the residual, hedge beta with an
  index future. It may fail. It is the only remaining idea that changes the
  structure rather than the parameters, and parameters are measured dead.

P2-6  NO ALERT ON DATA STALENESS
  If Yahoo fails, the cache serves the last bars. There must be a visible age on
  every price and a hard refusal to act on bars older than a stated threshold.

  ACCEPTANCE TEST
    - Every row carries barsAsOf; a row older than the threshold cannot be
      actionable and the UI shows the age.
