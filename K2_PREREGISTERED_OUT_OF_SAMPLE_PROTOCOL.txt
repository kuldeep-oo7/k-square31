================================================================================
PRE-REGISTERED OUT-OF-SAMPLE TEST OF THE APEX SIGNAL
Written 2026-09-17, BEFORE the test was run and BEFORE any result was seen.
Committed ahead of the result so the decision rule cannot move to meet it.
================================================================================

WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS

The Apex signal has already been measured on a 97-symbol panel across three
calendar windows. All three have been looked at. Once a window has been seen,
running it again does not test anything: the result is already known and any
adjustment made afterwards is fitting, however it is described.

So this test uses data that has never been examined for this signal, and the
rule for reading the outcome is written down here, in advance, in a commit that
precedes the run.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS BEING TESTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The signal exactly as it stands in scripts/apex-panel-study.js. No parameter is
changed for this test:

    ENTRY   returns5  > 0.01     (5-session return above 1%)
      AND   returns20 > 0.02     (20-session return above 2%)
      AND   volRatio  > 1.1      (session volume above 1.1x its 20-day average)

    EXIT    target +2.50%
            stop   -1.50%
            time   5 sessions, whichever comes first

    COSTS   real dated statutory charges via shadow-broker.js

This is a momentum-breakout rule. It is NOT the percentile-ranked engine that is
currently deployed; the deployed engine has never been backtested at all. That
gap is stated plainly rather than papered over, and it is the next thing to fix.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE HELD-OUT SAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The previous study used the 97 NSE large caps cached in .cache/panel-bars.
The NSE F&O universe holds 210 names. The remaining 113 have never been fetched,
never been simulated, and never appeared in any report:

  360ONE, ABCAPITAL, ALKEM, AMBER, ANGELONE, APLAPOLLO, ASHOKLEY, ASTRAL,
  ATHERENERG, AUBANK, AUROPHARMA, BANDHANBNK, BANKINDIA, BDL, BHARATFORG,
  BHEL, BIOCON, BLUESTARCO, BSE, CAMS, and 93 others.

These 113 symbols are the test set. They are a fresh CROSS-SECTION rather than a
fresh calendar period, because no future data exists yet. That is a real
limitation and it is recorded here rather than discovered later: the market
regime overlaps with the windows already seen, so a common factor could carry
across. What the held-out names do establish is whether the rule generalises
beyond the specific 97 securities it was measured on, which is the part that has
never been tested.

The 113 are mostly mid-cap. Mid-caps are more volatile and less liquid than the
97 large caps, which cuts both ways: a momentum rule may trigger more often, and
costs and impact bite harder. No adjustment is made for this.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE DECISION RULE, FIXED IN ADVANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Primary statistic: mean net return per fill after statutory costs, with a
cluster-robust standard error clustering on the calendar week, because names
entering in the same week share that week's market shock.

Selection hurdle: the expected maximum of SEARCH_TRIALS = 12 independent
standard normal draws, which is t >= 1.6648. The geometry and thresholds were
chosen from a search, so the bar is the bar that search would clear by luck.

    PASS      mean net per fill > 0
              AND cluster-robust t >= 1.6648
              AND at least 200 fills

    FAIL      anything else

There is no third outcome. A result between those two is a FAIL. If the test
fails, the finding is that the rule does not generalise across the cross-section
and it will be reported as such, in full, with the numbers.

A benchmark runs alongside: the Nifty 500 over the identical window, fetched not
typed. A rule that earns less than the index it trades inside has not earned
anything, whatever its t-statistic says.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT WILL NOT HAPPEN AFTERWARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If the result is poor, none of the following will be done:

  - changing the target, the stop, the holding period or the entry thresholds
    and re-running on this same sample
  - dropping symbols whose results are inconvenient
  - splitting the 113 into sub-groups until one of them passes
  - reporting the best of several variants as though it were the only one tried
  - describing a failure as "promising", "directionally positive" or "needs
    more data" when the number says no

Any of those would convert this held-out sample into a fitted one, and there is
no second held-out sample to replace it with. The sample is spent once.

If a change is genuinely warranted afterwards, it must be tested on a window
that does not exist yet: forward data, collected after the change is made.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PREDICTION ON RECORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stated before the run, so it can be wrong in public.

The prior study showed per-fill return decaying across its three windows:
0.3631% in development, 0.2282% in validation, 0.0613% in the final test, with
the final test at t 0.747 and p 0.455 -- indistinguishable from zero. A momentum
rule of this shape is also among the most widely known in the market, which
argues against a large surviving edge.

My expectation is therefore that this test FAILS: a small positive or slightly
negative mean, a t-statistic below the 1.6648 hurdle, and no confirmed edge.

If it passes cleanly, that is a genuine and surprising result and I will say so.
If it fails, that is the answer and the rule should not be traded.
