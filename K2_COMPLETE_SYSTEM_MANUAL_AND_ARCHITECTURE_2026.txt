6. BETTER APPROACH — IMPLEMENTATION / ACCEPTANCE ROADMAP
========================================================
Priority0: correctness before another indicator.
Raw source event -> immutable source snapshot -> schema/identity/time/basis checks
-> versioned feature snapshot -> pre-registered strategy decision -> portfolio
risk review -> manual recommendation -> fill evidence -> ledger -> outcome audit.
Every arrow needs an observable ID and failure state. Missing input means unknown.

Priority1: data foundation.
Use a contract-master daily snapshot plus entitled timestamped cash/future/option
bid/ask/depth feed. Maintain corporate actions, delistings, holidays and point-in-
time symbol/ISIN changes. Keep raw and adjusted histories separate. Compare key
fields against independent exchange records. Store provider, source/event/receipt
times, instrument identity, data basis, schema/version and hash. Preserve revisions.
Treat Sheets as an audit/control/reporting surface, not an every-second all-market
tick database. Evaluate durable append-only object/time-series storage with
retention/backups/access controls; migrate only after count/hash reconciliation.

Priority2: options/risk.
Use observed chain bid/ask and liquidity to choose valid expiries/strikes. Compute
IV only within no-arbitrage bounds; stress skew/smile, gap, time decay and IV crush.
Compare single option versus vertical spread AFTER all-leg costs and margin.
Require valid orderable lot allocations. Pricing precision cannot overcome bad
quotes or manufacture a forecast. Do not auto-sell a wing called a "free hedge".

Priority3: validation and holding horizon.
Compare a small set of pre-registered baselines (e.g. trend and mean reversion),
matched to instrument/horizon; nested purged walk-forward, embargo and date-block
bootstrap; count all parameter trials; costs/impact/turnover/capacity and stress.
Measure loss tails/drawdown and calibration, not just hit rate. Keep a genuinely
unseen prospective period. Public momentum research motivates a hypothesis; it
does NOT prove that Indian weekly options or this system share that edge.
For holding period, estimate distribution/time-to-target/stop/expiry conditional
on regime, with right-censoring for open trades. Compare 1/3/5/10/20-session policies
without choosing from the final test. Report ranges/uncertainty and invalidation,
not "hold exactly4days". No mandatory Friday sale unless independently justified.

Priority4: operation and learning.
Always-on quote service with heartbeat, source age, entitlement, backpressure,
restart replay, data-gap abstention, timestamped alerts and delivery receipts.
Research refresh independent of weekly anchor; portal reads continuous alerts.
Daily review checks data health, fills, forecast calibration, realised/unrealised
P&L, fees and model drift. Log proposals; no automatic model promotion after a
handful of wins. New model must beat a frozen challenger benchmark prospectively.
