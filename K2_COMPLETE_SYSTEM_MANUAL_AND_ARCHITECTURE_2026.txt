--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 WHAT IS THE K2 DESK?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
K2 is an institutional-grade quantitative algorithmic execution, screening, and
recommendation desk engineered specifically for the National Stock Exchange of
India (NSE).

It integrates two complementary trading engines:
1. THE STRUCTURAL LADDER ENGINE: A continuous futures multi-barrier swing framework
   operating across liquid NSE Stock Futures. It places 2% compounding geometric
   rungs anchored to weekly opening session prices, enforcing strict 1:2 risk-to-
   reward ratios, gap-aware execution, and continuous multi-session holding horizons.
2. THE MEDALLION-GRADE DEFINED-RISK DESK: A multi-cap quantitative recommendation
   engine covering Large Cap, Mid Cap, Small Cap, Index Derivatives, and Stock
   Options. Every options ticket is structured as a defined-risk spread (Bull Call
   Spreads, Bear Put Spreads, or Protective Puts), hard-capping maximum capital at
   risk and neutralizing black-swan gap risk.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 WHY WAS IT DESIGNED THIS WAY?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WHY NOT NAIVE TECHNICAL INDICATORS (RSI, MACD, SUPERTREND)?
  Standard technical indicators suffer from heavy lag and lack stationarity. Most
  importantly, price series have infinite memory (non-stationary random walk).
  Standard differencing (integer d=1) removes non-stationarity but obliterates all
  long-term memory. K2 uses Fractional Differentiation (d=0.55), retaining 55% of
  historical price memory while achieving stationarity for predictive modeling.

- WHY MANDATE DEFINED-RISK SPREADS OVER NAKED OPTIONS?
  Retail option buyers suffer 70-80% loss rates due to theta decay and volatility
  crush. Retail option sellers suffer catastrophic tail blowups on overnight gap-
  downs. K2 structures options exclusively as vertical debit spreads:
  * Buying an At-the-Money / Near-the-Money Call and selling an Out-of-the-Money Call.
  * Buying an ATM/NTM Put and selling an OTM Put.
  This cuts the required capital by 40-60%, funds theta decay via the short leg,
  and establishes a hard mathematically guaranteed maximum loss ceiling.

- WHY CONTINUOUS HOLDING HORIZONS INSTEAD OF FORCED FRIDAY LIQUIDATION?
  Historical backtests proved that forcing liquidation on Friday 3:15 PM IST
  consistently dumped profitable swings at weekend liquidity discounts, giving
  away institutional alpha. Positions are held continuously across weekends.
  Monday open merely recalculates the weekly reporting reference; exits occur
  strictly on target triggers, trailing stop adjustments, or the 3-day monthly
  futures expiry roll.

- WHY ONLY 3 STOCKS PASSED THE LADDER SCREEN ON MONDAY?
  The weekly ladder framework applies 7 severe statistical vetoes (Hurst > 0.55,
  VPIN < 0.20, OU half-life < 10 sessions, ML carry > 0, F&O ban clearance, zero
  binary earnings events in 14 days, and trade-through fill mechanics). Out of 210
  F&O candidates, only 3 (BEL, ABB, BANKBARODA) cleared every hurdle. K2 never
  lowers mathematical thresholds just to show more names.

4. CORE QUANTITATIVE MATHEMATICS & FORMULAS IN DEPTH
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 FRACTIONAL DIFFERENTIATION (MEMORY PRESERVATION AT d=0.55)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard integer differentiation (d=1, Delta P_t = P_t - P_{t-1}) strips all memory
of past support/resistance. K2 applies fractional differentiation via the binomial
series expansion:
    (1 - B)^d = sum_{k=0}^{infinity} (-1)^k * (d choose k) * B^k
              = 1 - d*B + (d*(d-1)/2!)*B^2 - (d*(d-1)*(d-2)/3!)*B^3 + ...
By fixing d=0.55 with a memory threshold of 1e-4, the transformed price series
passes the Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) test for stationarity while retaining
55% of the multi-month memory structure.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 VOLUME-SYNCHRONIZED PROBABILITY OF TOXICITY (VPIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Measures adverse selection and informed order flow toxicity. Trading volume is
sliced into equal volume bars (V). Trade direction is signed using tick bulk volume
classification:
    VPIN = (sum_{tau=1}^N |V_tau^B - V_tau^S|) / (N * V)
* VPIN < 0.15: Clean retail / passive institutional flow (Safe to enter).
* VPIN > 0.25: High order flow toxicity (Toxic dump or institutional liquidation).
Trades with VPIN > 0.20 are vetoed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 HURST EXPONENT (VARIANCE RATIO & PERSISTENCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evaluates whether a time series is mean-reverting, random walk, or trending:
    H = log(R/S) / log(T)
* H < 0.45: Mean-reverting (Stationary range).
* 0.45 <= H <= 0.55: Brownian motion / Pure random walk (Noise - No edge).
* H > 0.55: Persistent / Trending (Momentum persists).
K2 swing long orders require H > 0.55 to ensure directional follow-through.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 ORNSTEIN-UHLENBECK MEAN-REVERSION PROCESS & HALF-LIFE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Models price deviation dynamics toward equilibrium:
    dX_t = theta * (mu - X_t) * dt + sigma * dW_t
Discrete regression of Delta X_t on X_{t-1}:
    Delta X_t = a + b * X_{t-1} + epsilon_t
    theta = -log(1 + b) / dt
    Half-Life (t_{1/2}) = log(2) / theta
Swing setups require half-life < 10 trading sessions to prevent capital entrapment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 BLACK-SCHOLES-MERTON PDE & BLACK-76 OPTIONS GREEKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
European option valuation using closed-form analytical solutions:
    d1 = (log(S / K) + (r - q + 0.5 * sigma^2) * T) / (sigma * sqrt(T))
    d2 = d1 - sigma * sqrt(T)
    Call Price = S * e^(-q*T) * N(d1) - K * e^(-r*T) * N(d2)
    Put Price  = K * e^(-r*T) * N(-d2) - S * e^(-q*T) * N(-d1)
Greeks calculated analytically:
* Delta (dC/dS): Hedge ratio per underlying rupee change.
* Gamma (d^2C/dS^2): Rate of change of Delta.
* Theta (dC/dt): Calendar time decay per day.
* Vega (dC/d_sigma): Sensitivity to a 1% shift in Implied Volatility (IV).
Implied Volatility is solved using a bisection algorithm converging within 1e-8.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6 DEFINED-RISK SPREADS VS NAKED OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To protect traders from black swans:
* BULL CALL SPREAD: Buy Strike K1 CE, Sell Strike K2 CE (where K2 > K1).
  - Net Debit (Max Risk) = Premium(K1) - Premium(K2).
  - Max Profit = (K2 - K1) - Net Debit.
  - Breakeven = K1 + Net Debit.
* BEAR PUT SPREAD: Buy Strike K1 PE, Sell Strike K2 PE (where K2 < K1).
  - Net Debit (Max Risk) = Premium(K1) - Premium(K2).
  - Max Profit = (K1 - K2) - Net Debit.
  - Breakeven = K1 - Net Debit.
Even if an underlying gaps down 20% overnight, the trader CANNOT lose more than
the net debit.
