3. STOCHASTIC CALCULUS & INSTITUTIONAL RISK ENGINE
================================================================================
File: api/_lib/institutional-calculus.js

The mathematical core operates under continuous stochastic differential equations:
1. Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) & Itô's Lemma:
   Decomposes price trajectories into deterministic drift μ and stochastic diffusion:
   dS_t = μ S_t dt + σ S_t dW_t
   With Itô's drift correction (-0.5 * σ^2) applied to logarithmic returns.
2. GARCH(1,1) Conditional Volatility SDE:
   σ_t^2 = ω + α * ε_{t-1}^2 + β * σ_{t-1}^2
   Projects conditional 5-day volatility regimes (LOW_VOL, MODERATE_VOL, HIGH_VOL).
3. Cornish-Fisher 99% Value-at-Risk (VaR) & CVaR:
   Adjusts standard Gaussian quantiles for empirical skewness (S) and excess kurtosis (K):
   z_CF = z + ((z^2 - 1)/6)*S + ((z^3 - 3z)/24)*K - ((2z^3 - 5z)/36)*S^2
   Computes fat-tailed Rupee risk and Expected Shortfall under Black Swan shocks.
4. Microstructure Liquidity & Kyle's Lambda:
   ΔP_t = λ * Q_t + η_t
   Measures price impact per 100,000 shares traded, vetoing illiquid small-caps.
5. Kalman Filter State-Space Dynamic Beta:
   Tracks dynamic beta convergence against NIFTY to ensure market-neutral hedging.
