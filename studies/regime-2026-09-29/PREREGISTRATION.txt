PRE-REGISTRATION -- THE DESK'S OWN NIFTY TREND READER, AS A DIRECTION FILTER
Written 2026-09-29 ~20:55 IST, BEFORE any result of this test was computed.
Its sha256 is recorded before the study runs; nothing below may change after.

WHY
On 28-29 Sep 2026 the market fell while the desk showed only buys. The desk has a trend
reader (api/_lib/market-regime.js classifyMarketRegime) that has read NIFTY as
BEAR_HOSTILE ("long setups restricted", shorts allowed) every day since at least
21 Sep 2026, and it was never connected to the page. The user asked for puts when the
market is going down. This tests whether that reader, used as a direction filter,
would have made money on NIFTY over five years -- before real money relies on it.

DATA
- NIFTY 50 daily open/high/low/close from NSE's own index history
  (research/gap-filter-2026-09-29/nifty-50-nse.json, 1,258 sessions,
  2021-09-01 .. 2026-09-28).
- The regime for entry day d = classifyMarketRegime(bars up to and including d-1): known
  before d's open. Days before the reader has 200 sessions of history are still
  classified by the module's own rules (it needs 50).

THE DESK'S GEOMETRY, ON NIFTY, ON DAILY BARS (as the ledger scores trades)
LONG on day d: buy = open(d) x 0.992 rounded down to 0.05; filled only if low(d) < buy;
  target = buy x 1.025, stop = buy x 0.985 (nearest 0.05).
SHORT on day d (the stand-in for a put; there is no five-year option history):
  sell = open(d) x 1.008 rounded up to 0.05; filled only if high(d) > sell;
  target = sell x 0.975, stop = sell x 1.015 (nearest 0.05).
Both: the fill day's adverse touch is a STOP at the stop and its favourable touch is not
counted; each later session up to the 20th: an open through the stop or the target exits
at the open, else a stop touch exits at the stop (the stop first if both), else a target
touch at the target; otherwise the 20th session's close. Data end: the last close.
Net return = the move in the trade's favour / entry - charges (api/_lib/shadow-broker.js
calculateNseFuturesCharges, entry and exit, quantity = ceil(500000 / entry), dated).

HYPOTHESES
H1 SHORTS IN DOWNTRENDS: every entry day whose regime is BEAR_HOSTILE, a short.
   PASS = validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND final t >= the one-sided 5%
   Student-t critical value with (final-window months - 1) degrees of freedom.
H2 LONGS OUTSIDE DOWNTRENDS: every entry day whose regime is NOT BEAR_HOSTILE, a long.
   PASS = the same three conditions.
Reported, deciding nothing: longs on every day; longs in downtrends; shorts on every day.

UNIT AND ERRORS
One trade per entry day (NIFTY only). Consecutive days' 20-session trades overlap, so
errors are clustered by CALENDAR MONTH (api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean).

WINDOWS (by entry day; the calendar of every earlier study)
- development: before 2024-09-19; validation: 2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17;
- final test: 2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES, STATED IN ADVANCE
The reader goes on the page tonight in any case, because the user asked for the market's
direction and puts when it falls: in a downtrend no new buy levels, index puts instead.
The result decides only what the page SAYS about it: a PASS is "tested on five years:
passed"; a FAIL is "tested on five years: did NOT make money -- shown because you asked,
tracked on TRACK". Nothing here is a claim of an edge unless H1 or H2 passes.
