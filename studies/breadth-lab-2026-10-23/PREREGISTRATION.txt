2. DATA, UNIVERSE, PRICES
- Discovery NSE 2005-01 .. 2020-10 (release data-oos); confirmation 2020-10 .. 2026-10-01 (the research snapshot, with
  data-oos before 2020-09-01 as look-back only); they share October 2020.
- Breadth is measured on each month's universe: the night lab's (top 200 EQ stocks by traded value, >= 250 bars after the
  last reset). On day d-1 a stock counts if it has bars on d-1 and d-2 (and the history a measure needs).
- NIFTY trades exactly as the index lab prices them: the near-month NIFTY future (earliest expiry on or after the entry day
  with an official open and close), official open and close, rolled at an expiry, NSE futures charges, quantity ceil(Rs 5
  lakh / entry).

3. SIGNALS ON SESSION d-1 (the trade starts at the open of d)
 SB count = universe stocks with close(d-1)/close(d-2)-1 <= -1.5581% and total futures OI(d-1)/OI(d-2)-1 >= +3.0380%.
 LU count = the same price fall with OI(d-1)/OI(d-2)-1 <= -3.0380%.
 UP share = share of the universe closing above its previous close.
 ABOVE-50 share = share of the universe closing above its own 50-session average close.
 LOW share = share of the universe closing at or below its lowest close of the previous 250 sessions.
 OI change = sum of the universe's total futures OI on d-1 / the same stocks' sum on d-2, minus 1.
 "Top 20%" = at or above the 80th percentile of the same measure over the previous 250 sessions (at least 200 of them).

4. MEMBERS (6)
 B1 SHORT BUILD-UP CROWD: SB count in its top 20% -> SHORT NIFTY at the open of d, cover at the close of d.
 B2 LONG-UNWINDING CROWD: LU count in its top 20% -> BUY NIFTY at the open of d, sell at the close of d.
 B3 BREADTH THRUST: UP share >= 80% -> BUY NIFTY at the open of d, sell at the close of d+4 (5 sessions).
 B4 WASHOUT: ABOVE-50 share < 20% -> BUY NIFTY at the open of d, sell at the close of d+4.
 B5 NEW LOWS: LOW share >= 10% -> BUY NIFTY at the open of d, sell at the close of d+4.
 B6 OI SURGE ON A DOWN DAY: OI change in its top 20% AND NIFTY's near-month future closed below its previous close on d-1 ->
    SHORT NIFTY at the open of d, cover at the close of d.
 Overlapping holds are separate trades.

5. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member: monthly mean (%) of its trades' net returns; Newey-West t (lag 3). Holm across the 6 at 5%. Full Deflated
  Sharpe with N = 376 trials (370 before, these 6), as the night lab computes it.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2. A
  confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2. FAIL otherwise.
  NOT TESTABLE: fewer than 24 months with a trade.
- Reported, not judged: trades, win rate, worst month, mean a trade, and for 2020-2026 GM rupees at 20 units (gross).
- Expectation, stated now: the most likely outcome is that nothing passes both periods.
