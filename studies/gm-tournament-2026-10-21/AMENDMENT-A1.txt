3. THE SCORER (code, not rules)
 a. A member that enters or exits at a price other than the open or the close (a limit, a target, a stop) is scored at
    its own price. When GM's expiry cut comes first, the exit is the close of the cut session. Check 5.3 below.
 b. NIFTY members are scored on NSE's NIFTY 50 index (its official open and close), as a proxy for NIFTY futures, cut at
    NIFTY's monthly futures expiry from NSE's own expiry list. U20 = 20 units of the index. Labelled "GM's NIFTY
    listing not known".
 c. Trades come from each member's OWN code. Where that code does not output the symbol, the entry and exit sessions or
    the prices, the loader adds those fields to its trade record at load time. The text patch must match exactly once;
    the committed study.js files are not edited.
 d. Each stock of a multi-stock book (CM deciles, F7 pairs legs) is a separate GM trade at the same sizes as every
    member (U20 and EQ Rs 5 lakh).
 e. Dropped trades (no bars, crossing an 18% reset, unscorable) are counted and printed per member.
