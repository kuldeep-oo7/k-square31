Written 2026-10-21 ~00:30 IST (Wed), BEFORE any GM-scored result was computed. Branch claude/gm-tournament-2026-10-21.
Selection on 2020-2026 (the in-sample years every member was built on), then ONE confirmation of the leaders on NSE
2005-2020 where their inputs exist. Judged by protocol v2 with amendment A1 of the combined model.

1. WHY
The owner (20-21 Oct 2026): "run all of the strategies we do have ... on the gm global ... audit and deploy". GM Global
is the owner's paper futures competition. Its rules, in the owner's words (20 Oct): each Monday the buy price resets to
Monday's open and the previous week's P&L is booked; positions are sold by hand or auto-sold at month end; the score is
the running rupee total; 20 units is the minimum size. Nothing is fetched from GM.
This is NOT a new search for an edge: every member below was already tested on 2020-2026 and FAILED or was PROMISING.
It asks which of them would have scored best under GM's rules, and whether any of that survives on unseen years.
No member's rules are changed here. Fixing code so it runs is allowed; changing a rule to improve a score is not.

3. GM SCORING (fixed now)
 - Entry: each member's own entry (the open of the session after its signal). Exit: its own exit, but never later than
   the close of the stock's monthly futures expiry session (read from NSE's F&O files): GM lists only the expiring
   month and auto-sells. CALENDAR month-end instead of expiry is reported as a sensitivity, not judged.
 - Weekend gap: (G1) real P&L; (G0) P&L with every Friday-close-to-Monday-open move inside a holding removed (GM
   re-bases at Monday's open). G1 is judged; G0 is reported and labelled "an effect of GM's bookkeeping, not the market".
 - Size: (U20) 20 units, in rupees = GM's own score; (EQ) Rs 5 lakh a position, in rupees; (PCT) % a trade.
   The SIGNIFICANCE test uses EQ (so expensive stocks do not decide it); U20 is reported as GM's leaderboard.
 - Costs: NSE futures charges (api/_lib/shadow-broker.js, by date) on both sides as a stand-in for GM's charges, which
   are not known; a roll is not charged because the expiry cut ends every position.
 - Concurrency: every signal is taken (no cap); rupees per month and rupees per trade are both reported.

4. VERDICT
 Per member: monthly sums of EQ rupees / Rs 5 lakh (so a monthly % of one position's capital). Newey-West t (lag 3).
 Holm across all members. Deflated Sharpe with N = number of members + 100 (the trials already run on this desk).
 PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95. PROMISING = mean > 0 and t >= 2, not PASS. FAIL otherwise.
 CONFIRMATION: the 5 members with the highest EQ t on 2020-2026 (whatever their verdict) are re-scored once on NSE
 2005-2020 (data-oos) where their inputs exist; members whose inputs do not reach back are labelled NOT CONFIRMABLE.
 A member reaches TRACK as a GM paper candidate only if it PASSES here AND its 2005-2020 confirmation has mean > 0 and
 t >= 2. Nothing goes to FUTURES as a pick without that. The leaderboard itself goes to TRACK as information.

5. CHECKS BEFORE THE REAL RUN
 5.1 The GM scorer on a hand-built trade set: a long bought Mon open, held over a weekend to an expiry, gives the
     right U20, EQ and PCT numbers, the right G0/G1 difference, and is cut at the expiry close.
 5.2 Null: every member's trades re-dated to random sessions (same counts, sides, holds): at most 5% of members PASS.

6. POWER AND MULTIPLE TESTING, stated now
 With ~90 members and N = ~190 in the deflation, a PASS needs a very strong series; most members have already failed
 on these years. The likely outcome is a leaderboard with no PASS, which says which rules suit GM's scoring best, not
 that any of them earns money.
