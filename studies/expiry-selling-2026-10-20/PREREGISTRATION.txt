PRE-REGISTRATION -- SELLING NIFTY OPTIONS ON EXPIRY DAY (A FRIEND'S METHOD, AS FAR AS IT CAN BE WRITTEN DOWN)
Written 2026-10-20 (Tue, NSE holiday) ~23:20 IST, BEFORE any return of this test was computed. Branch
claude/expiry-selling-2026-10-20. Run ONCE on real data after the checks in section 7 pass. Judged by protocol v2 with
amendment A1 of the combined model (PASS needs mean > 0, Newey-West t >= 3, Holm, and the full Deflated Sharpe >= 0.95).

1. WHY, AND WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN
The owner's friend reportedly made about Rs 1 lakh in a month on about Rs 6 lakh, mostly selling NIFTY options on expiry
day (time value collapses fastest that day), plus some option buying. His exact strikes, timing and stops are not known;
the members below are the common written-down versions of expiry-day selling. The option buying is not tested (no rule).
ALREADY TESTED ON NIFTY OPTION SELLING (counted in the deflation, section 6): the NIFTY 2% weekly strangle (V2, t 4.66,
DSR 0.39; its 2005-2020 replication found the edge smaller), iron condors IC1-IC3 (best t 1.75), short NIFTY straddles in
the families study, and the results-straddle study. These held positions for days, not one expiry session.
LOOKED AT BEFORE THIS WAS WRITTEN: one day's file (19 Oct 2026) to check the format: the 22900 call opened 280.65 and
expired worthless (NIFTY settled 22,888.95). No other price of the test sample was seen.
RULE CHANGES INSIDE THE SAMPLE (reported as sub-periods, not used to choose anything): SEBI's index-derivative measures
from 20 Nov 2024 (extra 2% extreme-loss margin on short options on expiry day, larger contracts, one weekly-expiry
index per exchange; Zerodha, Business Standard, Fyers); NIFTY weekly expiry moved from Thursday to Tuesday from
2 Sep 2025 (NSE circular of 23 Jun 2025, per Business Today and TradingQnA).

2. DATA (NSE's own F&O bhavcopy; private release data-nifty-options; none committed)
- Every NIFTY weekly and monthly option expiry from Feb 2019 to the last expiry with data. An "expiry session" is a
  session whose file has NIFTY options with expiry = that session (read from the file, never computed).
- Entry price of a leg = its OPEN on the expiry session (the first trade of the day; the friend's exact time is unknown
  and NSE publishes no free intraday history, so the open is the only entry the data supports). A leg with open <= 0 is
  missing; an expiry with a missing leg is skipped and counted.
- NIFTY's open on the expiry session sets the strikes: NSE ind_close_all (files from 2014, so the whole sample from
  2019 is covered). If NIFTY's open is missing for a day, that expiry is skipped and counted.
- Exit: the leg's intrinsic value at NSE's final settlement price, which is the SETTLE column on the expiry session
  (checked on 2023-03-29 and 2026-10-19: it holds NIFTY's settlement, 17,080.70 and 22,888.95).

3. MEMBERS (fixed now; one lot each of whatever is sold; returns in % of NIFTY's open, so lot-size changes do not matter)
 X1 ATM STRADDLE, HELD: sell the call and the put at the strike nearest NIFTY's open; hold to settlement.
 X2 ATM STRADDLE, 2x STOP PER LEG: as X1, but a leg whose HIGH that day reaches 2x its entry is bought back at 2x its
    entry (assumed whenever touched, even late in the day; that is the cautious reading of a daily file).
 X3 1% STRANGLE, HELD: sell the call at the strike nearest open x 1.01 and the put nearest open x 0.99; hold.
 X4 1% STRANGLE, 2x STOP PER LEG: as X3 with X2's stop.
 Strikes: the listed strike nearest the target (ties: the farther from the money). Both legs must exist.

4. COSTS
 api/_lib/trade-costs.js roundTripCost for each leg (entry premium, exit = stop price or max(intrinsic, 0.05)), the
 model every option study here used (it applies today's STT to past years, so older years are scored a little worse:
 PROBLEMS.md B5), plus 1 point of slippage per leg.

5. SAMPLE AND SUB-PERIODS
 Verdict on all expiries Feb 2019 .. last. Reported, not judged: the three periods split at 20 Nov 2024 and 2 Sep 2025;
 by year; the worst single expiry; the worst month; the share of expiries won; and, for information, rupees per lot at
 a lot of 75 and the share of a Rs 6 lakh account each member would have made or lost per month.

6. SCORE AND VERDICT
 Per member: monthly sums of per-expiry net returns. Newey-West t, lag 3. Holm across X1-X4. Deflated Sharpe on the
 monthly series with N = 4 + 6 = 10 (the four members plus V2, IC1, IC2, IC3, the families short straddle and the
 results straddle). PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95. PROMISING = mean > 0 and t >= 2, not PASS. FAIL otherwise.
 POWER, stated now: about 360 expiries; selling premium usually wins most weeks and loses big rarely, so a few crash
 expiries decide the sign. A PASS still goes to TRACK as a paper test first; selling options needs margin and can lose
 more than the premium.

7. CHECKS BEFORE THE REAL RUN (written to RESULT.txt first)
 7.1 Planted edge: a synthetic expiry set where options are priced 20% above their fair (Black-Scholes, realised vol)
     value must PASS for X1; priced fairly with zero costs, at most 1 PASS among X1-X4 over 3 seeds.
 7.2 No look-ahead: every input is from the expiry session's own file (open, high, settle) and NIFTY's open; the code
     is checked to read nothing dated after the expiry session.
 If a check fails, the real run is not made and the failure is reported.

8. FILES
 study.js (members, costs, verdict), checks in the same file (--synthetic). Data stays in the private release.
