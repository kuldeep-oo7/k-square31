2. DATA AND PERIODS (as the futures lab, studies/futures-lab-2026-10-23)
- Discovery NSE 2005-01 .. 2020-10 (release data-oos); confirmation 2020-10 .. 2026-10-01 (the research snapshot, with
  data-oos before 2020-09-01 as look-back only); they share October 2020.
- Universe: the night lab's (top 200 by traded value, >= 250 bars after the last reset), each month.
- WEEK: ISO weeks of sessions. For a stock at the last session f of a week: R5 = share close(f) / share close 5 sessions
  before f - 1; OI5 = total futures OI(f) / total futures OI 5 sessions before f - 1 (both values above zero).
- TRADE: the next ISO week, the stock's near-month future bought or sold at its official open on the week's first session
  and closed at its official close on the week's last session, rolled at an expiry (the futures lab's futHold); NSE futures
  charges; quantity ceil(Rs 5 lakh / entry). EXCESS = the book net minus (long) / plus (short) the equal-weighted return of
  the universe's near-month futures over the same week (at least 30 names).

3. MEMBERS (4), each a weekly book of all qualifying stocks (at least 1)
 W1 WEEKLY LONG BUILD-UP: R5 >= +5% and OI5 >= +10% -> LONG. Excess.
 W2 WEEKLY SHORT BUILD-UP: R5 <= -5% and OI5 >= +10% -> SHORT. Excess.
 W3 WEEKLY SHORT COVERING: R5 >= +5% and OI5 <= -10% -> SHORT (a rally without new longs fades). Excess.
 W4 WEEKLY LONG UNWINDING: R5 <= -5% and OI5 <= -10% -> LONG (the selling is exhausted). Excess.

4. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member: monthly mean (%) of its weekly books; Newey-West t (lag 3). Holm across the 4 at 5%. Full Deflated Sharpe with
  N = 389 trials (385 before, these 4), as the night lab computes it.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2. A
  confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2. FAIL otherwise.
- Reported, not judged: weeks with a book, names a week, win rate, worst month.
- Expectation, stated now: the most likely outcome is that nothing passes both periods.
