PRE-REGISTRATION -- TECHNICAL ANALYSIS ATLAS (published chart, indicator and candlestick rules)
Written 3 Oct 2026 BEFORE any signal or result was computed on the real data.
Its sha256 is in PREREGISTRATION.sha256 and checked by study.js; if it changes, the result is void.

WHY
The owner asked to test the strategies that the open-source world and TradingView say work, mostly
chart patterns, indicators and candlesticks, and to keep only what passes. Each rule below is
someone else's published rule with its own parameters. Nothing is tuned here.

WHAT THIS DATA HAS ALREADY BEEN USED FOR
The same windows were used by about 11 earlier studies on this desk, all of which failed. The final
window is not a clean holdout. A PASS is a paper candidate, not a proof.

DATA
NSE CM bhavcopy 2020-09-01 .. 2026-10-01 (release data-nse-cm; local copy at
research/rising-stocks-2026-10-02/nse-data/cm). Series EQ, keyed by NSE symbol. Resets are handled
exactly as in research/rising-stocks-2026-10-02/PREREGISTRATION-v2.txt: an open >= 18% from the
previous close is a reset; the signal's bars must all come after the last reset, and a trade whose
window contains a reset is dropped. NSE's prevClose is not split-adjusted (shown there).
Universe: each month, the top 200 EQ symbols by median traded value over the previous 60 sessions,
among symbols with at least 250 sessions after their last reset (the longest rule needs 200).

ONE UNIFORM TRADE, so that only the SIGNAL is tested
A signal is read from completed bars up to and including d-1.
- LONG: buy at open(d), sell at close(d+9), which is 10 sessions. This is Brock, Lakonishok & LeBaron's
  fixed 10-day holding.
- SHORT: the reverse.
One trade per stock per member per signal day. Signals are EVENTS (the day a cross or pattern
occurs), not states.
Costs: api/_lib/shadow-broker.js calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs, dated, quantity =
ceil(500000/price), plus one extra round trip per month end crossed (a futures roll; approximate).
Net return in %.

MEMBERS (each LONG and SHORT is a separate member; Holm across ALL of them)
Brock, Lakonishok & LeBaron (1992), Journal of Finance 47(5):1731-1764 (abstract read on Wiley; the
rule details are as the paper is widely summarised, because the PDF was not reachable):
  M1  MA 1-50   close(d-1) crosses above SMA50 -> long; crosses below -> short
  M2  MA 1-150  the same with SMA150
  M3  MA 5-150  SMA5 crosses SMA150
  M4  MA 1-200  close crosses SMA200
  M5  MA 2-200  SMA2 crosses SMA200
  M6  TRB 50    close(d-1) > max of the 50 closes before it -> long; < min -> short (first day only)
  M7  TRB 150
  M8  TRB 200
Curtis Faith, The Original Turtle Trading Rules (2003): breakout entries (exits replaced by the
uniform trade):
  M9  20-day    close(d-1) above the highest high of the 20 sessions before it -> long;
                below the lowest low -> short
  M10 55-day    the same with 55
TradingView built-in strategies (help pages read 3 Oct 2026):
  M11 RSI Strategy   RSI(14) crosses up through 30 -> long; crosses down through 70 -> short.
                     The page states length 14 and does not state the levels; 30/70 is the standard
                     default.
  M12 MACD Strategy  the MACD(12,26,9) histogram turns from negative to positive -> long; positive
                     to negative -> short. The page states 12/26/9.
  M13 Supertrend     direction flips to up -> long; to down -> short. ATR 10, factor 3; the page
                     lists these inputs but not their values; 10/3 is the commonly stated default.
  M14 Bollinger Bands Strategy Directed  close crosses below the lower band (20, 2.0) -> long;
                     crosses above the upper band -> short. The page states 20 and 2.0.
Connors RSI(2), StockCharts ChartSchool:
  M15 RSI(2) < 10 and close > SMA200 -> long; RSI(2) > 90 and close < SMA200 -> short
      (the first day of each episode)
Crabel NR7, StockCharts ChartSchool:
  M16 bar d-1 is an NR7 (its range is the narrowest of the last 7) and the close of d-1 is in the top
      half of its range -> long; bottom half -> short.
      (A daily-bar version: the intraday breakout of the NR7 high/low cannot be timed on daily bars.)
The desk's own detectors, run per stock on its last 66 bars up to d-1, as the live desk feeds them:
  M17 Candlesticks   api/_lib/candlestick-engine.js identifyCandlestickPatterns bias BULLISH -> long,
                     BEARISH -> short (the day the bias appears, i.e. it differed on d-2's read)
  M18 Chart patterns api/_lib/chart-patterns.js scanChartPatterns primaryPattern CONFIRMED with bias
                     BULLISH -> long, BEARISH -> short (the day it first appears)
Not tested, and why: George & Hwang's 52-week high is a 6-month cross-sectional portfolio, not a
signal-day rule. Minervini has no primary-sourced exit. Weinstein is judgement.

WINDOWS (by entry session)
development: before 2024-09-19 | validation: 2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17 | final: 2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15

STATISTICS
Unit: the trade. Mean net % with errors clustered by calendar month of entry
(api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean). One-sided p from Student-t with (months - 1) df.
PASS = validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final p survives Holm-Bonferroni across all
36 members at 5% family-wise. Fewer than 30 final trades = NOT TESTABLE.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES
A PASS goes onto the page only as "passed on re-used data; paper first", tracked on TRACK, after
the owner sees the numbers. A FAIL is reported as a FAIL.

CODE CHECKS FIRST (synthetic only)
(1) A planted edge must be found by a trend rule.
(2) Random walks must give at most one chance PASS.
(3) Changing future prices must leave earlier trades identical.
