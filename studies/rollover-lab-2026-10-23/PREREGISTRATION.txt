PRE-REGISTRATION -- THE ROLLOVER LAB: DOES INDIAN BROKERS' "ROLLOVER DATA" PREDICT THE NEXT FUTURES SERIES? ONE BATCH, ONE RUN.
Written Friday 23 Oct 2026, about 21:20 IST, BEFORE any member below was computed on real data. The commit time is the
proof. Branch claude/rollover-lab-2026-10-23.
Owner, 23 Oct 2026: "keep testing more strategies".

1. WHAT IS KNOWN (disclosed)
- Rollover (the share of open interest carried into the next futures series at expiry) is a staple of Indian broker
  commentary: high rollover with a rising price is read as longs rolling (bullish), with a falling price as shorts rolling
  (bearish). Never tested on this desk (no earlier registration mentions rollover). The families study tested futures
  carry (basis), not rollover. Nothing below has been computed on real data.

2. DATA AND PERIODS
- NSE's F&O bhavcopy (stock and NIFTY futures, open interest per expiry) and CM bhavcopy. Discovery 2005-01 .. 2020-10
  (release data-oos); confirmation 2020-10 .. 2026-10-01 (the research snapshot, with data-oos before 2020-09-01 as
  look-back only); they share October 2020.
- An EXPIRY DAY X is a session on which a futures expiry date listed in NSE's file equals the session (for stocks: the
  stock's nearest expiry). On X, from that day's F&O file: ROLLOVER = OI of the stock's contracts expiring after X / OI of all
  its contracts. SERIES MOVE = the share's close on X / its close on the previous expiry day - 1.
- Universe on X: the night lab's (top 200 by traded value, >= 250 bars after the last reset) for X's month, stocks with a
  futures contract expiring after X; at least 50 stocks, else no book.
- A trade: the contract expiring next after X (the new near month), bought or sold at its official open on the session after
  X, closed at its official close on its own expiry day. Charges: NSE futures charges on both legs, quantity
  ceil(Rs 5 lakh / entry). EXCESS = trade net minus (for a long) / plus (for a short) the equal-weighted return of the same
  universe's new near-month futures over the same window (at least 30 names).

3. MEMBERS (5)
 R1 ROLLING LONGS: ROLLOVER in the universe's top fifth AND SERIES MOVE > 0 -> LONG. Excess.
 R2 ROLLING SHORTS: ROLLOVER in the top fifth AND SERIES MOVE < 0 -> SHORT. Excess.
 R3 BOTH: R1's longs and R2's shorts in one book, (mean long + mean short) / 2. Raw.
 R4 UNWOUND: ROLLOVER in the universe's bottom fifth -> SHORT (positions closed rather than carried). Excess.
 R5 NIFTY: NIFTY's ROLLOVER above the median of its previous 12 expiries AND NIFTY's near-month future rose over the series
    -> LONG NIFTY's next contract from the open after X to its expiry; above the median and fell -> SHORT; else no trade. Raw.

4. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member: monthly mean (%) of its trades or books (by the month of entry); Newey-West t (lag 3). Holm across the 5 at 5%.
  Full Deflated Sharpe with N = 381 trials (376 before, these 5), as the night lab computes it.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2. A
  confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2. FAIL otherwise.
  NOT TESTABLE: fewer than 24 months with a trade.
- Reported, not judged: trades, names a side, win rate, worst month, mean a trade.
- Expectation, stated now: about 190 monthly series in discovery; the most likely outcome is that nothing passes both.

5. CHECKS BEFORE THE REAL RUN (a failure refuses the run)
 5.1 Rollover by hand: a stock with OI 600 in the expiring contract and 300 + 100 in later ones has ROLLOVER 0.4; its trade
     uses the next contract's open after X and its close on its own expiry.
 5.2 Planted: a synthetic market where top-fifth-rollover stocks that rose over the series gain 2% more over the next
     series: R1 reaches t >= 3.
 5.3 Null: every member's trades moved to random expiry days in discovery (same counts and sides): at most 1 PASS of 5.
