- A short build-up signal exactly as api/_lib/oi-flow-forward.js RULE SB (its universe, resets, sessions d-1 and d-2):
  R = close(d-1) / close(d-2) - 1 <= -1.5581% and OI = total futures OI(d-1) / total futures OI(d-2) - 1 >= +3.0380%;

  from the previous close). At least 30 universe names must have a bar on d, or the trade is not counted.
