AMENDMENT A1 TO THE STRONG SHORT BUILD-UP FORWARD TEST -- written Friday 23 Oct 2026, about 00:45 IST, BEFORE the first
session it covers (09:15 IST today) and before any of its trades exists.

WHY
A descriptive look tonight, after the 09:15 lab (studies/open-lab-2026-10-23) and so post-hoc, on NSE 2021 .. 2026-10-01:
- short build-up trades priced on the stock's near-month FUTURE (NSE's official futures open -> close; the instrument the
  owner trades on GM Global) earned +0.092% a trade (t 3.81, 8,591 trades), against +0.218% (t 9.06) for the same trades
  priced on the SHARE's official open -> close, as every desk test so far has priced them;
- across all 276,440 stock-days, the future gained 0.085% on the share from open to close (0.136% on days the share opened
  at or above its previous close). The futures' closing price equals NSE's settlement price in every file checked, so the
  difference arises at the open.
The test should judge what the owner can trade.

CHANGE
- NET and EXCESS are computed on FUTURES: the contract is the earliest expiry on or after d listed for the stock in NSE's
  F&O file of d (as scripts/oi-flow-forward.js futuresLast); entry = its official open, exit = its official close. Charges
  as before, quantity ceil(500000 / futures open).
- EXCESS = NET + the equal-weighted open-to-close return on d of the same universe's near-month futures (names with a
  futures price that day; at least 30, else the trade is not counted).
- A signal with no futures price on d is not counted (status NO FUTURES PRICE).
- The share-priced net, as first registered, is reported alongside, not judged.
Everything else is unchanged: the signal, the strong cut-offs, the start (2026-10-23), the 100 trades and 3 months, the
one-sided 5% t clustered by ISO week.
