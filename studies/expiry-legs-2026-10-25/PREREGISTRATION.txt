EXPIRY-DAY NIFTY OPTION SELLING: THE CALL SIDE AGAINST THE PUT SIDE -- PRE-REGISTRATION
Written Sun 25 Oct 2026, before any number below has been computed on real data. The sha256 of this file (LF line ends)
is in PREREGISTRATION.sha256; both are committed and pushed before the one real run.

1. WHY
The owner asked on 25 Oct 2026 whether, in option selling, the call and the put "are the same". The desk's one PASS
(studies/expiry-selling-2026-10-20, branch claude/expiry-selling-2026-10-20; member X2: sell the ATM call and the ATM put
at their opens on the NIFTY expiry session, buy a leg back at 2x its sale price if the day's high reaches 2x, else settle
it at intrinsic) reported only the two legs together. This study splits them.
It DESCRIBES a rule that has already been tested, on data that has already been examined (the study's sample). It makes
no new rule: nothing here can become a page row, a TRACK rule or a suggestion.

2. DATA AND RULES (the study's, unchanged)
- NIFTY option rows of NSE's F&O bhavcopy: release data-nifty-options of the private repository kuldeep-oo7/k2-data
  (nod-2019 .. nod-2026). NIFTY's open: NSE ind_close_all, "Open Index Value" of "Nifty 50".
- Sample: the study's sessions through 2026-10-19 (402 expiry sessions, 351 expiries a member). Later expiries belong to
  the forward test (studies/expiry-selling-forward-2026-10-21) and are not used.
- Strikes, stop, settlement and costs: leg, nearest and expiryDay of api/_lib/expiry-forward.js, which
  scripts/expiry-forward-replay.js proved identical to the study (MATCH). Costs per leg: the desk's option cost model for
  75 units plus 1 point, as registered. The registered study's own run() and synth() are imported from
  studies/expiry-selling-2026-10-20/study.js.

3. MEMBERS (per expiry, in points and in % of NIFTY's open, after costs)
 C2 / P2  X2's call leg / put leg alone (the strike nearest NIFTY's open; 2x stop).
 C4 / P4  X4's call leg / put leg alone (the call nearest 1.01 x the open, the put nearest 0.99 x the open; 2x stop).
 C1 / P1 and C3 / P3  the same legs held to settlement with no stop (X1, X3). Reported only, because they show the tail.

4. THE QUESTION AND ITS VERDICT (fixed now)
 MAIN: D2 = P2 - C2 for each expiry (% of NIFTY's open). Summed by calendar month, as in the study; t = Newey-West with
 lag 3 (the study's function, copied verbatim and proven by check 6.4); TWO-SIDED.
   |t| >= 3        DIFFERENT (and which side earned more)
   2 <= |t| < 3    LEANING (and which side), not proven
   |t| < 2         NO DIFFERENCE FOUND
 The word SAME is never used: not finding a difference does not prove there is none.
 SECONDARY: D4 = P4 - C4, the same words. No other test is judged.
 EACH LEG ALONE (C2, P2, C4, P4 and the held legs): mean, t, won %, worst expiry, worst month -- INFORMATION ONLY. No
 verdict, no PASS, no rule. Selling one side alone adds a NIFTY direction bet (a sold put gains when NIFTY rises, a sold
 call when it falls), and that bet has not been tested; every NIFTY direction rule the desk has tested FAILED. A large t
 on one leg is not a reason to sell that side alone.

5. KNOWN LIMITS (written before the run)
 L1. Put-call parity: for a call and a put of one strike held to settlement, the call's result minus the put's = minus
     NIFTY's move from its open to the settlement, plus the parity gap at the open. So a held-leg difference measures
     NIFTY's expiry-day drift, not option pricing. The 2x stop breaks the identity, which is why D2 is not simply the drift.
 L2. The options' opening prints and NIFTY's open are not printed at the same moment. The study's audit A found the
     parity gap at the open at -40.4 .. +30.9 points (10th..90th percentile). That moves value between the call and the
     put; it cancels in the straddle but not in a single leg, so the split is noisier than X2.
 L3. The data fills a stop at exactly 2x. A real stop-limit order can fill worse, or not at all (Zerodha: no SL-M orders
     on index options).
 L4. The sample has been examined before (the study, its audit, and the forward test's design).
 L5. Power is low. D2 carries NIFTY's whole expiry-day move, which is large next to any pricing gap. In synthetic run 2
     (calibration, no real data), a put sold 20% rich at the open was found DIFFERENT on 1 seed of 3 (t 3.56, -0.04,
     2.00). So NO DIFFERENCE FOUND can hide a real gap of that size.

6. CHECKS. If any fails, the run is VOID and no figure is reported.
 6.1 Fair prices (the study's synth(), seeds 1-3, no costs): |t| < 3 for D2 and for D4 on every seed (no false DIFFERENT).
 6.2 Puts sold rich, calls fair (the study's synth(), seeds 1-3, with costs): each put's OPEN is scaled up and the rest of
     its day is left fair (its high is at least its open). At 50% over fair, D2's mean must be above 0 (put side higher)
     on all three seeds and D2 must be DIFFERENT on at least two. The same at 20% is printed as the test's power, not
     judged. (Synthetic run 1 scaled the put's open AND high, as the study's own plant did; that leaves the stop on the
     same paths, so a put 50% dear moved D2 only to t 1.05 and the check failed. That plant was a poor model of a rich
     put; it was replaced before any real data was read. RESULT.txt keeps run 1.)
 6.3 Labels and arithmetic: with 10 points added to every put's open and high (seed 4), every held put (P1) earns exactly
     10 points more and no held call (C1) changes.
 6.4 The study reproduced on the real data, before any result is printed: 402 sessions, 51 skipped, 351 X2 expiries;
     C2 + P2 = X2 on every expiry (to 1e-9); X2's mean = 17.590993 points and 0.093487% (to 1e-9 and 1e-12); X2's monthly
     t from the copied function = 4.425789 (to 1e-9). If 6.4 fails, the cause is found and written down before anything
     else is done.

7. REPORTED, NOT JUDGED
 - Each leg: mean points and %, won %, t, the mean premium at the open, how often its stop was hit, its worst expiry
   (with the date) and its worst month.
 - Each X2 leg's mean on expiries when NIFTY settled above its open, and when it settled below.
 - C2, P2 and D2 in the study's three periods (to 19 Nov 2024; 20 Nov 2024 - 1 Sep 2025; from 2 Sep 2025).

 - One real run. A re-run is allowed only if a check fails, for a reason then written here as an amendment before the
