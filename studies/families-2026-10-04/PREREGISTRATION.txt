PRE-REGISTRATION -- INSTITUTIONAL FOOTPRINTS AND THE UNTESTED TRADING FAMILIES
Written 4 Oct 2026 BEFORE any signal or result was computed for these rules. sha256 in PREREGISTRATION.sha256.

WHY
The owner asked to test the institutional footprints (delivery, option positioning) and the
trading-style families not yet tried: futures carry, pairs trading, calendar effects and the
options volatility premium. Every member is a short-horizon TRADE (the owner, 4 Oct: "trading,
not investing"). Bulk/block deals were dropped BEFORE any test. NSE's history API returns at most
70 rows per request, so a complete history cannot be read (checked 4 Oct: a week and a month both
returned exactly 70).

DATA (NSE's own files, checked on NSE 3-4 Oct 2026)
- CM bhavcopy 2020-09..2026-10 (release data-nse-cm); NIFTY 50 history (nifty-50-nse.json).
- F&O bhavcopy, futures rows (research/orderflow-2026-10-03/nse-fo/fut) and option aggregates
  (nse-extra/opt), both from content/historical/DERIVATIVES (to 2024-07-05) and content/fo (UDiFF).
- Delivery: products/content/sec_bhavdata_full_DDMMYYYY.csv (DELIV_PER), series EQ.
Universe, resets, windows and costs are as in research/ta-atlas-2026-10-03:
- top 200 by 60-session median traded value, at least 250 bars after the last reset;
- a reset is an open >= 18% from the previous close; a trade crossing a reset is dropped;
- futures charges both legs, dated, ceil(500000/price), a roll per month end crossed;
- options pay api/_lib/trade-costs.js round-trip charges.

MEMBERS (Holm across all 12; each one a trade)
INSTITUTIONAL FOOTPRINTS
  F1  Delivery accumulation. DELIV_PER(d-1) >= the 90th percentile of the stock's own previous 60
      sessions' DELIV_PER, AND close(d-1) > close(d-2) -> long open(d) to close(d+4).
      Source: IIM Calcutta WP 863: delivery % rises before large institutional (bulk) trades.
  F2  Delivery distribution. The same delivery condition AND close(d-1) < close(d-2) -> short,
      open(d) to close(d+4).
  F3  NIFTY option put-call ratio, contrarian. PCR = total NIFTY put OI / call OI (all expiries)
      on d-1. Top tercile (cut-offs from development days only) -> long NIFTY open(d) to
      close(d+9). Source: Jena et al. 2019: OI-PCR predicts NIFTY returns over about 12 days.
  F4  The same, bottom tercile -> short NIFTY.
FUTURES CARRY (Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen & Vrugt 2018, "Carry", JFE)
  F5  Each session, the near-month stock future with 5..30 calendar days to expiry:
      carry = (spot close(d-1) / future close(d-1) - 1) x 365 / days-to-expiry. Universe stocks
      in the top 10% of carry (the future cheapest against the share) -> long that future,
      open(d) to close(d+4), only if the contract does not expire inside the hold.
  F6  The bottom 10% -> short that future.
PAIRS TRADING (Gatev, Goetzmann & Rouwenhorst 2006, RFS)
  F7  On the first session of each month: among universe stocks, normalise each price to 1 at the
      start of the previous 250 sessions; pick the 20 pairs with the smallest sum of squared
      differences. During the next 125 sessions, when a pair's normalised spread moves more than 2
      of its formation-period standard deviations, short the high one and buy the low one at the
      next open. Close at the next open after the spread crosses zero, or at the last close of the
      125 sessions. Each pair trades at most once at a time. Return = the average of the two legs.
      Both legs pay futures charges.
CALENDAR (all on NIFTY, index as the stand-in for its future, futures charges)
  F8  Turn of the month (Ariel 1987; Lakonishok & Smidt 1988): long from the open of the last
      session of a month to the close of the 3rd session of the next month.
  F9  Monthly-expiry week: long from the open of the 4th session before each NIFTY FUTIDX expiry
      to the close of the expiry session. Expiries from
      research/situations-2026-09-29/nifty-expiries-nse.json (NSE's own list).
  F10 Monday: short from the Monday open to the Monday close. A Monday that is a holiday is skipped.
OPTIONS VOLATILITY PREMIUM (Carr & Wu 2009, "Variance Risk Premiums", RFS)
  F11 Short NIFTY straddle. On the first session of each NIFTY weekly-expiry cycle with 4 to 10
      calendar days left, sell the call and the put at the strike nearest to NIFTY's close, at their
      closing prices; hold to expiry, settled at intrinsic value against NIFTY's close on the
      expiry date. Return = (premium - payout - charges) / NIFTY close, as % of notional.
      The worst single trade is also reported.
  F12 The same with BANKNIFTY (monthly expiries only once NSE dropped its weeklies; nearest expiry
      with 4..35 days left), against the BANKNIFTY close from the futures file's IDX rows (nearest
      future's close as the index stand-in).

WINDOWS
development: before 2024-09-19 | validation: 2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17 | final: 2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15

STATISTICS
The unit is the trade, clustered by calendar month (api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean).
p is one-sided Student-t with (months - 1) df.
PASS = validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final p survives Holm across all 12 at 5%.
Fewer than 30 final trades = NOT TESTABLE.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES
A PASS goes to TRACK as a forward paper test (like the springy dip), labelled, before any money.
F11/F12 are option SELLING. Even if they pass, they come with their worst-day loss on the page.
They need margin, which the owner's Zerodha options buying does not have, so they would be
information, not a row to buy.
