PRE-REGISTRATION -- THE DESK'S OWN RATING, REPLAYED ON SIX YEARS OF NSE DATA (first real test)
Written 4 Oct 2026 BEFORE any replayed rating or result was computed. sha256 in PREREGISTRATION.sha256.
Judged under research/audit-2026-10-04/PROTOCOL.txt (protocol v2).

WHY
The page has cited "the BUY signal failed its pre-registered final test (1,205 trades, t = 0.75; panel
study, 24 Sep 2026)". Reading scripts/apex-panel-study.js on 4 Oct 2026 showed it never ran the desk's
rating. It tested a stand-in ("5-day return > 1%, 20-day > 2%, volume > 1.1x its 20-day average"), with
the target counted before the stop on the same bar. The desk's actual rating has therefore never been
tested on history. This replays it, unchanged.

THE RATING (exactly the live code, as of commit b81325b; the candlestick term is neutral since 4 Oct)
For each session d, for each stock of that month's universe:
1. analyzeCandidate (api/_lib/unified-quant-scanner.js) on the stock's last 66 completed daily bars up to
   d-1 (the live desk feeds about three months). Bars come from NSE's CM bhavcopy (open/high/low/close/
   volume, series EQ, by symbol), unadjusted. A stock with a reset (an open >= 18% from the previous
   close) inside those 66 bars is skipped that day.
2. Cross-sectional percentiles across that day's rated stocks (universe-210.js):
   - neural percentile on rawZ;
   - composite percentile on rawCompScore. universe-210 also adds a balance-sheet adjustment from TODAY's
     fundamentals; that is look-ahead on history, so it is NOT applied. Stated.
3. Arbiter (universe-210.js):
   - BUY: composite >= 90 and neural >= 75; ACCUMULATE: >= 75 and >= 60;
   - SELL: <= 10 and <= 25; REDUCE: <= 25 and <= 40;
   - STAND_ASIDE if NSE's results meeting for the stock falls between d and that week's Friday (the
     results veto), or if the 20-session average traded value is under Rs 5 crore.
Universe: the top 200 by 60-session median traded value with >= 250 bars after the last reset (protocol
v2). The live desk uses the owner's 280-stock F&O list instead; the top 200 stands in for it.

THE TRADES (each signal day is a trade, like TRACK; trades may overlap)
- DESK GEOMETRY (the live futures rule, research/rising-stocks-2026-10-02):
  LONG: buy = open(d) x 0.992 rounded down to 0.05, filled only if low(d) < buy; target +2.5%,
  stop -1.5% (nearest 0.05); the stop first on any bar that touches both; gaps exit at the open; the
  fill day's target does not count; at most 20 sessions.
  SHORT: the exact mirror.
- FIXED HOLD: open(d) to close(d+19), 20 sessions.
Futures charges both legs, dated, quantity ceil(500000/price), a roll per month end crossed. A trade
crossing a reset is dropped.

MEMBERS (Holm across all 5)
  G1  BUY or ACCUMULATE, desk geometry long  -- THE LIVE FUTURES/CALLS RULE. Scored on net return.
  G2  BUY or ACCUMULATE, fixed 20-session long. Scored in EXCESS of B(d,20).
  G3  SELL or REDUCE, desk geometry short    -- what the PUTS tab leans on. Scored on net.
  G4  SELL or REDUCE, fixed 20-session short. Scored in EXCESS (net + B(d,20)).
  G5  BUY only (the top tier), fixed 20-session long. Scored in EXCESS.

VERDICT (protocol v2)
Monthly series over the whole sample with a Newey-West(3) t: PASS at t >= 3, PROMISING at 2-3,
otherwise FAIL. Holm across the 5 and the Deflated Sharpe probability are reported.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES
- G1 FAILS: the futures and calls tabs say "the desk's own rating was tested on six years of NSE
  data and failed", with its numbers. That REPLACES the wrong t = 0.75 citation.
- G1 PASSES: the tabs say so, with the numbers.
Either way the false citation is removed.

CODE CHECKS FIRST (synthetic only): the replay must reproduce a planted edge in a stock the rating
favours; noise must not pass; changing future prices must leave earlier ratings and trades identical.
