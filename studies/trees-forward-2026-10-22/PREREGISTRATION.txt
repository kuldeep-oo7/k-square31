THE TRADE (C1, as the study)
- At each rebalance feature date d with at least 50 names predicted: rank by the trees' prediction T. LONG the top
  floor(n/10), SHORT the bottom floor(n/10).
- Entry: the official open of the SECOND NSE session after d (features.js: e = S[i+2]; one session of lag for every file).
  Exit: the official close of the 5th session counted from the entry (the entry is the first). Books do not overlap.
- The list is made on the evening of the session after d (after NSE posts d's files) and counts only if it is committed
  to the repository before 09:15 IST on the entry day. A list made later is shown as LATE and not counted. A stored list
  is never recomputed or overwritten.
- Scored on the stocks' shares (NSE CM official open and close), as the study. A trade whose window crosses a reset (an
  open >= 18% from the previous close) is VOID. Charges: stock futures, calculateNseFuturesCharges (dated, quantity
  ceil(500000 / entry), both sides) plus one round trip per month end crossed, as the study.
- Value of a book V = (mean net of its longs + mean net of its shorts) / 2.
- The page shows, for information: which names have NSE stock futures (NSE's F&O file of d), the contract month, and
  GM's 20 units. Nothing from GM Global is read.

SCORING AND VERDICT
- Counted: every on-time book whose list was made after this registration's commit.
- Series: one value per book (books are weekly). t = Newey-West, 4 lags.
- VERDICT only at >= 52 books (about a year): PASS if mean > 0 AND t >= 3; otherwise FAIL. Before that, COLLECTING, with
  the running mean, winners and t. Power stated now: the study's per-book Sharpe was about 0.20, so 52 books give an
  expected t near 1.4; a PASS is unlikely unless the trees do better live than in their test.
