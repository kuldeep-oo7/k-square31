THE MODEL (fixed now; one fit, made after this file is committed)
- Training rows: every row of the day-trader lab's tables (run.js --features of that branch): discovery rows before
  2020-10-01 and confirmation rows from 2020-10-01 to 2026-10-01; its 18 features; label = FUTURES EXCESS > 0.
- Python 3.12, scikit-learn 1.9.1, numpy 2.5.4: HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=200, learning_rate=0.05,
  max_leaf_nodes=15, min_samples_leaf=500, l2_regularization=1.0, early_stopping=False, random_state=0), fitted once.
- The fit is deterministic and uses no data after 2026-10-01. The model file stays in the private repo k2-data (release
  daytrader-model); FIT.txt records the fit's date, rows and the file's sha256 before any session is scored.

LIVE BOOKS
- Every session d from 2026-10-26: the day's universe exactly as the lab (the night lab's top 200 by traded value with >= 250
  bars after the last reset; stocks with a near-month future, open and close above zero, on d), its 18 features computed
  exactly as the lab's features() from NSE's files only (CM bars to d-1 and the share's official open of d; F&O files to
  d-1; NIFTY's open of d and closes to d-1). Only what is known at 09:15 is used; the computation runs after the close.
- Rank by the model's probability; k = floor(n / 10), n >= 50 or no book. LONG the top k futures at their official open, SHORT
  the bottom k, all closed at the official close. Each leg net of calculateNseFuturesCharges (dated; quantity ceil(Rs 5 lakh /
  open)); the BOOK = (mean long leg + mean short leg) / 2, in % of the position.

VERDICT (one rule, one test)
COLLECTING until at least 60 sessions with a book AND 3 calendar months since 2026-10-26. Then PASS if the mean BOOK > 0 and
the one-sided t of the weekly means (ISO weeks) is at or above the one-sided 5% critical value of Student's t with
(weeks - 1) degrees of freedom (the next LOWER listed degrees of freedom when not listed); otherwise FAIL. A PASS makes it a
candidate for a real-money discussion with the owner, never automatic.
Reported, not judged: the long and short legs against the universe's futures, and a TOP-3 sub-book (the 3 highest and 3
lowest) for practicality.
