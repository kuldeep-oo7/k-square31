THE HONEST READING
- Only ONE rule holds up every way we look at it: SHORT BUILD-UP (the stock fell >= 1.56% and its futures
  open interest rose >= 3.04% -> short at the next open, cover at that day's close). Positive on unseen
  NSE data in every 5-year period, not driven by outliers, and positive in its own 2020-26 backtest too.
  But it has been shrinking for 15 years, slippage at the open is not counted, and it is a 1-day trade.
- The strangle replicated, but its edge has almost gone since 2015 and it carries crash risk.
- The springy dip and cheap-vol straddle "STRONG" verdicts do not survive a look at single trades.
- The 150-day shorts and the industry reversal failed everywhere: little reason to trust them.
