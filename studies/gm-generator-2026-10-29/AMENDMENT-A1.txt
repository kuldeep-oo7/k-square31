AMENDMENT A1 TO THE GM STRATEGY GENERATOR'S PRE-REGISTRATION (sha256 0a053cf5...).
Written Thursday 29 Oct 2026, about 22:45 IST, BEFORE the real run and before any real data was read. Both reasons below
come from the SYNTHETIC market only (check 6.2's market, run locally).

A1.1 CHECK 6.3 (the null) is changed.
- Was: each stock's GM-week outcomes circularly shifted by >= 52 weeks; G-LONG and G-SHORT both below t 2.
- Why: the shift keeps each stock's average outcome. A rule that merely tracks WHICH stocks did well over the years can
  pass it without any look-ahead. In the planted synthetic market, the shifted null still gave G-LONG t 3.11.
- Now (judged): within each week, the outcomes are randomly permuted among that week's stocks (labels fixed, as the
  futures lab's 5.3 shuffled within each month). The whole generator is re-run. G-LONG and G-SHORT must both be below
  t 2 over the 2008-2020 trading weeks. The circular shift is still run and REPORTED, not judged; it shows how much of
  any result is stock-level persistence.

A1.2 THE DEFLATED SHARPE BENCHMARK (SR0) is changed.
- Was: as the earlier labs, the standard deviation of the judged members' monthly Sharpe ratios.
- Why: with only 2 judged members, that spread is not an estimate of the spread across trials. In the planted synthetic
  market, a t 12.38 edge got DSR 0.00 because the other side was flat, so no PASS was possible at all.
- Now: SR0 uses the standard deviation of the monthly Sharpe ratios of all 930 rules the generator searches, over the
  same period, on the ranking value (GM gross excess). For each rule: the monthly means over the trading period, at
  least 24 months; a short rule's ratio is minus its long rule's. These are the trials actually searched. N stays 442.
  Everything else in the verdict is unchanged: t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95, then confirmation.

Nothing else changes.
