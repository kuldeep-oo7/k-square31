PRE-REGISTRATION -- THE GM STRATEGY GENERATOR: A MACHINE THAT WRITES AND RE-PICKS ITS OWN WEEKLY CHART RULES EVERY YEAR,
USING ONLY THE PAST, SCORED THE WAY GM GLOBAL SCORES. BOTH DIRECTIONS. ONE BATCH, ONE RUN.
Written Thursday 29 Oct 2026, about 22:15 IST, BEFORE any rule below was computed on real data. The commit time is the
proof. Branch claude/gm-generator-2026-10-29 (built on the GM weekly lab's branch).

1. WHY, AND WHAT HAS BEEN SEEN (disclosed)
- The owner, 29 Oct: "why don't you generate on your own new strategies". Tonight's chart lab (14 members) and GM weekly
  lab (10 members) both gave 0 PASS. Their results on 2005-2020 and 2020-2026 HAVE been seen, and several blocks below
  reuse their definitions. The tested object here is therefore not any single rule but the GENERATOR: a fixed procedure
  that re-picks rules every January from the past only, and whose trades are always out of sample.
- The blocks are taken mechanically from definitions already written (the weekly lab, the chart lab, the 09:15 lab's
  feature list); none is added or dropped because of tonight's results.

2. DATA, WEEKS AND GM SCORING: exactly the GM weekly lab's (studies/gm-weekly-lab-2026-10-29). Bought at the week's first
   session's open on the near-month future, booked at its last session's close, cut at expiry (rest of that week sat out),
   holdings join consecutive weeks, charges only at a holding's first open and last close, the same drop rules, the same
   market M_w and universe. One data load: NSE 2005-01 .. 2026-10-01 (data-oos to 2020-08, the research snapshot after).
   Futures open interest per stock per day = the sum over its expiries (as the futures lab).

3. THE 30 BLOCKS (true/false for a stock at the close of week w-1, for week w; "fifth" = the top or bottom floor(n/5) of
   that week's universe ranked on the value, n >= 30 with a value; weekly bars and validity as the weekly lab)
  B1 close > SMA30          B2 close < SMA30          B3 SMA30 rising (vs 4 bars earlier)   B4 SMA30 falling
  B5 weekly Donchian state long (W2L)                 B6 weekly Donchian state short (W2S)
  B7 relative strength top fifth (W3's 25-week return, skipping the latest week)    B8 relative strength bottom fifth
  B9 weekly bullish engulfing or hammer               B10 weekly bearish engulfing or shooting star
  B11 at the line from above: low <= 1.02 x SMA30 and close >= 0.98 x SMA30
  B12 at the line from below: high >= 0.98 x SMA30 and close <= 1.02 x SMA30
  B13 NIFTY trend up (W5's signal +1)                 B14 NIFTY trend down (W5's signal -1)
  B15 last week's return top fifth                    B16 last week's return bottom fifth
  B17 weekly volume / mean of the previous 20 weekly volumes: top fifth      B18 bottom fifth
  B19 weekly range (high - low) / mean of the previous 10 weekly ranges: bottom fifth (tight)   B20 top fifth (wide)
  B21 futures OI at the week's last session / at the previous week's last session: top fifth (build-up)
  B22 the same: bottom fifth (unwinding)
  B23 basis = near-month future close / share close - 1 at the week's last session: top fifth   B24 bottom fifth
  B25 close >= 0.95 x the highest high of the last 250 daily bars    B26 close <= 1.05 x the lowest low of the last 250
  B27 standard deviation of the last 20 daily close-to-close returns: top fifth    B28 bottom fifth
  B29 share price (close): top fifth                  B30 bottom fifth
- CONDITIONS: each block alone (30) and each pair of blocks joined by AND (435): 465 conditions. A contradictory pair
  simply never fires. Each condition is a LONG rule and a SHORT rule: 930 rules.

4. THE GENERATOR (walk-forward, every January from 2008)
- Training data for year Y: every position-week whose last session is BEFORE 1 Jan Y (so nothing that ends in Y is read).
- Ranking value of a position-week = GM gross excess = (close / open - 1) - M_w (no charges). For each condition: its
  monthly mean of that value (month of the week's first session) and the Newey-West t (lag 3); a LONG rule's t is that t,
  a SHORT rule's t is minus it. Eligible: at least 36 months and 200 position-weeks in the training data, t > 0.
- Selection: the 5 best LONG rules and the 5 best SHORT rules by t, in order, skipping a rule if 80% or more of its
  training position-weeks are also in a rule already selected on the same side.
- Trading year Y (weeks whose first session falls in Y): a stock is a LONG pick in week w if any selected long rule fires,
  a SHORT pick if any selected short rule fires; a stock that is both is dropped. Picks are ranked by the sum of the
  training t of the rules that fire for them (then by symbol); the BOOK keeps the top 5 long and top 5 short picks a week.
- JUDGED (2 members): G-LONG = the long book, G-SHORT = the short book; value of a position-week = GM EXCESS net of the
  exact holding charges (the weekly lab's value()). Reported, not judged: all picks without the cap; the rules selected
  each year (block names only); GM rupees at 20 units; expiry months up; worst month; the top-priced tenth.

5. SCORE AND VERDICT
- DISCOVERY = trading weeks 2008-01-01 .. 2020-10-31; CONFIRMATION = trading weeks 2020-10-01 .. 2026-10-01 (the same
  walk-forward continuing). Monthly mean of the values; Newey-West t (lag 3); fewer than 24 months: NOT TESTABLE. Holm
  across the 2 at 5%. Full Deflated Sharpe with N = 442 (440 before, plus these 2; the 930 rules inside are chosen out
  of sample each year and are not separate trials).
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm and DSR >= 0.95. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2.
  PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2. A confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first.
- Expectation, stated now: 0 PASS. Re-picked chart rules usually chase last year's winner.

6. CHECKS BEFORE THE REAL RUN (a failure refuses the run)
 6.1 The weekly lab's GM bookkeeping hand check.
 6.2 Planted: a synthetic market (250 stocks, 3,000 sessions) with +0.3% a session in the week after every "B9 AND B3"
     (weekly bullish candle with a rising SMA30): the generator must select that LONG rule in a year after it is eligible,
     and G-LONG must reach t >= 3 over the synthetic trading years.
 6.3 Null: each stock's GM-week outcomes circularly shifted by a random number of weeks (52 or more), the whole generator
     re-run: G-LONG and G-SHORT both below t 2.
 6.4 No look-ahead: blocks recomputed on bars cut after the signal week (3,000 stock-weeks for per-stock blocks, 100 weeks
     for the cross-sectional ones) give the same answers; and every training position-week used for year Y ends before
     1 Jan Y (asserted while ranking).
 6.5 Counts printed before any value is scored or shown.
