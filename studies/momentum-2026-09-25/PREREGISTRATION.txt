PRE-REGISTRATION -- CROSS-SECTIONAL MOMENTUM ON THE K2 F&O PANEL
Written 2026-09-25, BEFORE any result of this test was computed.
Its sha256 is recorded before the study runs; nothing below may change after.

WHY
The user asked to apply the "universal" methods of the trading map to their three
instruments only (stock futures, calls, puts). Momentum was profiled on this panel
before as a SHORT-TERM FILTER on 5-session ladder trades and flipped sign. The
literature's momentum is a different strategy: rank stocks on their past-year
return and HOLD the winners for a month or more. This tests that strategy once.

DATA
.cache/panel-bars: 97 NSE F&O large caps, daily bars 2021-09-15 .. 2026-09-15.
Month-end close = the last daily close in each calendar month (IST date).

THE ONE PRIMARY RULE (no variants will be run)
- Formation at month-end F. Signal = 12-1 momentum = close(F-1) / close(F-12) - 1
  (the past twelve months, skipping the most recent month).
- Eligible: a stock with month-end closes at F-12, F-1, F and H = F+1.
- Hold during month H (month-end F close to month-end H close), equal weight.
- LONG leg  = the 10 highest signals.  SHORT leg = the 10 lowest signals.
- Benchmark = the equal-weight average return of ALL eligible stocks in month H.
  (Not Nifty: this panel is today's large caps and survivorship-biased; against the
  index a long-only result would mostly measure that bias.)
- Costs: 0.20% per month per leg (a full round trip each month: brokerage, STT,
  exchange, stamp and slippage, assumed; NSE futures costs are not re-verified here).

TWO RESULTS, REPORTED SEPARATELY
- H1 (futures and calls):  LONG  - benchmark - 0.20%  per month.
- H2 (puts):               benchmark - SHORT - 0.20%  per month.

WINDOWS (by the last day of the holding month; the calendar of
K2_PREREGISTERED_OUT_OF_SAMPLE_PROTOCOL.txt)
- development: holding months ending before 2024-09-19   (Oct 2022 .. Aug 2024)
- validation:  2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17                  (Sep 2024 .. Aug 2025)
- final test:  2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15, complete months (Sep 2025 .. Aug 2026)

DECISION RULE, FOR EACH OF H1 AND H2
PASS  = final-test mean net monthly excess > 0
        AND final-test t >= 1.6648 (plain t on monthly excess returns)
        AND validation mean net monthly excess > 0
FAIL  = anything else. There is no third outcome.

WHAT A PASS MEANS, STATED IN ADVANCE
- This data is NOT untouched: 12-1 momentum was profiled on all three windows before
  (PANEL_STUDY_ROUND3, as a conditioner). So a PASS promotes the rule to PAPER
  TRACKING on the desk's TRACK tab, never straight to live suggestions.
- Low power: about 12 monthly returns per test window. A pass needs a large effect;
  a FAIL is weak evidence against momentum, not proof that it never works.
- Options are not backtested (no five-year option price history). H1 decides whether
  calls on the long names are shown; H2 decides whether puts return at all.
- Reversal, relative strength and distance from the 52-week high are NOT retested:
  they flipped sign on this data already, and retesting them is searching.
