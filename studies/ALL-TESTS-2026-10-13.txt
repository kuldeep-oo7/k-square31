================================================================================================================
1. HOW EVERY TEST WAS RUN (the method)
================================================================================================================
- Rules first: each test's rules were written down and hashed with sha256 BEFORE any result was computed on real
  data. Changing a rule after seeing a result changes the hash. Each test was then run once.
- Costs included: futures and options charges (brokerage, STT, exchange, stamp, GST) are taken off every trade.
- Two judging standards were used:
  * Protocol v1 (Sep to 4 Oct): data split into 3 periods in time order (development, validation, final year).
    PASS needed: validation mean > 0, final mean > 0, and a final-year t-statistic over a Holm-corrected bar.
    Weakness (found 4 Oct): one final year is too short. A real but modest edge would usually FAIL. So those
    FAILs mean "not proven", not "proven useless".
  * Protocol v2 (from 4 Oct, studies/audit-2026-10-04/PROTOCOL.txt): the whole sample, return in EXCESS of the
    market for stock trades, a monthly Newey-West t >= 3 to PASS, with Holm (many-tests correction) and the
    Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey and Lopez de Prado 2014) reported. t >= 3 is Harvey, Liu and Zhu (2016).
- Data: NSE's own files wherever possible (nsearchives.nseindia.com / www.nseindia.com). Exceptions are named below.
- A PASS would go to TRACK as a paper test first, never straight onto the tabs.

----- studies/audit-2026-10-04/PROTOCOL.txt -----
EVALUATION PROTOCOL v2 -- re-scoring every strategy tested on 2-4 Oct 2026, written BEFORE re-scoring.
sha256 in PROTOCOL.sha256.

WHY (the audit, 4 Oct 2026)
The owner asked whether the testing itself is wrong. Measured against the literature, the old verdict
rule had too little statistical power:
- It judged each strategy on the final 12 months only, clustered by month (11 df).
- It required the validation year to be positive as well.
- It applied Holm across up to 36 members at once.
The one-sided critical t was then about 4 on 12 monthly points: a strategy needed an annual Sharpe
of about 4 inside one year to PASS. Good real strategies run a Sharpe of 1-2.
Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016, RFS 29(1)) set the bar for a new factor at t > 3.0 over the WHOLE sample.
Bailey & Lopez de Prado (2014) correct the Sharpe ratio for the number of trials. Splitting six
years into three windows is needed only where parameters were chosen on the data. Most rules here
are PUBLISHED with their own parameters, fixed long before 2020, so all six years are out-of-sample
for them.
Also, long-only stock signals were scored on raw returns, so market moves swamped them. The
standard is the return in EXCESS of the market.

WHAT IS RE-SCORED (rules and trades unchanged; only the judging changes)
Every member of research/rising-stocks-2026-10-02 (3), ta-atlas-2026-10-03 (36), dip-ml-2026-10-04
(10), orderflow-2026-10-03 (12), families-2026-10-04 (12) and results-vrp-2026-10-04 (5): 78
members. Each study's own run() produces its trades from the same data. The earlier panel studies
(composite, momentum, published strategies, regime, situations) used other data and are not
included; that is stated.

THE NEW SCORE
1. Excess return. For a stock trade of a member with a fixed holding h (sessions) entered at the
   open of d:
     excess = net - side x B(d, h),
   where B is the equal-weighted return, open(d) to close(d+h-1), of that month's universe stocks
   (the top 200 by 60-session median traded value, at least 250 bars after the last reset, whole
   window traded, no reset inside). side is +1 for a long and -1 for a short.
   Members on NIFTY, BANKNIFTY or options, pairs (already hedged), and desk-geometry members
   (variable exit) are scored on net return; the score says which.
   Fixed holdings (in sessions):
   - ta-atlas: 10;
   - dip-ml: D6 = 1, the rest = 5;
   - orderflow: B odd = 1, B even = 10;
   - families: F1, F2, F5, F6 = 5;
   - results: R1, R2 = 10.
2. Monthly series. For each calendar month from Sep 2020 to Sep 2026 with at least one trade: the
   mean excess (or net) per trade of the trades entered that month.
3. Statistic. t = mean of the monthly series / its Newey-West standard error (lag 3), over ALL
   months of all three old windows together. Annualised Sharpe of the monthly series = mean / sd x
   sqrt(12), with the number of months.
4. Verdict.
   PASS       = mean > 0 AND t >= 3.0 (Harvey-Liu-Zhu).
   PROMISING  = mean > 0 AND 2.0 <= t < 3.0. Never a trading rule by itself; a candidate for a
                forward paper test.
   FAIL       = otherwise.
   NOT TESTABLE = fewer than 24 months with trades.
   Also reported:
   - Holm across all 78 (one-sided, 5% family-wise; normal approximation, since df are about 70);
   - the Deflated Sharpe Ratio probability (Bailey & Lopez de Prado 2014, with N = 78 trials, using
     the cross-member variance of the annualised Sharpe ratios), a DSR >= 0.95 counting as
     significant after selection.
5. Members whose parameters were fitted on this data are flagged FITTED: the order-flow terciles,
   the PCR terciles, the results cut-offs, and ridge ML (refit walk-forward, so its own trades are
   out-of-sample by construction). For them only, the old development window is excluded and the
   score uses validation + final.
6. Power, stated with the result: over N months a true annual Sharpe S gives t of about
   S x sqrt(N/12); with N = 72, t = 3 needs S of about 1.22.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES

Exactly as research/ta-atlas-2026-10-03/PREREGISTRATION.txt:
- NSE CM bhavcopy 2020-09..2026-10, series EQ by symbol;

TRADE (uniform)
A signal is read from completed bars up to d-1.
- LONG: buy at open(d), sell at close(d+4) (5 sessions); SHORT: the reverse.
- Exception: D6 is intraday, open(d) to close(d).
- Costs: calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs, dated, quantity ceil(500000/price), plus a roll
  round trip per month end crossed. Net %.
- A trade whose window contains a reset is dropped.

MEMBERS (Holm across all 10)
Published mean reversion:
  D1  Short-term reversal (Lehmann 1990, Jegadeesh 1990): each day, the universe stocks in the
      bottom 10% by 5-session return (close d-1 vs close d-6) -> long.
  D2  The same, the top 10% -> short.
  D3  Connors-style trend dip: close(d-1) > SMA200 AND it is the third lower close in a row -> long.
  D4  Internal bar strength: IBS = (close-low)/(high-low) of d-1 <= 0.2 AND close > SMA200 -> long.
  D5  Oversold in an uptrend: RSI(14) of d-1 < 35 AND SMA50 > SMA200 -> long (first day of the episode).
  D6  Gap-down fade: open(d) <= close(d-1) x 0.98 -> long at open(d), exit at close(d).
      The open is known when the trade is placed.
Our own (new; labelled OWN on any page):
  E1  OU dip: a stock whose own lag-1 autocorrelation of daily returns over the previous 120 bars
      is below -0.05 (it mean-reverts, like an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process), AND whose
      z = (close - SMA20) / sd20 <= -2 on d-1 -> long.
  E2  The same filter, z >= +2 -> short.

THE RULES (identical to studies/options-2026-10-04/study.js)
Data: NSE's own files only.
- CM bhavcopy (series EQ, by symbol).
- F&O bhavcopy OPTSTK: the two nearest expiries, strikes within +/-12% of the CM close.
- Industries: the latest "sm_indusrty" of NSE's board-meeting filings
  (research/industry-2026-10-04/industries.json, 2,584 symbols, sha256
  47c3516eebd6ff1205794ddfd26391541bbe4e6796b6250d98a7bf1447826405; kept in release data-research,
  research-misc.tar.gz). Only this exact file is used. If it is missing or its sha256 differs, INDREV
  is NOT RUN that day and the page says so; it is never computed with another file.
Universe: each month, the top 200 EQ stocks by 60-session median traded value, with >= 250 bars after
the last reset (an open >= 18% from the previous close).
CHEAPVOL. On each session d, for each universe stock that has options:
- the nearest expiry with >= 8 calendar days left; ATM = the listed strike nearest the CM close of d;
- straddle IV = (CE close + PE close) / (0.8 x S x sqrt(days/365)) on d;
- realised = the annualised population SD of the last 20 daily log returns of the CM close up to d.
If IV < 0.8 x realised, BUY the straddle at the closing prices of d and SELL at the closing prices of d+5
(5 sessions), at most once per stock per 5 sessions. The 6 sessions must be consecutive with no reset.
Net = (exit - entry - option charges) / entry, as % of premium. The charges are
api/_lib/trade-costs.js roundTripCost .totalCost per leg, quantity round(100000 / leg premium).
The entry is the official CLOSE of the signal day. In practice the owner would act near 15:25 on
near-close prices; the paper test uses the official close. Stated.
INDREV. On every 5th session of NSE's session list counted from 2020-09-01 (index divisible by 5):
- each universe stock's 5-session return (close d-1 / close d-6), minus the equal-weighted 5-session
  return of the universe stocks in its industry (industries with >= 3 such stocks);
- LONG the bottom 10%, SHORT the top 10%, from open(d) to close(d+4).
Futures charges on every leg (calculateNseFuturesCharges, dated, quantity ceil(500000/price), a roll per
month end crossed). Score = (mean long net + mean short net) / 2, one observation per rebalance.

PRE-REGISTRATION -- THE GAP-DOWN FILTER ON THE DESK'S OWN FUTURES RULES
Written 2026-09-29 (IST, after midnight), BEFORE any result of this test was computed.
Its sha256 is recorded before the study runs; nothing below may change after.

WHY
On Mon 28 Sep 2026 NIFTY 50 fell 1.56% (NSE: 23,140.50 -> 22,780.25) and the desk's
futures took 23 stops and 1 target of 24 finished. The user asked for "only one" trade
that makes money and approved a proper test (29 Sep). The desk's own ranking cannot be
replayed on past days, and the rankings that can be replayed already failed on this
panel (12-1 momentum, time-series momentum, low volatility, the 10-month trend filter;
the desk's own ranking t = 0.75). What CAN be tested honestly is a timing rule on the
desk's own futures geometry: do not take the day's buys on a morning NIFTY opens down.
This is one rule, tested once.

DATA (all from NSE's own site, saved beside this file before the study runs)
- Stocks: the 97 names of .cache/panel-bars, daily open/high/low/close from NSE's
  security-wise history (series EQ, unadjusted), 2021-09-01 .. 2026-09-28, following
  renames through NSE's symbol-change file (nse-panel/). The Yahoo panel is NOT used: it
  had wrong bars (IRFC 15 Jan 2024 flat at 109.55; NSE: 116.70 -> 130.10).
  Today's large caps: survivorship bias, upward; stated.
- Sessions: NSE's own NIFTY 50 index history (nifty-50-nse.json); a stock bar on a date
  that is not in it is dropped.
- NIFTY 50 open and previous close: the same file.
- Removed stock-days (corporate-actions-nse.json and NSE's dividend filings):
  (a) the ex-date of every bonus, split, consolidation, demerger, rights issue, scheme of
      arrangement, capital reduction or amalgamation, and any day whose open/previous-close
      ratio equals one of that stock's bonus or split ratios within 1.5%
      (api/_lib/risk-view.js contaminatedDays);
  (b) every ex-dividend date whose dividend per share is at least 1% of the previous
      close (an unadjusted share drops by the dividend; a future does not);
  (c) any day with no range (open = high = low = close).
  A trade whose entry day or any holding session is a removed day is dropped.

THE DESK'S FUTURES RULES (api/_lib/session-levels.js, scripts/paper-ledger.js), ON DAILY BARS
Entry day d, every stock with a bar on d:
- anchor = open(d); buy = anchor x 0.992 rounded DOWN to 0.05.
- filled on d only if low(d) < buy (a trade through the limit); otherwise UNFILLED (a day
  order; not a trade).
- target = buy x 1.025, stop = buy x 0.985, each rounded to the nearest 0.05.
- the fill day: low(d) <= stop is a STOP at the stop (the ambiguity counts against the
  rule); a target touched on the fill day is NOT counted.
- each later session s, up to the 20th session counting d as the first:
  open(s) <= stop -> exit at open(s) (a gap); open(s) >= target -> exit at open(s);
  low(s) <= stop -> exit at the stop (the stop first if both are touched);
  high(s) >= target -> exit at the target. Otherwise exit at the close of the 20th
  session. A trade still open when the data ends (2026-09-28) exits at the last close.
- net return = exit / buy - 1 - charges, where charges = api/_lib/shadow-broker.js
  calculateNseFuturesCharges for the BUY on d and the SELL on the exit day (the dated STT
  schedule), quantity = ceil(500000 / buy) (about one NSE lot), divided by buy x quantity.
LIMITATION, stated in advance: daily bars cannot say whether a day's low came before the
desk's freeze time (09:16-09:33); the open stands in for the price at the freeze.

THE SIGNAL
gap(d) = NIFTY 50 open(d) / NIFTY 50 close(previous session) - 1.
GAP-DOWN MORNING: gap(d) <= -0.50%. One threshold, fixed here, not varied.

THE UNIT OF ANALYSIS
The entry day. The day's return = the mean net return of that day's filled trades (a day
with no fill is not an observation). Standard errors clustered by ISO week
(api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean): trades on one day, and days in one week, are not
independent (on the worst days about 95% of these stocks fall together).

WINDOWS (by entry day; the calendar every earlier study on this panel used)
- development: before 2024-09-19
- validation:  2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17
- final test:  2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15

PRIMARY HYPOTHESIS -- THE ONLY ONE THAT CAN PASS
H1  FILTERED: every entry day EXCEPT gap-down mornings.
    PASS = validation mean > 0
           AND final mean > 0
           AND final t >= the one-sided 5% Student-t critical value with
               (number of final-window weeks - 1) degrees of freedom.
    FAIL = anything else.

SECONDARY (reported; none of them can pass anything)
S1  UNFILTERED: every entry day.
S2  GAP-DOWN MORNINGS ONLY.
S3  H1 - S1 per window.

WHAT A PASS MEANS, STATED IN ADVANCE
- PAPER tracking on the TRACK tab only, never straight to real money; the page keeps
  saying there is no proven edge until paper tracking agrees.
- A FAIL means the filter does not make the desk's futures pay on this evidence, and the
  page and the user are told so plainly.
- Options are not tested: there is no five-year option price history here.
- If a coding error surfaces after the first run, it is fixed to THIS text and both runs
  are reported (as with the momentum study of 25 Sep).

PRE-REGISTRATION -- CROSS-SECTIONAL MOMENTUM ON THE K2 F&O PANEL
Written 2026-09-25, BEFORE any result of this test was computed.
Its sha256 is recorded before the study runs; nothing below may change after.

WHY
The user asked to apply the "universal" methods of the trading map to their three
instruments only (stock futures, calls, puts). Momentum was profiled on this panel
before as a SHORT-TERM FILTER on 5-session ladder trades and flipped sign. The
literature's momentum is a different strategy: rank stocks on their past-year
return and HOLD the winners for a month or more. This tests that strategy once.

DATA
.cache/panel-bars: 97 NSE F&O large caps, daily bars 2021-09-15 .. 2026-09-15.
Month-end close = the last daily close in each calendar month (IST date).

THE ONE PRIMARY RULE (no variants will be run)
- Formation at month-end F. Signal = 12-1 momentum = close(F-1) / close(F-12) - 1
  (the past twelve months, skipping the most recent month).
- Eligible: a stock with month-end closes at F-12, F-1, F and H = F+1.
- Hold during month H (month-end F close to month-end H close), equal weight.
- LONG leg  = the 10 highest signals.  SHORT leg = the 10 lowest signals.
- Benchmark = the equal-weight average return of ALL eligible stocks in month H.
  (Not Nifty: this panel is today's large caps and survivorship-biased; against the
  index a long-only result would mostly measure that bias.)
- Costs: 0.20% per month per leg (a full round trip each month: brokerage, STT,
  exchange, stamp and slippage, assumed; NSE futures costs are not re-verified here).

TWO RESULTS, REPORTED SEPARATELY
- H1 (futures and calls):  LONG  - benchmark - 0.20%  per month.
- H2 (puts):               benchmark - SHORT - 0.20%  per month.

WINDOWS (by the last day of the holding month; the calendar of
K2_PREREGISTERED_OUT_OF_SAMPLE_PROTOCOL.txt)
- development: holding months ending before 2024-09-19   (Oct 2022 .. Aug 2024)
- validation:  2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17                  (Sep 2024 .. Aug 2025)
- final test:  2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15, complete months (Sep 2025 .. Aug 2026)

DECISION RULE, FOR EACH OF H1 AND H2
PASS  = final-test mean net monthly excess > 0
        AND final-test t >= 1.6648 (plain t on monthly excess returns)
        AND validation mean net monthly excess > 0
FAIL  = anything else. There is no third outcome.

WHAT A PASS MEANS, STATED IN ADVANCE
- This data is NOT untouched: 12-1 momentum was profiled on all three windows before
  (PANEL_STUDY_ROUND3, as a conditioner). So a PASS promotes the rule to PAPER
  TRACKING on the desk's TRACK tab, never straight to live suggestions.
- Low power: about 12 monthly returns per test window. A pass needs a large effect;
  a FAIL is weak evidence against momentum, not proof that it never works.
- Options are not backtested (no five-year option price history). H1 decides whether
  calls on the long names are shown; H2 decides whether puts return at all.
- Reversal, relative strength and distance from the 52-week high are NOT retested:
  they flipped sign on this data already, and retesting them is searching.

PRE-REGISTRATION -- FORWARD PAPER TESTS of two OPEN-INTEREST patterns
Written Sunday 4 Oct 2026, BEFORE the first session they cover (Monday 5 Oct 2026). The commit time
is the proof.

WHERE THEY COME FROM
research/orderflow-2026-10-03 (pre-registered, sha256 22d0f927..., run once 4 Oct 2026 on NSE's F&O
and CM bhavcopies 2020-09..2026-10). Both FAILED there.
  B3  short build-up -> short for 1 session: positive in all three windows (development +0.296%,
      t 5.87, 5,576 trades; validation +0.020%, 1,604; final +0.131%, t 1.86, 1,325). Not
      significant after the correction for 12 tests.
  B8  long unwinding -> SHORT for 10 sessions LOST in all three windows (development -1.970%,
      t -4.96, 3,225; validation -0.847%, 768; final -1.036%, t -1.76, 433). Read the other way,
      that is a BOUNCE: the stock rises after a long unwinding. The bounce (going LONG) is a new
      reading of old numbers. Only future prices can test it, and both patterns have been seen, so
      both are tested forward.

DEFINITIONS (identical to research/orderflow-2026-10-03 except the bounce's direction)
Data: NSE's own CM bhavcopy (series EQ, by symbol) and F&O bhavcopy (futures rows; a stock's total
open interest = the sum over all its futures expiries).
Universe and resets: as research/orderflow-2026-10-03:
- the top 200 by 60-session median traded value;
- a reset is an open >= 18% from the previous close; a trade crossing one is void.
For entry session d, from sessions d-1 and d-2 (both trading sessions in a row, no reset):
  R  = close(d-1) / close(d-2) - 1  (the share)
  OI = totalOI(d-1) / totalOI(d-2) - 1
  A "moving" day: |R| >= 1.5581% AND |OI| >= 3.0380%. These are the cut-offs fixed on the old
  development window, frozen here.
RULE LU (long-unwinding BOUNCE):  R < 0 and OI < 0 on a moving day -> BUY at the official open of d,
                                   SELL at the official close of d+9 (10 sessions).
RULE SB (short build-up):          R < 0 and OI > 0 on a moving day -> SELL SHORT at the official open
                                   of d, BUY back at the official close of d (1 session).
Net = the signed price return - calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs (dated, quantity
ceil(500000/open)) - a roll round trip per month end crossed. Paper futures trades; no option is priced.

Written 4 Oct 2026 BEFORE any signal or result was computed for these rules (the downloads were

DATA (all NSE's own)
- Stock options: the F&O bhavcopy (OPTSTK), the two nearest expiries, strikes within +/-12% of the
  CM close (scripts/nse-stock-options-history.js).
- CM bhavcopy (series EQ, by symbol); universe and resets as research/ta-atlas-2026-10-03:
  - the top 200 by 60-session median traded value;
  - at least 250 bars after the last reset (an open >= 18% from the previous close);

- F&O ban list: archives/fo/sec_ban/fo_secban_DDMMYYYY.csv. The file for trade date d is published
  before d opens.
- Industries: the "sm_indusrty" field of NSE's corporate board-meeting filings (the latest value

OPTION COSTS
api/_lib/trade-costs.js roundTripCost .totalCost, quantity = round(100000 / entry premium) (about
Rs 1 lakh of premium). An option return is (exit - entry - costs per unit) / entry: % of premium.
Stock options in India settle by DELIVERY at expiry. Pinning (P1) uses intrinsic value as the cash
equivalent; delivery STT and margin are not modelled. Stated.

MEMBERS (Holm across all 10; PASS = mean > 0 and Newey-West monthly t >= 3 over the whole sample)
OPTION BUYING on real prices. Universe stocks with options; the nearest expiry with >= 8 calendar
days left at entry; ATM = the listed strike nearest the CM close of the entry day; entry and exit at
the option's closing price; a trade with no exit price, or with a price <= 0, is dropped.
  O1  Baseline: buy the ATM call at the close of every 5th session (session index divisible by 5);
      sell 3 sessions later.
  O2  Baseline: the same with the ATM put.
  O3  Cheap volatility: when the ATM straddle's implied vol, (CE + PE) / (0.8 x S x sqrt(days/365)),
      is below 0.8 x the stock's 20-session realised vol, buy the straddle at that close; sell 5
      sessions later. Once per stock per 5 sessions.
  O4  Results straddle: buy the ATM straddle at the close of the session before the results meeting
      session M0; sell at the close of M0 + 1.
EXPIRY PINNING (monthly stock-option expiry E, read at the close of E-1)
  K* = the strike with the largest call + put open interest for expiry E.
  P1  If |S - K*| / S <= 3%: sell the K* straddle at E-1's close; settle at intrinsic value against
      the CM close of E.
  P2  If K* > S by >= 0.5%, long the share's future from open(E) to close(E); if K* < S by >= 0.5%,
      short. Futures charges; scored in EXCESS of B(E,1).
F&O BAN LIST
  N1  The first session a stock is OUT of the ban after being in it: long from open(d) to
      close(d+4); excess.
  N2  The same: short; excess.
INDUSTRY
  I1  Industry momentum (Moskowitz & Grinblatt 1999), at a trading horizon. On each month's first
      session, rank industries with >= 3 universe stocks by the equal-weighted return of their
      universe stocks over the previous 126 sessions. Long the stocks of the top 3 industries,
      short those of the bottom 3; hold 21 sessions. The score is the long-short spread per
      month-start (hedged).
  I2  Industry-adjusted short-term reversal (Da, Liu & Schaumburg 2014). Every 5th session: a
      stock's 5-session return minus its industry's equal-weighted 5-session return. Long the
      bottom 10%, short the top 10%; hold 5 sessions. The score is the long-short spread (hedged).
Futures charges both legs on I1/I2/N/P2, dated, quantity ceil(500000/price), with a roll per month
end crossed.

VERDICT AND WHAT IT DOES (protocol v2)
- PASS (t >= 3) becomes a forward paper test on TRACK before any page rule.
- PROMISING (2 <= t < 3) may become one.
- The Deflated Sharpe probability (N = 10) is reported.
- O1/O2 are baselines: they measure what the owner's option buying costs on stock options, whatever
  their verdict.

PRE-REGISTRATION -- THREE PUBLISHED STRATEGIES ON THE K2 F&O PANEL
Written 2026-09-27, BEFORE any result of these tests was computed.
Its sha256 is recorded before the study runs; nothing below may change after.

WHY
The user asked to test the best-known published strategies on their instruments
(long stock futures, bought calls). Published anomalies lose 26% of their return
out of sample and 58% after publication (McLean & Pontiff 2016, 97 anomalies), and
with many strategies tried a t of 2 is not enough (Harvey, Liu & Zhu 2016: t > 3).
So: a short list, rules fixed here, one run, a multiple-testing correction.
Already tested on this panel and excluded (retesting is searching): 12-1
cross-sectional momentum (FAIL 2026-09-25), short-term reversal, relative strength,
distance from the 52-week high (sign flips, PANEL_STUDY_ROUND3).

DATA
.cache/panel-bars: 97 NSE F&O large caps, daily bars 2021-09-15 .. 2026-09-15.
Month-end close = last daily close in each calendar month (IST date). Only complete
months; the partial month holding 2026-09-15 is excluded. Formation month-end F,
holding month H = F+1, return = close(H) / close(F) - 1.
Benchmark = equal-weight mean return in month H of all eligible stocks.
Cost = 0.20% per holding month per position change (as in the momentum study).

THREE HYPOTHESES, EACH ONE RULE, NO VARIANTS
T1  TIME-SERIES MOMENTUM (Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012, applied per stock, long
    only). Eligible: closes at F-12, F-1, F, H. LONG = every eligible stock with
    close(F-1)/close(F-12) - 1 > 0, equal weight. Months with fewer than 10 such
    stocks are skipped. Excess = LONG - benchmark - 0.20%.
T2  LOW VOLATILITY (Frazzini & Pedersen 2014 "Betting against beta"; the low-risk
    anomaly). Eligible: at least 60 daily closes in the 3 calendar months ending at
    F, and a close at H. Volatility = standard deviation of daily log returns over
    those months. LONG = the 10 lowest-volatility stocks, equal weight.
    Excess = LONG - benchmark - 0.20%.
T3  MARKET TREND FILTER (Faber 2007; time-series momentum on the market). Market
    index = cumulative equal-weight benchmark return. Signal ON at F when the index
    at F is above the mean of its last 10 month-end values (F-9 .. F). Strategy
    return in H = benchmark if ON, 0 (cash) if OFF; minus 0.20% in any month the
    signal changed from the month before. Excess = strategy - benchmark.

WINDOWS (by the last trading day of the holding month; the protocol calendar)
- development: before 2024-09-19
- validation:  2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17
- final test:  2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15

DECISION RULE, FOR EACH OF T1, T2, T3
PASS = final-test mean monthly excess > 0
       AND final-test t >= the one-sided Student-t critical value at alpha = 0.05/3
           (Bonferroni over three tests) with the final window's n-1 degrees of
           freedom, computed numerically in study.js
       AND validation mean monthly excess > 0.
FAIL = anything else.

WHAT A PASS MEANS, STATED IN ADVANCE
- A PASS goes to PAPER tracking on the TRACK tab only, never straight to live
  suggestions. About 12 months per window: power is low, so a FAIL is weak
  evidence, not proof; a PASS needs a large effect.
- The panel is today's large caps (survivorship); the benchmark is the same
  panel, so the bias largely cancels in the excess.
- Options are not backtested (no five-year option price history). A T1 or T2 PASS
  would decide which names get calls; T3 whether calls are shown in a down market.

RESULTS-DAY DRIFT (Chan, Jegadeesh & Lakonishok 1996; Brandt, Kishore, Santa-Clara & Venkatachalam 2008)
For a results event of a universe stock with meeting date M:
- M0 = the first session on or after M.
- REACTION = close(M0 + 1) / close(session before M0) - 1. This covers results released during
  the meeting day or after hours.
- The cut-offs are the 20th and 80th percentiles of REACTION over DEVELOPMENT-window events only,
  then frozen.
  R1  REACTION >= the 80th percentile -> long from open(M0 + 2) to close(M0 + 11), 10 sessions.
  R2  REACTION <= the 20th percentile -> short over the same days.
Window by the entry session. A trade crossing a reset is dropped. Futures charges, both legs, dated.

OPTIONS VOLATILITY PREMIUM (Carr & Wu 2009) -- NIFTY weekly options, held to expiry
The cycle and strike choice are F11 of research/families-2026-10-04:
- the first session of each weekly-expiry cycle with 4..10 calendar days left;
- the strike nearest NIFTY's close; closing prices; settled at intrinsic value on NIFTY's close
  on the expiry date.
Return in % of NIFTY's close, after charges.
  V1  Conditional short straddle. Sell only when the straddle's implied volatility exceeds 1.2x
      NIFTY's 20-session realised volatility. Straddle IV = (CE + PE) / (0.8 x S x sqrt(days/365));
      realised = the annualised population SD of the last 20 daily log returns up to that session.
  V2  Short strangle. Sell the call at the strike nearest S x 1.02 and the put nearest S x 0.98.
  V3  Long straddle, the BUYER's side of F11: buy CE + PE at the ATM strike, hold to expiry.
      Included because the owner buys options. Its PASS would mean buying volatility paid.

FAMILY: R1, R2, V1, V2, V3. Holm across all 5.
PASS = validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final one-sided p (Student-t, months - 1 df,
clustered by calendar month) survives Holm at 5%. Fewer than 30 final trades = NOT TESTABLE.
Worst single trade reported for every member.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES
A PASS goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first. V1/V2 are option SELLING (margin, crash risk);
even a PASS would be information for the page, not a row for the owner's Zerodha buying.

CORPORATE ACTIONS (splits, bonuses, other price resets)
On a session t where a stock's prevClose differs from its own close on its previous session by more
than 0.5%, factor(t) = prevClose(t) / close(t-1) marks a price reset. Signals are computed on prices
back-adjusted by the product of later factors. Any trade whose holding window (entry session to exit
session, both included) contains a reset day for that stock is DROPPED (counted and reported). If
prevClose turns out NOT to carry the reset (checked on RELIANCE 28 Oct 2024 1:1 bonus, TATASTEEL
28 Jul 2022 1:10 split, IRCTC 28 Oct 2021 1:5 split), the study stops before any signal is computed.
Ordinary dividends are not adjusted: a share falls by its dividend and a future does not. Stated, not
corrected.

UNIVERSE (point in time)

value over the previous 60 sessions, among stocks with at least 66 sessions of history. Held for that
month. This stands in for the F&O list. Delisted names stay in, so there is no survivorship filter.

THE SIGNALS (for entry session d, using completed sessions up to and including d-1 only)
- NIFTY regime: api/_lib/market-regime.js classifyMarketRegime on every NIFTY bar up to d-1.
  NIFTY_DOWN = regime "BEAR_HOSTILE" (close < EMA20 and EMA20 < SMA50).
- Stock regime: the same function on the stock's last 66 back-adjusted bars up to d-1. That is what
  the live desk feeds it: about three months of bars, so the 200-day test is not applied.
  RISING = "BULL_TRENDING" (close >= EMA20 >= SMA50); FALLING = "BEAR_HOSTILE".
- The live rule also excludes stocks the desk rates SELL or REDUCE. That rating cannot be replayed
  historically, so it is NOT applied here. Stated.

H3 (normal market)  NIFTY not NIFTY_DOWN and the stock RISING        -> LONG
Every universe stock on every session where its member's condition holds is one trade (as on the
desk's TRACK, where each session's frozen level is a new paper trade; trades may overlap).

TRADE GEOMETRY (the desk's futures rules; api/_lib/session-levels.js, scripts/paper-ledger.js)
LONG: buy = open(d) x 0.992 rounded DOWN to 0.05. Filled on d only if low(d) < buy, otherwise it is not
a trade. target = buy x 1.025, stop = buy x 0.985, each rounded to the nearest 0.05.
- Fill day: low(d) <= stop -> exit at the stop. A target touched on the fill day is not counted.
- Each later session s, up to the 20th session counting d as the first:
  open(s) <= stop or open(s) >= target -> exit at open(s);
  low(s) <= stop -> exit at the stop (the stop is first if both are touched);
  high(s) >= target -> exit at the target.
- Otherwise exit at the close of the 20th session. A trade still open at the end of the data exits at
  the last close.
SHORT (the exact mirror): sell = open(d) x 1.008 rounded UP to 0.05. Filled only if high(d) > sell.
target = sell x 0.975, stop = sell x 1.015, nearest 0.05. Fill day: high(d) >= stop -> stop.
Later: open >= stop or open <= target -> exit at open; high >= stop -> stop; low <= target -> target;
else the 20th close. A short is a stand-in for a put, and does not price the option.
Prices are the stock's actual (unadjusted) prices.

COSTS
api/_lib/shadow-broker.js calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs, dated (asOfDate = the leg's
session), quantity = ceil(500000 / entry price). Net return = signed price return - charges /
(entry x quantity). A trade that crosses a month end pays one extra round trip at its exit price as
a futures roll (an approximation, stated).

WINDOWS (by entry session)
development: before 2024-09-19 | validation: 2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17 | final: 2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15

STATISTICS
Unit: the trade. Mean net return in %, errors clustered by calendar month of entry
(api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean). One-sided p from Student-t with (months - 1) degrees of freedom.
PASS (each member) = validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final p survives Holm-Bonferroni
across the three members at 5% family-wise (smallest p against 0.05/3, the next against 0.05/2, the
last against 0.05). Fewer than 30 final trades = NOT TESTABLE.
Also reported, with no verdict: share of winners, trade counts, and trades dropped for resets.

"GAP(d)" = open(d) / close(d-1) - 1 on NIFTY; the regime and candle of d are read from bars up
to and including d-1 (classifyMarketRegime; identifyCandlestickPatterns on the last 7 bars).

COSTS
Every trade pays api/_lib/shadow-broker.js calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs, dated,
quantity = ceil(500000 / entry), as the regime study did. Returns are NET, in % of entry.

PART A -- DIRECTION HYPOTHESES (each a trade on NIFTY or a stock; signed so a correct call is +)
A1 US OVERNIGHT. US(d) <= -1%: short NIFTY at open(d), out at close(d). US(d) >= +1%: long.
A2 CRUDE SPIKE. CRUDE(d) >= +3%: short NIFTY open(d)->close(d). CRUDE(d) <= -3%: long.
A3 GAP CONTINUATION. GAP(d) <= -1%: short NIFTY open(d)->close(d). GAP(d) >= +1%: long.
A4 VIX SPIKE. India VIX up >= 10% on the session before d: short NIFTY open(d) -> close(d+4).
A5 NIFTY CANDLE. The desk's candlestick bias of NIFTY's bar d-1 (identifyCandlestickPatterns):
   BULLISH -> long NIFTY open(d) -> close(d+4); BEARISH -> short; NEUTRAL or none -> no trade.
P1 STOCK CANDLE. The same, per stock of the panel, on each stock's own bars: BULLISH -> long
   the stock open(d) -> close(d+4), BEARISH -> short. Errors clustered by CALENDAR MONTH.
Unit: one trade per signal-day (per stock-day for P1); errors clustered by calendar month
(api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean), because consecutive days share a market.
PASS (each): validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final one-sided p-value passes
Holm-Bonferroni across the SIX hypotheses at a family-wise 5% (the smallest p against 0.05/6,
the next against 0.05/5, ...; stop at the first that fails). p from Student-t with
(final-window months - 1) degrees of freedom. Fewer than 20 final-window trades: NOT TESTABLE.

PART B -- DESCRIPTIVE: EVERY DESK RULE, BY SITUATION (no verdict, no PASS, counts shown)

PRE-REGISTRATION -- NIFTY GAMMA EXPOSURE (GEX), six members, scored under PROTOCOL v2
Written Sunday 4 Oct 2026, BEFORE any gamma-exposure number has been computed on real data. The commit
time and the sha256 in PREREGISTRATION.sha256 are the proof; study.js refuses to run if this file changes.
Owner, 4 Oct: "go start the gamma exposure test".

IDEA (what is being tested)
Option dealers who are SHORT gamma hedge by trading WITH the move (buy rises, sell falls), which can
make moves larger and continue; dealers LONG gamma hedge AGAINST the move, which can damp it. Primary
source: Baltussen, Da, Lammers & Martens, "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum", Journal of
Financial Economics 142(1), 2021, 377-403. Their effect is INTRADAY (the last 30 minutes); this study
uses NSE's end-of-day files only, so it tests the DAILY version of the idea, not their result.
The open question for India is the SIGN: who is short the gamma. Two answers are tested side by side.

DATA (NSE's own end-of-day files only; release data-research)
- NIFTY options: F&O bhavcopy OPTIDX NIFTY, the two nearest expiries listed that day, every strike:
  close and open interest (scripts/nse-options-delivery-history.js -> opt/<date>.json, idx.NIFTY rows
  [expiry, strike, CE/PE, close, settle, OI]).
- NIFTY futures: F&O bhavcopy FUTIDX NIFTY open and close (scripts/nse-fo-history.js -> fut/<date>.json).
- Who holds what: NSE's participant-wise open interest, fao_participant_oi_DDMMYYYY.csv (part/<date>.csv),
  columns "Option Index Call Long / Put Long / Call Short / Put Short" for Client, DII, FII, Pro, TOTAL.
Sessions: the dates with all three files, 2020-09-01 .. 2026-10-01.

GAMMA EXPOSURE ON DAY d (known only after day d's close)
- F = close of the NIFTY futures contract with the nearest expiry AFTER d.
- For each option expiry E listed in day d's file with E > d: T = calendar days(d, E) / 365. IV of
  that expiry from its at-the-money straddle: K* = the strike nearest F with CE and PE close > 0;
  sigma = (CE + PE) / (0.8 x F x sqrt(T)).
- Gamma of each strike K (Black-76, no discounting): d1 = (ln(F/K) + sigma^2 T / 2) / (sigma sqrt(T)),
  gamma = phi(d1) / (F sigma sqrt(T)). Exposure per 1% move = gamma x OI x F^2 x 0.01.
- G1 "US convention": dealers are long the calls and short the puts:
  GEX = sum over strikes (call exposure) - sum (put exposure).
- G2 "NSE's own split": the hedgers are everyone except Clients (FII + DII + Pro), whose net position is
  minus the Clients' net position in NSE's file. fc = -(Client Call Long - Client Call Short) / TOTAL Call
  Long; fp = -(Client Put Long - Client Put Short) / TOTAL Put Long (all index options, as NSE publishes
  them; assumed to hold strike by strike for NIFTY). GEX = fc x sum (call exposure) + fp x sum (put exposure).
- "Short gamma" = GEX < 0; "long gamma" = GEX > 0. No threshold is fitted: the sign only.

THE SIX MEMBERS (g = G1, G2). Signal from day d's files; trades start the NEXT session (d+1).
  M1-g  momentum when short gamma: if GEX_g(d) < 0, take the side of day d's NIFTY futures move
        (sign of close(d)/close(d-1) - 1, same contract), from the OPEN to the CLOSE of d+1.
  M2-g  reversal when long gamma: if GEX_g(d) > 0, take the side AGAINST day d's move, open to close of d+1.
  V-g   buy the straddle when short gamma: if GEX_g(d) < 0, buy the NIFTY at-the-money straddle (nearest
        listed expiry E > d+2, strike nearest the futures close of d+1, both legs with close > 0) at the
        close of d+1 and sell it at the close of d+2 (a leg with no close on d+2: NSE's settlement price
        for it that day; neither: no trade).
Futures contract for M1/M2: the nearest NIFTY expiry after d+1, the same contract on d-1, d and d+1 (else
no trade). A zero move on day d: no trade.
Charges: futures -- api/_lib/shadow-broker calculateNseFuturesCharges, dated, quantity ceil(500000/price),
buy and sell. Options -- api/_lib/trade-costs roundTripCost(...).totalCost per leg, quantity
round(100000 / leg premium). Futures net = side x (close/open - 1) - charges/notional. Straddle net =
(exit CE + exit PE - entry CE - entry PE - charges per unit) / (entry CE + entry PE).

SCORING: PROTOCOL v2 (studies/audit-2026-10-04/PROTOCOL.txt), unchanged
- The whole sample; one value per calendar month = the mean net of that month's trades; Newey-West(3) t
  on the monthly series. At least 24 months, else NOT TESTABLE.
- PASS: mean > 0 and t >= 3. PROMISING: mean > 0 and 2 <= t < 3. Otherwise FAIL.
- Reported beside it: Holm across the six members (one-sided 5%), the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey &
  Lopez de Prado; N = 6), the number of trades and months, and the share of days each sign convention
  calls short gamma.
- Diagnostics only (not members, no verdict): the same trades taken on EVERY day regardless of GEX.

TIMING (one rule for every member; no look-ahead)
NSE posts many of these files late (the short-selling file dated D carries D-1's trades; category turnover
and margin files arrive a day or two late). So a signal from data of trading day d is acted on at the OPEN
of the SECOND session after d (call it e = d+2), never earlier. Insider filings: d = the IST calendar date
of NSE's broadcast time ("date" field); e = the first session strictly after d.

PRICES AND CHARGES
- NIFTY members trade NIFTY futures, priced on NSE's NIFTY 50 index open/close (ind_close_all), with
  futures charges (api/_lib/shadow-broker calculateNseFuturesCharges, dated, quantity ceil(500000/price),
  both sides) and one extra round trip per month end crossed (the roll). Net = side x (close/open - 1) - charges.
- Stock members trade stock futures, priced on NSE's CM bhavcopy (EQ series) open/close, same charges,
  universe = each month's top 200 EQ stocks by 60-session median traded value with >= 250 bars after the
  last reset (an open >= 18% from the previous close); a window crossing a reset is skipped
  (api/_lib/forward-v3.js context(), identical to the earlier studies). Scored IN EXCESS of B(e,h) = the
  equal-weighted gross return of the universe stocks from open(e) to close(e+h-1) (side-signed).
- Hold h sessions: exit at the close of the h-th session counted from e (e itself is the first).
- At most one open trade per member per name: a new signal while a trade is open is ignored.

SIGNAL VARIABLES. z = (x - mean) / SD of the same variable over the previous 250 available observations
(at least 120, else no signal).

NIFTY MEMBERS (h = 5 unless stated; position +1 = long, -1 = short)

    (fao_participant_vol). z >= +1 -> long; z <= -1 -> short.

 N3 Client index futures flow, contrarian: as N1 for "Client", with the side reversed.
 N4 FII index options flow: x = (Call Long - Call Short) - (Put Long - Put Short) contracts traded by FII.

THE FEE RATE (one number per stock per file day d)
For each series row of stock s on d with quantity > 0 and at least 7 calendar days from d to the series'

The stock's rate on d = the quantity-weighted mean of its rows' rates. Eligible on d if: total quantity x P
>= Rs 10 lakh, s has stock options in NSE's F&O file on d (F&O membership), and s is in that month's
universe (below).

 M1 TimesFM 2.5, 200M (google/timesfm-2.5-200m-pytorch, Apache-2.0; NOT 3.0, whose weights are non-commercial).
    Close prices only; median of its quantile head; normalize_inputs, flip invariance, positive inputs.

    forward: at rebalance index i it is trained only on rebalances with index <= i - 10 (history from Sep 2021).

CONTAMINATION, AND THE WINDOW
Kronos' paper (arXiv 2508.02739) says its pre-training data "extends up to June 2024" and lists India (XNSE) among
its in-distribution exchanges, so it has very likely seen NSE prices before July 2024. TimesFM 2.5's card lists
GiftEvalPretrain (no dates), Wikipedia to Nov 2023, Google Trends to end-2022 and synthetic data; Chronos-2's page
states no training data. So every member is scored ONLY on entries from 2024-07-01 on. Even that cannot rule out
that a model saw these stocks; the forward paper test after a PASS is the real test.

  divisible by 10), e >= 2024-07-01, with 5 sessions of data after it. d = the session before e.

  api/_lib/forward-v3.js context(): top 200 EQ with >= 250 bars after the last reset, an open >= 18% from the

  window from e. A rebalance needs >= 20 names with a forecast.

  stock-futures charges (calculateNseFuturesCharges, dated, quantity ceil(500000/price), both sides, + a round
  trip per month end crossed). Value = (mean long net + mean short net) / 2, one per rebalance; not in excess of
