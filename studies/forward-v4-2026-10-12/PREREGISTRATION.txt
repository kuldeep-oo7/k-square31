THE RULE (copied word for word from the atlas registration; the code is copied from its study.js)
 S2 Borrowing surge, short, h = 5: x = SLB outstanding quantity on d (slb_openpos, summed over series) /
    its average over the previous 20 available days (>= 10). x >= 2 and outstanding >= 100,000 shares -> short.
With the atlas study's common rules, unchanged:
- TIMING: a signal from the file of trading day d is acted on at the OPEN of the SECOND session after d
  (e = d+2), never earlier.
- Hold h = 5 sessions: exit at the close of the 5th session counted from e (e itself is the first).
- At most one open trade per name: a new signal while a trade is open is ignored.
- Prices: NSE CM bhavcopy (EQ series) open/close. Universe = each month's top 200 EQ stocks by 60-session
  median traded value with >= 250 bars after the last reset (an open >= 18% from the previous close); a
  window crossing a reset is skipped (api/_lib/forward-v3.js context()).
- Charges: stock futures, calculateNseFuturesCharges (dated, quantity ceil(500000/price), both sides) plus
  one extra round trip per month end crossed (the roll).
- Score: net IN EXCESS of B(e,5) = the equal-weighted gross return of the universe stocks from open(e) to
  close(e+4) (side-signed); B needs >= 20 stocks, else no trade.
- Data: NSE's own slb_openpos files (nsearchives .../archives/slbs/open_pos/slb_openpos_DDMMYYYY.csv), as the
  atlas downloader saves them. Only the SLB history from 2026-08-01 is needed for the 20-day averages.

WHAT IS COUNTED
- Every S2 trade whose ENTRY session e >= 2026-10-13. A signal from a file dated before 13 Oct can enter on
  or after 13 Oct; its entry and exit prices are all after this commit, so it counts.
- Official NSE files only.

A LIMIT STATED BEFORE COMMIT (not a rule change)
The universe is the top 200 stocks by traded value, as in the backtest. Some of them may have no NSE stock
futures. The page marks each signal "has NSE futures" or "no NSE futures (cash short is intraday only in
India)", from the latest NSE F&O futures file. Every signal is counted either way, as in the backtest.

VERDICT (judged at 2.5% one-sided, as forward-v3's CHEAPVOL)
COLLECTING until 100 finished trades and 3 months from 13 Oct 2026. Then PASS if the mean excess > 0 AND
the one-sided t (clustered by ISO week of entry) is at or above the 2.5% Student-t critical value with
(clusters - 1) df. Otherwise FAIL. Nothing may change after this commit.
