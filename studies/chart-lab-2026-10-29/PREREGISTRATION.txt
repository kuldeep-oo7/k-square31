2. DATA AND DESIGN
- Discovery: NSE 2005-01-01 .. 2020-10-31 (release data-oos: nse-old cm, fut, idx). Confirmation: 2020-10-01 .. 2026-10-01
  (research snapshot cm and nse-fo fut; data-oos before 2020-09-01 as look-back only; NIFTY from data-oos idx and
  data-macro ind_close_all). Exactly the futures lab's loaders (studies/futures-lab-2026-10-23/run.js).
- Bars: the SHARE's daily NSE bars (EQ series, unadjusted). A session whose open is 18% or more from the previous close is
  a reset (corporate action); a signal needs at least 260 bars since the last reset.
- Universe: the futures lab's (each month, the top 200 by the median traded value of the previous 60 bars, at least 250
  bars since the last reset), and a near-month future with an open and a close on the entry session.
- Signal on day k uses only bars to the close of k. Entry at the OPEN of the next session (i) on the near-month FUTURE.
- One open trade per stock per member: a signal while that member holds that stock is skipped.

3. INDICATORS (share bars, through day k)
- SMA n = mean of the last n closes. EMA20: alpha 2/21, seeded with the first close. ATR14, +DI, -DI and ADX14: Wilder.
- HI250 / LO250 = highest high / lowest low of the last 250 bars. VOL50 = mean volume of the 50 bars before k.
- RS = percentile (0-100) of the 126-bar close-to-close return among that month's universe on day k (ties averaged).
- NIFTY (index close): UP when SMA10 > SMA20 and SMA20 above its value 5 sessions earlier; DOWN when SMA10 < SMA20 and
  SMA20 below its value 5 sessions earlier; otherwise neither.

4. MEMBERS (7 ideas x 2 directions = 14). Conditions are on day k unless stated.
 A1 LEADER BREAKOUT (long; Minervini trend template + tight base + volume, O'Neil/Kullamagi):
    C > SMA50 > SMA150 > SMA200; SMA200 > its value 21 bars earlier; C >= 1.30 x LO250; C >= 0.75 x HI250; RS >= 70;
    base: (highest high - lowest low of bars k-20..k-1) <= 0.15 x C(k-1); C > highest high of k-20..k-1;
    volume >= 1.5 x VOL50; NIFTY UP. Exit TRAIL.
 A2 LAGGARD BREAKDOWN (short; the mirror): C < SMA50 < SMA150 < SMA200; SMA200 < its value 21 bars earlier;
    C <= 0.70 x HI250; C <= 1.25 x LO250; RS <= 30; the same base; C < lowest low of k-20..k-1; volume >= 1.5 x VOL50;
    NIFTY DOWN. Exit TRAIL.
 B1 GAP-AND-HOLD UP (long; episodic pivot, daily): gap g = O(k)/C(k-1) - 1 with 0.04 <= g < 0.18; volume >= 3 x VOL50;
    C >= O; H > L and (C - L)/(H - L) >= 0.5; NIFTY UP. Exit TRAIL.
 B2 GAP-AND-HOLD DOWN (short): -0.18 < g <= -0.04; volume >= 3 x VOL50; C <= O; (H - C)/(H - L) >= 0.5; NIFTY DOWN.
    Exit TRAIL.
 C1 CAPITULATION BOUNCE (long): C(k-1)/C(k-11) - 1 <= -0.20; at least 4 of the 5 bars k-5..k-1 closed below their previous
    close; k is the first green bar: C > O and C > C(k-1). Exit FIX5. No NIFTY filter.
 C2 PARABOLIC FADE (short; Kullamagi): C(k-1)/C(k-11) - 1 >= +0.20; at least 4 of the 5 bars k-5..k-1 closed above their
    previous close; k is the first red bar: C < O and C < C(k-1). Exit FIX5. No NIFTY filter.
 D1 HOLY GRAIL UP (long; Raschke): on k-1: ADX14 >= 30, +DI > -DI and L(k-1) <= EMA20(k-1); on k: C > H(k-1). Exit TRAIL.
 D2 HOLY GRAIL DOWN (short): on k-1: ADX14 >= 30, -DI > +DI and H(k-1) >= EMA20(k-1); on k: C < L(k-1). Exit TRAIL.
 E1 TURTLE SOUP PLUS ONE, LONG (Raschke and Connors): PL = lowest low of bars k-21..k-2, last reached at least 4 bars before
    k-1; on k-1: L < PL and C <= PL; on k: C > PL. Exit FIX5.
 E2 TURTLE SOUP PLUS ONE, SHORT: PH = highest high of k-21..k-2, last reached at least 4 bars before k-1; on k-1: H > PH and
    C >= PH; on k: C < PH. Exit FIX5.
 F1 BULLISH CANDLE AT THE 50-DAY, UPTREND (Nison's location rule): SMA50 > SMA200; SMA50 > its value 10 bars earlier;
    L <= SMA50 + 0.5 x ATR14 and C >= SMA50 - 0.5 x ATR14; and a bullish engulfing (C(k-1) < O(k-1), C > O,
    O <= C(k-1), C >= O(k-1), body larger than the previous body) or a hammer (R = H - L > 0; body |C - O| <= 0.35R;
    lower shadow >= 2 x body and >= 0.5R; upper shadow <= 0.15R). Exit FIX5.
 F2 BEARISH CANDLE AT THE 50-DAY, DOWNTREND: SMA50 < SMA200; SMA50 < its value 10 bars earlier; H >= SMA50 - 0.5 x ATR14 and
    C <= SMA50 + 0.5 x ATR14; and a bearish engulfing (the mirror) or a shooting star (body <= 0.35R; upper shadow >= 2 x
    body and >= 0.5R; lower shadow <= 0.15R). Exit FIX5.
 G1 TIGHT INSIDE DAY, UPTREND (Crabel IDnr4 + trend): H <= H(k-1) and L >= L(k-1); H - L smaller than each of the 3 bars
    before; C > SMA50 > SMA200; NIFTY UP. Exit TRAIL.
 G2 TIGHT INSIDE DAY, DOWNTREND: the same inside and NR4 bar; C < SMA50 < SMA200; NIFTY DOWN. Exit TRAIL.
 Exits (sessions counted on the market calendar from the entry session i):
 - TRAIL: the first session t >= i on which the share closes below its SMA10 (long) or above it (short); exit at the close
   of the session after t; at the latest at the close of session i+19 (20 sessions, GM Global's longest hold).
 - FIX5: exit at the close of session i+4.

5. PRICE, VALUE, SCORE, VERDICT
- Price: the futures lab's futHold: the near-month future from its official open on session i to its official close
  (settlement) on the exit session, rolling at expiry at that day's close into the next contract; dated NSE futures charges
  on every leg, quantity ceil(Rs 5 lakh / entry price). A trade is DROPPED (not counted) when a price is missing, when the
  held contract moves more than -18% / +22% from one close to the next (corporate action or bad print), or when the share
  has a reset during the hold.
- Market: B = (1 + b_open(i)) x product over t = i+1..exit of (1 + b_cc(t)) - 1, where b_open(i) = mean open-to-close of the
  universe's near-month futures on i and b_cc(t) = mean close-to-close of each universe stock's held near-month contract
  (rolled as above, same -18%/+22% guard); each day needs at least 30 names, else the trade is dropped.
- VALUE of a trade = FUTURES EXCESS = net - side x B (side +1 long, -1 short).
- Member score: monthly mean (%) of its trades' values by entry month; Newey-West t (lag 3). Fewer than 24 months: NOT
  TESTABLE. Holm across the testable members at 5% (one-sided). Full Deflated Sharpe (Bailey and Lopez de Prado 2014, with
  skew and kurtosis) with N = 430 trials (393 counted through the VRP lab, plus the expiry legs 10, the order book 6, the
  GM panel's exploratory tests rounded up to 7, plus these 14), as the earlier labs.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm and DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2.
  PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2 without a PASS. FAIL otherwise. A confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward
  paper test first, never straight to the page.
- Reported, not judged: trades, months, win rate, mean NET % and mean EXCESS % a trade, mean hold, worst month, in both
  periods; the discovery halves 2005-2012 and 2013-2020; long and short of each idea side by side.
- Expectation, stated now: most members FAIL. The literature and the 3 Oct atlas say short-horizon chart rules on single
  stocks keep little after costs. The likeliest to show anything: C1 (close to the long-unwinding bounce) and B1/B2 (close
  to drift after big news gaps).
