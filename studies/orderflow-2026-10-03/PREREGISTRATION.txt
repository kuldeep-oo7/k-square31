PRE-REGISTRATION -- ORDER FLOW / OPEN INTEREST / FII POSITIONING
Written 3 Oct 2026 BEFORE any signal or result was computed on the real data. Only the file formats
were looked at, to write the parser. sha256 in PREREGISTRATION.sha256, checked by study.js.

WHY
The owner: "think like market makers, FIIs, DIIs, banks, hedge funds ... many people are saying
order flow". The public footprint of that flow on NSE is open interest by contract and by
participant. The practitioner reading of price + OI is: long build-up, short build-up, short
covering, long unwinding. FII index-futures positioning is widely watched.

WHAT THIS DATA HAS ALREADY BEEN USED FOR
The stock prices were used by about 12 earlier studies. Open interest and participant data have
never been used on this desk. The windows are the same, and the final window is not a clean
holdout for prices.

DATA (all NSE's own files, checked on NSE 3 Oct 2026)
- F&O bhavcopy, futures rows only: old format content/historical/DERIVATIVES (to 2024-07-05), new
  UDiFF content/fo (from 2024-07-08). Per stock: total open interest across all its futures expiries,
  and its change. Summing every expiry makes the monthly roll neutral.
- Stock prices: the CM bhavcopy as in research/rising-stocks-2026-10-02 (series EQ, by symbol), with
  the same reset rule (an open >= 18% from the previous close).
- Participant OI: content/nsccl/fao_participant_oi_DDMMYYYY.csv, FII row, "Future Index Long" and
  "Future Index Short" (contracts).
- NIFTY 50: nifty-50-nse.json (NSE's own index history).

SIGNALS (read from session d-1 against d-2; trade from the open of d)
Per stock that has futures on d-1 and d-2 and is in that month's universe (top 200 by median traded
value over the previous 60 sessions, as in the earlier studies):
  R  = close(d-1) / close(d-2) - 1  (the share, CM bhavcopy; no reset on d-1 or d-2)
  OI = totalOI(d-1) / totalOI(d-2) - 1
  A day "moves" when |R| >= the 67th percentile of |R|, AND |OI| >= the 67th percentile of |OI|. Both
  cut-offs are fixed on DEVELOPMENT-window stock-days only, and then frozen.
  On a moving day:
    R > 0, OI > 0: LONG BUILD-UP  -> long
    R < 0, OI > 0: SHORT BUILD-UP -> short
    R > 0, OI < 0: SHORT COVERING -> long
    R < 0, OI < 0: LONG UNWINDING -> short
FII (market level): F = FII Future Index Long - FII Future Index Short, and dF = F(d-1) - F(d-2).
  dF in the top tercile of development-window dF -> long NIFTY; in the bottom tercile -> short NIFTY.

TRADES
Stocks: enter at open(d); exit at close(d) for horizon 1, or at close(d+9) for horizon 10.
NIFTY: enter at NIFTY open(d); exit at close(d) for horizon 1, or at close(d+4) for horizon 5. This
is the index as a stand-in for the NIFTY future.
Costs: api/_lib/shadow-broker.js calculateNseFuturesCharges, both legs, dated, quantity =
ceil(500000/price), plus one roll round trip per month end crossed. Net %.
A stock trade whose window contains a reset is dropped.

MEMBERS (Holm across all 12)
  B1  long build-up -> long, 1 session        B2  long build-up -> long, 10 sessions
  B3  short build-up -> short, 1 session      B4  short build-up -> short, 10 sessions
  B5  short covering -> long, 1 session       B6  short covering -> long, 10 sessions
  B7  long unwinding -> short, 1 session      B8  long unwinding -> short, 10 sessions
  C1  FII adding (top tercile) -> long NIFTY, 1 session    C2  the same, 5 sessions
  C3  FII cutting (bottom tercile) -> short NIFTY, 1 session C4  the same, 5 sessions

WINDOWS
development: before 2024-09-19 | validation: 2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17 | final: 2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15

STATISTICS
The unit is the trade, clustered by calendar month of entry (api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean).
p is one-sided Student-t with (months - 1) df.
PASS = validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final p survives Holm across all 12 at 5%.
Fewer than 30 final trades = NOT TESTABLE.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES
A PASS goes to the page only as "passed on re-used price data; paper first", on TRACK, after the
owner sees the numbers. A FAIL is reported as a FAIL.

CODE CHECKS FIRST (synthetic only): a planted OI -> return link must be found; noise must not pass;
changing future prices must leave earlier trades identical.
