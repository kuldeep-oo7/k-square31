2. DATA AND PRICES
- NSE's F&O bhavcopy, NIFTY index futures rows: discovery 2005-01 .. 2020-10 (release data-oos), confirmation 2020-10 ..
  2026-10-01 (the research snapshot). They share October 2020, as the night lab.
- On day d the near-month contract is the earliest NIFTY futures expiry on or after d with an official open and close above
  zero. Prices: its official open and its official close (NSE's settlement price). A hold over several sessions keeps the
  contract and, on its expiry day, rolls at that day's close into the next contract (one roll round trip in charges).
- Charges: calculateNseFuturesCharges (dated) on both legs, quantity ceil(Rs 5 lakh / entry). Net = signed return - charges.

3. MEMBERS (6)
 N1 OVERNIGHT: every session d: buy the near-month contract of d at its close on the previous session, sell at its open on d.
 N2 INTRADAY: every session d: buy the near-month contract at its open, sell at its close.
 N3 TURN OF THE MONTH: buy at the close of each month's second-to-last session, sell at the close of the next month's third
    session.
 N4 EXPIRY RUN-UP: for each NIFTY futures expiry E: buy that contract at the close of the 4th session before E, sell at its
    close on E.
 N5 GAP FADE: on a session where the near-month contract opens at least 0.5% above its previous close, short at the open and
    cover at the close; at least 0.5% below, buy at the open and sell at the close.
 N6 BIG-DROP BOUNCE: after a session where the near-month contract closed at least 2% below its previous close, buy at the
    next open and sell at the close 5 sessions later.
 (Previous close = the same contract's close on the previous session.)

4. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member: monthly mean (%) of its trades' net returns; Newey-West t (lag 3). Holm across the 6 at 5%. Full Deflated
  Sharpe with N = 365 trials (359 before, these 6), as the night lab computes it.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2. A
  confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2. FAIL otherwise.
  NOT TESTABLE: fewer than 24 months with a trade.
- Reported, not judged: trades, win rate, worst month, mean per trade, and for 2020-2026 GM rupees at 20 units (gross).
- N1 and N2 split each day's buy-and-hold in two; both are reported whatever their sign.
- Expectation, stated now: the most likely outcome is that nothing passes both periods.
