THE MODEL (fixed now; one fit, made after this file is committed)
- Training rows: every short build-up (SB) trade in the futures lab's tables -- discovery rows (NSE 2005-01 .. 2020-09-30,
  release data-oos) and confirmation rows (2020-10-01 .. 2026-10-01, the research snapshot with data-oos look-back) -- with
  the 17 features of studies/open-lab-2026-10-23 (R, OI, GAP, NGAP, NRET, VOLR, TREND, VOL20, BASIS, NSIG, DOW, TOEXP, LOGP,
  LOGV, OIREL, PREV, R2) and the label FUTURES EXCESS > 0, all exactly as the futures lab's run.js builds them.
- Python 3.12, scikit-learn 1.9.1, numpy 2.5.4: HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=200, learning_rate=0.05,
  max_leaf_nodes=15, min_samples_leaf=200, l2_regularization=1.0, early_stopping=False, random_state=0), fitted once on all
  those rows. THRESHOLD = the median of its predicted probabilities on the same rows.
- The fit uses no data after 2026-10-01 and is deterministic. The fitted model is kept in the private repo k2-data
  (it is derived from market data); FIT.txt in this folder records the date of the fit, the number of rows, the threshold
  and the model file's sha256, before any session is scored.

LIVE DECISIONS
- For every SB signal (api/_lib/oi-flow-forward.js, unchanged) entering on d: the 17 features computed as the 09:15 lab's
  features() does, from NSE's files only: CM bars to d-1 and the share's official OPEN of d; F&O files to d-1; NIFTY's open
  of d and closes to d-1 from NSE's index file. Only what is known at 09:15 on d is used; the computation itself runs after
  the close of d.
- TAKE if the model's probability > THRESHOLD, else SKIP.

PRICING (as the futures lab and the strong-SB test's amendment A1)
- Short the near-month future (the earliest expiry on or after d in NSE's F&O file of d) at its official open, cover at its
  official close. NET after calculateNseFuturesCharges (dated; quantity ceil(Rs 5 lakh / open)).
- FUTURES EXCESS = NET + the equal-weighted near-month futures open-to-close return on d of the night lab's universe (top
  200 by traded value, >= 250 bars after the last reset; at least 30 names, else the trade is not counted).
- Counted: every SB signal entering on or after 2026-10-23 with a futures price and the benchmark.

VERDICT (one rule, one test)
COLLECTING until at least 100 counted trades AND 3 calendar months since 2026-10-23 AND 10 ISO weeks with both TAKE and
SKIP trades. Then PASS if the mean of the weekly SPREAD (mean TAKE excess - mean SKIP excess, weeks with both) > 0 and its
one-sided t is at or above the one-sided 5% critical value of Student's t with (weeks - 1) degrees of freedom (the next
LOWER listed degrees of freedom when not listed); otherwise FAIL. A PASS makes it a candidate for FUTURES, never automatic.
Reported, not judged: TAKE's own futures net and excess a trade, win rate, and GM rupees at 20 units (gross, futures) for
TAKE and for all SB trades.
