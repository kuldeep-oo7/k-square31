1. WHAT IS KNOWN (disclosed)
- Earlier tests on this desk that used stock options: buying straddles across results LOSES (-5.6% a trade, t -6.7); the
  cheap-vol straddle was a near miss (t 1.94); put-call ratios FAILED (families study). None of them used implied
  volatilities to predict the STOCK's return. Nothing in this file has been computed on real data.
- Published evidence (each direction read in the paper's own abstract tonight):
  Xing, Zhang and Zhao 2010 (JFQA 45): stocks with the steepest smirk (OTM put IV - ATM call IV) underperform the flattest
    by 10.9% a year.
  Cremers and Weinbaum 2010 (JFQA 45): stocks with relatively expensive calls (call IV - put IV, same strike and expiry)
    outperform those with expensive puts by about 50 bp a week; the effect shrank over their sample.
  An, Ang, Bali and Cakici 2014 (JF 69): large increases in call IV over the past month -> high future returns; large
    increases in put IV -> low future returns.
  Bali and Hovakimian 2009 (Management Science 55): realized minus implied volatility is NEGATIVELY related to future
    returns; the call-put IV spread positively.
  Johnson and So 2012 (JFE 106): the lowest option-to-stock volume decile outperforms the highest by 0.34% a week.
- NSE stock options were American-style for contracts expiring before 27 Jan 2011 and European-style from then (SEBI
  circular CIR/DNPD/6/2010 of 27 Oct 2010; NSE's circular). Implied volatilities below use Black-76 for all years; before
  2011 that ignores the early-exercise value (disclosed, not corrected).

3. IMPLIED VOLATILITY (on each formation day, per stock)
- Expiry: the nearest one at least 7 calendar days after the formation day. F = that expiry's futures close for the stock
  (NSE's F&O file of the same day); none -> no signal. T = calendar days to expiry / 365; r = 6.5%.
- Black-76; the volatility solved by bisection in [0.01, 5]; no value when the price is below the discounted intrinsic value
  or the solution sits on a bound.
- SMIRK = IV of the traded put with K/F in [0.80, 0.95] closest to 0.95, minus IV of the traded call with K/F in [0.95,
  1.05] closest to 1.00 (XZZ, with the future for the share).
- CPS (call-put spread) = average over strikes with both a traded call and a traded put of (call IV - put IV), weighted by
  the pair's mean open interest (CW).
- IVATM = mean of the call and put IV at the strike closest to F with both traded.
- DIV = (call IV at K/F closest to 1, traded) change minus (put IV at K/F closest to 1, traded) change, from the last session
  of the previous month to the formation day (AABC).
- RVIV = standard deviation of the last 21 daily close-to-close log returns x sqrt(252), minus IVATM (B&H).
- OF = the formation week's stock-option contracts in the file / the week's futures contracts, all expiries (adaptation of
  Johnson and So: NSE's futures stand in for the share volume, since both count contracts of the same lot).

4. MEMBERS (8). Deciles among the stocks with the signal that are in the night lab's universe (the top 200 by traded
   value, >= 250 bars after the last reset) for the holding period's first session; at least 50 stocks, else no book.
 WEEKLY (formed at the last session of each ISO week; held from the open of the next week's first session to the close of
 its last session):
  O1 SMIRK, LONG-SHORT: long the flattest tenth (lowest SMIRK), short the steepest. Raw.
  O2 SMIRK, SHORT ONLY: short the steepest tenth. Excess.
  O3 CALL-PUT SPREAD, LONG-SHORT: long the highest CPS tenth, short the lowest. Raw.
  O4 CALL-PUT SPREAD, LONG ONLY: long the highest CPS tenth. Excess.
  O6 OPTION/FUTURES VOLUME, LONG-SHORT: long the lowest OF tenth, short the highest. Raw.
  O8 COMBINED: the mean of the z-scores (across that day's stocks) of -SMIRK and CPS (stocks with both); long the top
     tenth, short the bottom. Raw.
 MONTHLY (formed at the last session of each month; held from the open of the next month's first session to the close of
 its last):
  O5 IV CHANGES, LONG-SHORT: long the highest DIV tenth, short the lowest. Raw.
  O7 REALIZED MINUS IMPLIED, LONG-SHORT: long the LOWEST RVIV tenth, short the highest. Raw.
 Books, costs and excess exactly as the night lab: each leg net of calculateNseFuturesCharges (dated; quantity ceil(Rs 5
 lakh / entry)) plus a roll round trip per month end crossed; a long-short book = (mean long leg + mean short leg) / 2; a
 leg that crosses a reset or misses a session is dropped; excess = the book minus the same-side equal-weighted universe
 return over the same window.

5. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member: its books' net returns -> the monthly mean (%), Newey-West t (lag 3). Holm across the 8 at 5%. Full
  Deflated Sharpe with N = 352 trials (344 before, these 8), as the night lab computes it.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2.
  A confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first, never straight to FUTURES. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0
  and t >= 2. FAIL otherwise. NOT TESTABLE: under 24 months with a book.
- Reported, not judged: every member on 2020-2026; books, stocks a side, win rate, worst month.
- Expectation, stated now: Indian stock options were thinly traded in the early years, so several members may be thin or
  NOT TESTABLE in discovery; the most likely outcome is that nothing passes both periods.
