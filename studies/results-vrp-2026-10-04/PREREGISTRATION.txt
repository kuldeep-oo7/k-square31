RESULTS-DAY DRIFT (Chan, Jegadeesh & Lakonishok 1996; Brandt, Kishore, Santa-Clara & Venkatachalam 2008)
For a results event of a universe stock with meeting date M:
- M0 = the first session on or after M.
- REACTION = close(M0 + 1) / close(session before M0) - 1. This covers results released during
  the meeting day or after hours.
- The cut-offs are the 20th and 80th percentiles of REACTION over DEVELOPMENT-window events only,
  then frozen.
  R1  REACTION >= the 80th percentile -> long from open(M0 + 2) to close(M0 + 11), 10 sessions.
  R2  REACTION <= the 20th percentile -> short over the same days.
Window by the entry session. A trade crossing a reset is dropped. Futures charges, both legs, dated.

OPTIONS VOLATILITY PREMIUM (Carr & Wu 2009) -- NIFTY weekly options, held to expiry
The cycle and strike choice are F11 of research/families-2026-10-04:
- the first session of each weekly-expiry cycle with 4..10 calendar days left;
- the strike nearest NIFTY's close; closing prices; settled at intrinsic value on NIFTY's close
  on the expiry date.
Return in % of NIFTY's close, after charges.
  V1  Conditional short straddle. Sell only when the straddle's implied volatility exceeds 1.2x
      NIFTY's 20-session realised volatility. Straddle IV = (CE + PE) / (0.8 x S x sqrt(days/365));
      realised = the annualised population SD of the last 20 daily log returns up to that session.
  V2  Short strangle. Sell the call at the strike nearest S x 1.02 and the put nearest S x 0.98.
  V3  Long straddle, the BUYER's side of F11: buy CE + PE at the ATM strike, hold to expiry.
      Included because the owner buys options. Its PASS would mean buying volatility paid.

FAMILY: R1, R2, V1, V2, V3. Holm across all 5.
PASS = validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final one-sided p (Student-t, months - 1 df,
clustered by calendar month) survives Holm at 5%. Fewer than 30 final trades = NOT TESTABLE.
Worst single trade reported for every member.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES
A PASS goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first. V1/V2 are option SELLING (margin, crash risk);
even a PASS would be information for the page, not a row for the owner's Zerodha buying.

CODE CHECKS FIRST (synthetic only): a planted drift must be found by R1; a planted premium by V1;
noise must not pass; changing future prices must leave earlier trades identical.
