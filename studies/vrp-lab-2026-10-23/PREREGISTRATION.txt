2. DATA AND PERIODS
- NSE's F&O bhavcopy, NIFTY index options expiring within 8 calendar days of each session, with open, high, low, close,
  settle, open interest and contracts (release data-nifty-options, nod-2019 .. nod-2026, scripts/nse-nifty-option-days.js).
  NIFTY's official open: NSE's ind_close_all (release data-macro).
- Discovery: sessions from 2019-02-11 to 2022-12-31; confirmation: 2023-01-01 to 2026-10-01.
- DAY -k: a session that is k sessions before the nearest weekly expiry after it (sessions = the days with an option file);
  the trade uses that expiry's options.

3. THE TRADE (the expiry-day X2 trade, except the exit)
- An ATM straddle: the call and the put with the listed strike nearest NIFTY's official open (ties as the expiry study:
  the higher call, the lower put), each SOLD at its official open (open > 0).
- Each leg is bought back at 2x its entry if the day's high reached it (the stop), otherwise at the day's official close
  (the settle when the close is not above zero).
- Costs exactly as the expiry study: api/_lib/trade-costs.js roundTripCost for 75 units, plus 1 point of slippage a leg.
- Value of a trade: the straddle's points (sold minus bought back minus costs) as a % of NIFTY's open.

4. MEMBERS (4)
 D1 the session before expiry (day -1).   D2 day -2.   D3 day -3.   D4 day -4.

5. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member: monthly mean (%) of its trades; Newey-West t (lag 3). Holm across the 4 at 5%. Full Deflated Sharpe with
  N = 393 trials (389 before, these 4), as the night lab computes it.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2019-2022. CONFIRMED = then on 2023-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2. A
  confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first (information only: selling needs margin and can lose many
  days' premium in one move). PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2. FAIL otherwise.
- Reported, not judged: trades, points a trade, win rate, worst trade (points and % of NIFTY), stops hit.
- Expectation, stated now: discovery is short (about 4 years, ~200 trades a member); the most likely outcome is that
  nothing passes both periods.
