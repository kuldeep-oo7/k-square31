DEFINITIONS (identical to research/orderflow-2026-10-03 except the bounce's direction)
Data: NSE's own CM bhavcopy (series EQ, by symbol) and F&O bhavcopy (futures rows; a stock's total
open interest = the sum over all its futures expiries).
Universe and resets: as research/orderflow-2026-10-03:
- the top 200 by 60-session median traded value;
- a reset is an open >= 18% from the previous close; a trade crossing one is void.
For entry session d, from sessions d-1 and d-2 (both trading sessions in a row, no reset):
  R  = close(d-1) / close(d-2) - 1  (the share)
  OI = totalOI(d-1) / totalOI(d-2) - 1
  A "moving" day: |R| >= 1.5581% AND |OI| >= 3.0380%. These are the cut-offs fixed on the old
  development window, frozen here.
RULE LU (long-unwinding BOUNCE):  R < 0 and OI < 0 on a moving day -> BUY at the official open of d,
                                   SELL at the official close of d+9 (10 sessions).
RULE SB (short build-up):          R < 0 and OI > 0 on a moving day -> SELL SHORT at the official open
                                   of d, BUY back at the official close of d (1 session).
Net = the signed price return - calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs (dated, quantity
ceil(500000/open)) - a roll round trip per month end crossed. Paper futures trades; no option is priced.

WHAT IS COUNTED
Every signal with entry on or after 2026-10-05, from NSE's official files only.

VERDICT (each rule on its own; two rules, so each is judged at 2.5%)
COLLECTING until at least 100 finished trades AND 3 calendar months since 2026-10-05.
Then: PASS if mean net > 0 AND the one-sided t (clustered by ISO week of entry) is at or above the
2.5% Student-t critical value with (weeks - 1) df. Otherwise FAIL.
Nothing may change after this commit.
