1. WHY, AND WHAT HAS BEEN SEEN (disclosed)
- The owner, 29 Oct: "we want to do it in the GM Global and I want to earn money in 1 month before expiry ... after
  Friday closing your loss will be booked and Monday opening will be your new buying price for the whole week".
  The desk recorded the same rule from the owner on 20 Oct: each Monday the buy price resets to Monday's open and the
  previous week's P&L is booked; GM lists only the expiring month and auto-sells it; the score is the running rupee total;
  20 units is the minimum size. Booking is taken to apply to profits and losses alike. If the owner says otherwise, a
  dated amendment comes BEFORE the run.
- So under GM only Monday open -> Friday close counts; a weekend gap never counts; every position ends at expiry.
- Already seen: the 21 Oct GM tournament (86 old rules under these GM rules, 0 PASS; it judged real P&L and only
  reported the gap-removed number); tonight's chart lab (14 daily members, 0 PASS); the night lab's overnight/intraday

  12-1 momentum (FAIL on 2020-2026 in final-year tests the 4 Oct audit called underpowered); relative strength was
  "profiled" before 27 Sep. 2020-2026 is therefore not a virgin sample for trend or relative-strength ideas.
- A Monday-gap member was considered and DROPPED before any data: its rank must be known before the fill, and from

  Business Standard; the equity pre-open is 09:00-09:08 order entry), so the daily files cannot time it honestly.

2. DATA AND DESIGN
- The chart lab's data and loaders (studies/chart-lab-2026-10-29/run.js), plus NIFTY's near-month index future from the
  same NSE F&O files (IDX rows). Discovery 2005-01-01 .. 2020-10-31; confirmation 2020-10-01 .. 2026-10-01.
- Week = the market's sessions in one calendar week (Monday to Friday). Its first session is GM's "Monday open" (Tuesday
  if Monday is a holiday); its last session is GM's "Friday close".
- Signal: from WEEKLY bars of the share (EQ, unadjusted; weekly open = first session's open, high/low = extremes,
  close = last session's close), through the close of week w-1. Position in week w.
- A stock signal needs at least 260 daily bars since the last reset (a session opening 18% or more from the previous
  close) at the signal close, and at least 35 weekly bars. Universe: the futures lab's (top 200 by median traded value),
  for the month of week w's first session, with a near-month future on that session.

3. GM SCORING (judged as GM scores; real P&L reported beside it)
- Each position-week on the near-month future: GM gross = side x (close at end / open of the first session - 1), where
  end = the week's last session, or the contract's expiry session if that falls inside the week (GM auto-sells; the rest
  of that week is sat out, and re-entry waits for the next week's first session on the new contract).
- A HOLDING = consecutive position-weeks of one member in one stock, same side, same contract. Charges (dated NSE futures
  charges, quantity ceil(Rs 5 lakh / first open)) only at the holding's first open and its last close. None at a
  Monday re-base, because no trade happens there.
- Net = GM gross - the charges of that week, as % of the holding's first notional.
- VALUE judged: stock members (W1-W4) = GM EXCESS = net - side x M_w, where M_w = the mean GM gross (same week rules) of
  the universe's near-month futures (at least 30 names). NIFTY members (W5) = net.
- Dropped (not counted, and the holding ends): a missing price; any close-to-close move of the held contract inside the
  week (or open to first close) beyond -18% / +22%; a share reset inside the week.
- Real P&L (with weekend gaps) of each holding = side x (last close / first open - 1); reported, with the GM-minus-real
  difference flagged when large. That difference is GM's bookkeeping, not the market, and would not carry over to real
  money.

4. MEMBERS (5 ideas x 2 directions = 10). SMA30 = mean of the last 30 weekly closes; "rising" = SMA30 above its value 4
   weekly bars earlier, "falling" = below it.
 W1L STAGE 2 (Weinstein): weekly close > SMA30, SMA30 rising.        W1S STAGE 4: close < SMA30, SMA30 falling.
 W2L WEEKLY DONCHIAN LONG (Turtle on weeks): long from a weekly close above the highest high of the previous 20 weekly
     bars until a weekly close below the lowest low of the previous 10.  W2S the mirror (20-week low in, 10-week high out).
     Exits are evaluated before entries on the same bar.
 W3L RELATIVE STRENGTH (O'Neil/Minervini RS, Jegadeesh-Titman): each week rank the universe by the return from the close
     27 weekly bars before week w to the close 2 weekly bars before (skip the latest week); long the top tenth (at least
     30 ranked).  W3S short the bottom tenth.
 W4L WEEKLY CANDLE AT THE 30-WEEK LINE (Nison's location rule): SMA30 rising; low <= 1.02 x SMA30 and close >= 0.98 x
     SMA30; and a bullish engulfing or a hammer on the weekly bars (the chart lab's candle definitions). One week.
 W4S the mirror: SMA30 falling; high >= 0.98 x SMA30 and close <= 1.02 x SMA30; a bearish engulfing or a shooting star.
 W5L NIFTY TREND: on the weekly closes of NIFTY's near-month future (whole period; index closes cover only part of
     2005-2020): close > SMA30 and SMA30 rising -> long NIFTY's near-month future.  W5S close < SMA30, falling -> short.

5. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Member score: monthly mean (%) of its position-week values (month of the week's first session); Newey-West t (lag 3).
  Fewer than 24 months: NOT TESTABLE. Holm across the testable members at 5% (one-sided). Full Deflated Sharpe with
  N = 440 trials (430 before, plus these 10).
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm and DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and
  t >= 2. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2. FAIL otherwise. A confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper
  test first.
- Reported, not judged, both periods: position-weeks, holdings, win rate, mean net and excess % a week; GM rupees at 20
  units (gross) and real rupees; the share of expiry months (NIFTY's monthly expiry to the next) with a positive rupee
  total, and the worst one; how much of the rupee total came from the top tenth of position-weeks by price; the
  discovery halves 2005-2012 and 2013-2020.
- Expectation, stated now: most members FAIL. Trend following has its best evidence on diversified futures over
  months; single NSE stocks over weeks after charges may show little.

     - week 2 is cut at a Tuesday expiry;
