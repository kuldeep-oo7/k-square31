CYCLE (identical to V2 / F11)
For each NIFTY option expiry E (sorted): the entry day d is the first session after the previous
expiry with 4..10 calendar days to E that has NIFTY option rows and a NIFTY close. E must have a
NIFTY close. Enter at d's closing option prices; hold to expiry; every leg settles at intrinsic
value on NIFTY's close on E. S = NIFTY close on d.

MEMBERS (all short the inner pair and long the outer pair: a defined-risk iron condor)
"nearest(x, type)" = the listed strike of that expiry and type with a positive close nearest x.
  IC1  Fixed 2% / 4%: short CE nearest S x 1.02, short PE nearest S x 0.98; long CE nearest
       S x 1.04, long PE nearest S x 0.96.
  IC2  The market's own range (OWN): M = CE + PE at the strike nearest S (the ATM straddle, the
       market's priced expected move). Short CE nearest S + M, short PE nearest S - M; long CE
       nearest S + 2M, long PE nearest S - 2M.
  IC3  IC2 with a volatility-clustering brake (OWN; Mandelbrot 1963, Engle 1982: big moves cluster):
       skip the week when NIFTY's 5-session realised volatility exceeds 1.5x its 60-session realised
       volatility, both from daily log closes up to and including d.
A week is skipped if any leg is missing, or a long strike is not strictly beyond its short strike.

RETURN (per condor, in % of S, after costs)
  net = (credit - settlement loss - costs) / S, where credit = shortCE + shortPE - longCE - longPE,
  settlement loss = max(SE - KshortCE, 0) - max(SE - KlongCE, 0) + max(KshortPE - SE, 0)
                    - max(KlongPE - SE, 0), SE = NIFTY close on E.
  Costs per leg: api/_lib/trade-costs.js roundTripCost({entryPremium, exitPremium:
  max(intrinsic, 0.05), quantity: 75}).totalCost / 75 (as V2), PLUS slippage of 0.25 index points
  per leg (4 legs = 1.0 point a condor), because closing prices are not fills and wings are cheap.
  Also reported, descriptively: net as % of the condor's maximum risk (wider wing width - credit).

VERDICT (protocol v2)
Monthly series: for each calendar month with a trade, the mean net per trade (% of S) of the trades
entered that month. t = mean / Newey-West SE (lag 3) over ALL months. Annual Sharpe = mean / sd x
sqrt(12).
  PASS       mean > 0 AND t >= 3.0
  PROMISING  mean > 0 AND 2.0 <= t < 3.0 (a forward-test candidate, never a trading rule)
  FAIL       otherwise;  NOT TESTABLE = fewer than 24 months with trades.
Also reported: Holm across the 3 members (one-sided normal p, 5%); the Deflated Sharpe Ratio
probability against the protocol-v2 audit's benchmark of 2.05 (expected best annual Sharpe of 73
skill-less trials), DSR = Phi((Sharpe - 2.05) x sqrt(years)); the old three windows
(development < 2024-09-19, validation .. 2025-09-17, final 2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15), descriptively;
win rate, worst week, worst month, and the naked V2 strangle recomputed with the same slippage,
for comparison only (not a member).

CODE CHECKS FIRST (synthetic data only; the real run is refused unless all three pass)
 (1) a planted premium (options priced at 1.3x the true volatility) must give IC1 mean > 0, t > 2;
 (2) fair prices (implied = true volatility) and zero costs, 3 markets x 3 members: at most 1 PASS;
 (3) changing every price after a cut date must leave every earlier trade identical.
