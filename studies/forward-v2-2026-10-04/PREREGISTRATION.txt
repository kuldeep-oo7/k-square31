THE RULES (identical to the studies)
STRANGLE. Data: NSE's own F&O bhavcopy (NIFTY options, the two nearest expiries) and NSE's daily
index file (content/indices/ind_close_all_DDMMYYYY.csv, "Nifty 50" closing value; checked 4 Oct 2026:
1 Oct 2026 = 22,421.95).
- For each NIFTY option expiry E (as listed in the bhavcopy), the first session d after the previous
  expiry with 4..10 calendar days to E.
- S = NIFTY's close on d. Sell the call at the listed strike nearest S x 1.02 and the put nearest
  S x 0.98, at their closing prices on d. Hold to E; settle at intrinsic value against NIFTY's close
  on E.
- Net = (premium - payout - charges) / S, with charges from api/_lib/trade-costs.js roundTripCost
  .totalCost, quantity 75 per leg. Option SELLING: it needs margin, and a crash loses more than one
  week's premium. Information, not a row for the owner's Zerodha buying.
MA150-A / MA150-B. Data: NSE CM bhavcopy (series EQ, by symbol); the universe and resets of
research/ta-atlas-2026-10-03:
- the top 200 by 60-session median traded value;
- at least 250 bars after the last reset (an open >= 18% from the previous close);
- the signal bars all after the last reset; a trade crossing a reset is void.
On bars up to d-1:
- A: close(d-2) >= SMA150(d-2) and close(d-1) < SMA150(d-1);
- B: SMA5(d-2) >= SMA150(d-2) and SMA5(d-1) < SMA150(d-1).
Short at the official open of d, cover at the official close of d+9 (10 sessions). Futures charges,
both legs, dated, quantity ceil(500000/open), plus a roll per month end crossed.
SCORED AGAINST THE MARKET: excess = net + B(d,10), where B is the equal-weighted open(d) ->
close(d+9) return of that month's universe stocks (protocol v2). This is a short against the market
(e.g. stock future short + NIFTY future long), not a bet that the market falls.

WHAT IS COUNTED
Every signal with entry on or after 2026-10-05, prices from NSE's official files only.

VERDICT (three rules; each judged at 5% / 3 = 1.67% one-sided)
- STRANGLE: COLLECTING until 26 finished trades (about 6 months).
- MA150-A, MA150-B: COLLECTING until 100 finished trades and 3 months.
Then PASS if the mean (net for STRANGLE, excess for MA150) > 0 AND the one-sided t (clustered by
ISO week of entry) is at or above the 1.67% Student-t critical value with (weeks - 1) df. Otherwise
FAIL. Nothing may change after this commit.
