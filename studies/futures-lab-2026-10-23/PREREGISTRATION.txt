2. DESIGN
- Discovery NSE 2005-01 .. 2020-10 (release data-oos); confirmation 2020-10 .. 2026-10-01 (the research snapshot, with
  data-oos before 2020-09-01 as look-back only); as the night lab and the 09:15 lab, sharing October 2020.
- FUTURES PRICE: for a stock on day d, the near-month contract = the earliest expiry on or after d in NSE's F&O file of d
  with an open and a close above zero; entry at its official open, exit at its official close (NSE's settlement price).
  A multi-day hold keeps that contract and, on its expiry day, rolls at that day's close into the next contract (the
  return chains the two contracts' own closes; one roll round trip in charges per expiry crossed).
- Charges: calculateNseFuturesCharges (dated) on both legs, quantity ceil(Rs 5 lakh / entry price).
- FUTURES EXCESS for a one-session trade = net + the equal-weighted open-to-close return on d of the night lab's universe
  (top 200 by traded value, >= 250 bars after the last reset), each on its own near-month future (at least 30 names).
- Signals exactly as api/_lib/oi-flow-forward.js (SB and LU), on the share's closes and the total futures OI, as before.

3. MEMBERS (7)
 U1 SB ON FUTURES: every SB trade; short the future at the open of d, cover at the close of d. Judged on NET.
 U2 STRONG SB ON FUTURES (night lab S7: fall >= 3%, OI >= 6%): as U1, judged on FUTURES EXCESS.
 U3 LONG-UNWINDING BOUNCE ON FUTURES (oi-flow-forward LU): buy the future at the open of d, sell at the close of d+9
    (10 sessions), rolling at an expiry as above. Judged on NET.
 U4 THE SHARE DOES NOT GAP DOWN, ON FUTURES (09:15 lab F2): SB trades split by the share's gap at the open (take if >= 0);
    SPREAD of FUTURES EXCESS, take minus skip, each month.
 U5 TREES, U6 LOGISTIC, U7 NEURAL NETWORK: the 09:15 lab's M1-M3 exactly (its 17 features, models, settings, pinned
    libraries, walk-forward by calendar year from 2008, take above the training median), except the label is FUTURES
    EXCESS > 0; SPREAD of FUTURES EXCESS, take minus skip, each month.
 A trade without a futures price is not counted.

4. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member: the monthly mean (%) of its trades' values (U1, U3 net; U2 excess; U4-U7 the monthly spread); Newey-West t
  (lag 3). Holm across the 7 at 5%. Full Deflated Sharpe with N = 359 trials (352 before, these 7), as the night lab.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020 (U5-U7 from 2008). CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026
  mean > 0 and t >= 2. A confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and
  t >= 2. FAIL otherwise.
- Reported, not judged: every member on 2020-2026; trades, win rate, worst month; for U1-U3 the same trades priced on the
  share, side by side; for 2020-2026 GM rupees at 20 units (gross) on futures.
- Expectation, stated now: SB on futures is likely positive but weaker than on shares in 2005-2020 as well; the learning
  machines' share-priced advantage leaned on the share's gap, so on futures it may shrink or vanish.
