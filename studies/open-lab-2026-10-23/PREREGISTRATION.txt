2. DESIGN (as the night lab, studies/night-lab-2026-10-23, PREREGISTRATION + AMENDMENT-A1)
- Discovery NSE 2005-01 .. 2020-10 (release data-oos); confirmation 2020-10 .. 2026-10-01 (the research snapshot, with
  data-oos before 2020-09-01 as look-back only). They share October 2020, as the night lab.
- Every SB trade: short at NSE's official open of d, cover at the close of d. EXCESS = net after calculateNseFuturesCharges
  + the equal-weighted open-to-close return on d of the night lab's universe (>= 30 names), as night lab S5-S7.
- A trade's features use only what is known at 09:15 on d: bars up to d-1, NSE's F&O file of d-1, and the official OPEN of
  d (the stock's and NIFTY's). NIFTY: NSE's index file (open and close); before it, the near-month NIFTY future, its open
  and close against the SAME contract's previous close.

3. FEATURES (17, at 09:15 on d)
 R     close(d-1) / close(d-2) - 1                      OI    total futures OI(d-1) / OI(d-2) - 1
 GAP   open(d) / close(d-1) - 1                          NGAP  NIFTY open(d) / NIFTY close(d-1) - 1
 NRET  NIFTY close(d-1) / close(d-2) - 1                 VOLR  volume(d-1) / mean volume(d-21 .. d-2)
 TREND close(d-1) / mean close(d-20 .. d-1) - 1          VOL20 st. dev. of daily close-to-close returns, d-20 .. d-1
 BASIS near-month future close(d-1) / close(d-1) - 1     NSIG  number of SB signals entering on d
 DOW   weekday of d                                       TOEXP calendar days from d to the nearest stock-futures expiry
                                                                on or after d (NSE's F&O file of d-1)
 LOGP  ln close(d-1)                                      LOGV  ln median traded value of the 60 bars before d
 OIREL OI(d-1) / mean OI(d-20 .. d-1)                     PREV  SB signals of this stock entering in the 10 sessions
 R2    close(d-2) / close(d-3) - 1                              before d
 A feature whose inputs are missing is left missing.

4. MEMBERS (10). Each splits the SB trades into TAKE and SKIP.
 F1 NIFTY GAPS DOWN:            take if NGAP < 0.
 F2 THE STOCK DOES NOT GAP DOWN: take if GAP >= 0.
 F3 HEAVY VOLUME:               take if VOLR >= 2.
 F4 FUTURE AT A DISCOUNT:       take if BASIS < 0.
 F5 DOWNTREND:                  take if TREND < 0.
 F6 FIRST SIGNAL:               take if PREV = 0.
 F7 NOT EXPIRY WEEK:            take if TOEXP >= 7.
 M1 TREES: scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=200, learning_rate=0.05, max_leaf_nodes=15,
    min_samples_leaf=200, l2_regularization=1.0, early_stopping=False, random_state=0) on all 17 features (missing kept).
 M2 LOGISTIC: LogisticRegression(C=1.0, max_iter=2000) on standardized features (missing -> the training median).
 M3 NEURAL NETWORK: MLPClassifier(hidden_layer_sizes=(32, 16), alpha=1e-3, learning_rate_init=1e-3, max_iter=200,
    early_stopping=True, validation_fraction=0.1, random_state=0) on standardized features (missing -> training median).
 M1-M3: label = EXCESS > 0. Walk-forward: for each calendar year Y from 2008 to 2026, fit on every SB trade entering before
 1 Jan Y (from the discovery data before 2020-10-01, the confirmation data from 2020-10-01, so no trade twice), predict the
 trades of year Y; TAKE if the predicted probability is above the median predicted probability on that year's training
 trades. Python 3.12, scikit-learn 1.9.1, numpy 2.5.4, pinned.

5. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each month: SPREAD = mean EXCESS of the TAKE trades - mean EXCESS of the SKIP trades (months with both). The monthly
  series -> mean (%), Newey-West t (lag 3).
- Holm across the 10 at 5%. Full Deflated Sharpe (Bailey and Lopez de Prado 2014, skew and kurtosis) on the monthly series
  with N = 344 trials (317 before the night lab, its 17, these 10), as the night lab computes it.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020 (M1-M3 from 2008, their first predicted year).
  CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2. A confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first, never
  straight to FUTURES. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2, not a pass. FAIL otherwise.
- Reported, not judged: TAKE's own EXCESS (monthly mean, t), the share of trades taken, and for 2020-2026 GM rupees at 20
  units (gross) of TAKE and of all SB.
- Expectation, stated now: the most likely outcome is that nothing passes both periods.
