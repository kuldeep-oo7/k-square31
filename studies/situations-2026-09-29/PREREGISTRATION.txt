ALIGNMENT (nothing from after the decision may enter it)
For an NSE session d: "US(d)" is the S&P 500's close-to-close return on the last US session
dated BEFORE d; "CRUDE(d)" is Brent's close-to-close return on the last Brent session dated
BEFORE d; "VIX(d)" is India VIX's close on the NSE session before d and its change that day;
"GAP(d)" = open(d) / close(d-1) - 1 on NIFTY; the regime and candle of d are read from bars up
to and including d-1 (classifyMarketRegime; identifyCandlestickPatterns on the last 7 bars).

COSTS
Every trade pays api/_lib/shadow-broker.js calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs, dated,
quantity = ceil(500000 / entry), as the regime study did. Returns are NET, in % of entry.

PART A -- DIRECTION HYPOTHESES (each a trade on NIFTY or a stock; signed so a correct call is +)
A1 US OVERNIGHT. US(d) <= -1%: short NIFTY at open(d), out at close(d). US(d) >= +1%: long.
A2 CRUDE SPIKE. CRUDE(d) >= +3%: short NIFTY open(d)->close(d). CRUDE(d) <= -3%: long.
A3 GAP CONTINUATION. GAP(d) <= -1%: short NIFTY open(d)->close(d). GAP(d) >= +1%: long.
A4 VIX SPIKE. India VIX up >= 10% on the session before d: short NIFTY open(d) -> close(d+4).
A5 NIFTY CANDLE. The desk's candlestick bias of NIFTY's bar d-1 (identifyCandlestickPatterns):
   BULLISH -> long NIFTY open(d) -> close(d+4); BEARISH -> short; NEUTRAL or none -> no trade.
P1 STOCK CANDLE. The same, per stock of the panel, on each stock's own bars: BULLISH -> long
   the stock open(d) -> close(d+4), BEARISH -> short. Errors clustered by CALENDAR MONTH.
Unit: one trade per signal-day (per stock-day for P1); errors clustered by calendar month
(api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean), because consecutive days share a market.
PASS (each): validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final one-sided p-value passes
Holm-Bonferroni across the SIX hypotheses at a family-wise 5% (the smallest p against 0.05/6,
the next against 0.05/5, ...; stop at the first that fails). p from Student-t with
(final-window months - 1) degrees of freedom. Fewer than 20 final-window trades: NOT TESTABLE.

PART B -- DESCRIPTIVE: EVERY DESK RULE, BY SITUATION (no verdict, no PASS, counts shown)
Rules on NIFTY: B-LONG the desk's futures buy (limit 0.8% under open(d), target +2.5%, stop
-1.5%, 20 sessions, as research/regime-2026-09-29); B-SHORT its mirror (the put stand-in);
B-DAY NIFTY open(d)->close(d), long; B-WEEK NIFTY close(d-1)->close(d+4), long.
Situations of d (each known at d's open): GAP(d) in <= -1%, -1..-0.3%, -0.3..+0.3%, +0.3..+1%,
>= +1%; weekly expiry day; the session before an expiry; monthly expiry week (d within the 5
sessions ending at a FUTIDX expiry); VIX(d) tercile and NIFTY 20-session realised volatility
tercile (both terciles fixed on the DEVELOPMENT window only); regime BULL / NEUTRAL / BEAR;
NIFTY above / below its 200-session average; US(d) <= -1%, between, >= +1%; CRUDE(d) <= -3%,
between, >= +3%. Every bucket: trades, mean, month-clustered t, share of winners, over the whole
period and per window. A bucket under 30 trades is marked "too few". Nothing here is a test.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES, STATED IN ADVANCE
A PART A PASS goes onto TRACK as a paper rule, labelled "passed on re-used data", and onto the
page only after TRACK confirms it forward. A FAIL is reported as a FAIL with its numbers.
PART B is shown to the user as it comes out, marked descriptive, in the dated folder.
