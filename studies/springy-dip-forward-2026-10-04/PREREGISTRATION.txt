PRE-REGISTRATION -- FORWARD PAPER TEST of "SPRINGY DIP" (OWN rule E1)
Written Sunday 4 Oct 2026, BEFORE the first session it covers (Monday 5 Oct 2026). Committed to the repo
before that session opens; the commit time is the proof.

WHERE THE RULE COMES FROM
research/dip-ml-2026-10-04 (pre-registered, sha256 89a8eca5..., run once on NSE's CM bhavcopy
2020-09..2026-10). E1 was the only one of 10 members positive in all three windows:
development +0.551% a trade (t 1.89, 1,773 trades), validation +0.663% (t 1.53, 874), final
+0.209% (t 0.41, 639). It FAILED that test: too weak to rule out luck. Because that history has now
been looked at, only FUTURE prices can test it honestly. That is this test.

THE RULE (identical to E1)
Data: NSE's own CM bhavcopy (series EQ, by symbol), one file per session.
Reset: an open >= 18% away from the previous close (splits, bonuses, demergers). A stock's signal
bars must all come after its last reset, and a trade whose window contains a reset is void.
Universe: on each month's first session, the 200 EQ stocks with the highest median daily traded
value over the previous 60 sessions, among stocks with at least 250 bars after their last reset.
Signal for session d, read from completed bars up to d-1:
  ac = lag-1 autocorrelation of the stock's daily close-to-close returns over the 120 bars to d-1;
  z  = (close(d-1) - SMA20(d-1)) / SD20(d-1), with SD the population standard deviation;
  SIGNAL when ac < -0.05 AND z <= -2.
Trade: buy at the official open of d, sell at the official close of d+4 (5 sessions).
Net return = close/open - 1 - charges. Charges are api/_lib/shadow-broker.js
calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs, dated, quantity ceil(500000/open), plus one roll round
trip per month end crossed. A paper futures trade: no option is priced.

WHAT IS COUNTED
Every signal with entry on or after 2026-10-05. Prices come only from NSE's official files, never
a live quote, so nothing can be chosen after the fact.

VERDICT (stated now)
COLLECTING until at least 100 finished trades AND at least 3 calendar months since 2026-10-05.
Then, and at every later update:
PASS if the mean net return > 0 AND the one-sided t (errors clustered by ISO week of entry) is at or
above the 5% Student-t critical value with (weeks - 1) df. Otherwise FAIL.
This is ONE test of ONE rule, so there is no multiple-testing correction. Nothing about the rule may
change after this commit. A changed rule is a new test with a new date.

WHAT IT DOES ON THE PAGE
The TRACK tab shows the list and the running score, labelled "OWN rule, forward paper test", with
the backtest numbers above. It is not a recommendation until it PASSES here.
