EVALUATION PROTOCOL v2 -- re-scoring every strategy tested on 2-4 Oct 2026, written BEFORE re-scoring.
sha256 in PROTOCOL.sha256.

WHY (the audit, 4 Oct 2026)
The owner asked whether the testing itself is wrong. Measured against the literature, the old verdict
rule had too little statistical power:
- It judged each strategy on the final 12 months only, clustered by month (11 df).
- It required the validation year to be positive as well.
- It applied Holm across up to 36 members at once.
The one-sided critical t was then about 4 on 12 monthly points: a strategy needed an annual Sharpe
of about 4 inside one year to PASS. Good real strategies run a Sharpe of 1-2.
Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016, RFS 29(1)) set the bar for a new factor at t > 3.0 over the WHOLE sample.
Bailey & Lopez de Prado (2014) correct the Sharpe ratio for the number of trials. Splitting six
years into three windows is needed only where parameters were chosen on the data. Most rules here
are PUBLISHED with their own parameters, fixed long before 2020, so all six years are out-of-sample
for them.
Also, long-only stock signals were scored on raw returns, so market moves swamped them. The
standard is the return in EXCESS of the market.

WHAT IS RE-SCORED (rules and trades unchanged; only the judging changes)
Every member of research/rising-stocks-2026-10-02 (3), ta-atlas-2026-10-03 (36), dip-ml-2026-10-04
(10), orderflow-2026-10-03 (12), families-2026-10-04 (12) and results-vrp-2026-10-04 (5): 78
members. Each study's own run() produces its trades from the same data. The earlier panel studies
(composite, momentum, published strategies, regime, situations) used other data and are not
included; that is stated.

THE NEW SCORE
1. Excess return. For a stock trade of a member with a fixed holding h (sessions) entered at the
   open of d:
     excess = net - side x B(d, h),
   where B is the equal-weighted return, open(d) to close(d+h-1), of that month's universe stocks
   (the top 200 by 60-session median traded value, at least 250 bars after the last reset, whole
   window traded, no reset inside). side is +1 for a long and -1 for a short.
   Members on NIFTY, BANKNIFTY or options, pairs (already hedged), and desk-geometry members
   (variable exit) are scored on net return; the score says which.
   Fixed holdings (in sessions):
   - ta-atlas: 10;
   - dip-ml: D6 = 1, the rest = 5;
   - orderflow: B odd = 1, B even = 10;
   - families: F1, F2, F5, F6 = 5;
   - results: R1, R2 = 10.
2. Monthly series. For each calendar month from Sep 2020 to Sep 2026 with at least one trade: the
   mean excess (or net) per trade of the trades entered that month.
3. Statistic. t = mean of the monthly series / its Newey-West standard error (lag 3), over ALL
   months of all three old windows together. Annualised Sharpe of the monthly series = mean / sd x
   sqrt(12), with the number of months.
4. Verdict.
   PASS       = mean > 0 AND t >= 3.0 (Harvey-Liu-Zhu).
   PROMISING  = mean > 0 AND 2.0 <= t < 3.0. Never a trading rule by itself; a candidate for a
                forward paper test.
   FAIL       = otherwise.
   NOT TESTABLE = fewer than 24 months with trades.
   Also reported:
   - Holm across all 78 (one-sided, 5% family-wise; normal approximation, since df are about 70);
   - the Deflated Sharpe Ratio probability (Bailey & Lopez de Prado 2014, with N = 78 trials, using
     the cross-member variance of the annualised Sharpe ratios), a DSR >= 0.95 counting as
     significant after selection.
5. Members whose parameters were fitted on this data are flagged FITTED: the order-flow terciles,
   the PCR terciles, the results cut-offs, and ridge ML (refit walk-forward, so its own trades are
   out-of-sample by construction). For them only, the old development window is excluded and the
   score uses validation + final.
6. Power, stated with the result: over N months a true annual Sharpe S gives t of about
   S x sqrt(N/12); with N = 72, t = 3 needs S of about 1.22.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES
A PASS becomes a forward paper test on TRACK (like the springy dip), then a page rule only if that
forward test passes. A PROMISING member may become a forward paper test. The page changes only
through those steps.
