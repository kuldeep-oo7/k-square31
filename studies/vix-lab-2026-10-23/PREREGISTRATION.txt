3. MEMBERS (4)
 V1 FEAR SPIKE: VIX closes at least 20% above its previous close -> BUY NIFTY, hold 5 sessions (sell at the close of the 5th).
 V2 FEAR HIGH: VIX closes at its highest close of the previous 250 sessions -> BUY NIFTY, hold 10 sessions.
 V3 CALM: VIX closes at its lowest close of the previous 250 sessions -> SHORT NIFTY, hold 10 sessions.
 V4 VARIANCE PREMIUM: VIX minus NIFTY's 20-session realized volatility (st. dev. of daily log returns x sqrt(252) x 100) at
    or above its 80th percentile of the previous 250 sessions -> BUY NIFTY, hold 5 sessions.
 Overlapping holds are separate trades. The previous 250 sessions need at least 200 values.

4. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member: monthly mean (%) of its trades' net returns; Newey-West t (lag 3). Holm across the 4 at 5%. Full Deflated
  Sharpe with N = 385 trials (381 before, these 4), as the night lab computes it.
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2014-2020. CONFIRMED = then on 2020-2026 mean > 0 and t >= 2. A
  confirmed pass goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2. FAIL otherwise.
  NOT TESTABLE: fewer than 24 months with a trade.
- Reported, not judged: trades, win rate, worst month, mean a trade, GM rupees at 20 units for 2020-2026 (gross).
- Expectation, stated now: discovery is short (about 6 years); fear signals are rare; the most likely outcome is that
  nothing passes both periods.
