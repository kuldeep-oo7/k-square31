PRE-REGISTRATION -- BUY THE DIP / MEAN REVERSION, AND TWO OF OUR OWN (statistics and machine learning)
Written 4 Oct 2026 BEFORE any signal or result was computed for these rules. sha256 in PREREGISTRATION.sha256.

WHY
The owner asked for buy-the-dip, for published research rules not yet tried, and for "our own"
strategy built from mathematics, statistics, physics and machine learning. The TA atlas (3 Oct)
noticed that dip-buying rules did better than breakout rules in its DEVELOPMENT window. That
observation motivates this family, so this family is NOT independent of it. Stated.

WHAT THIS DATA HAS ALREADY BEEN USED FOR
About 13 earlier studies on these windows, all FAIL. The final window is not a clean holdout.
A PASS is a paper candidate only.

DATA, UNIVERSE, RESETS
Exactly as research/ta-atlas-2026-10-03/PREREGISTRATION.txt:
- NSE CM bhavcopy 2020-09..2026-10, series EQ by symbol;
- an open >= 18% from the previous close is a reset;
- the top 200 by 60-session median traded value, with at least 250 bars after the last reset.

TRADE (uniform)
A signal is read from completed bars up to d-1.
- LONG: buy at open(d), sell at close(d+4) (5 sessions); SHORT: the reverse.
- Exception: D6 is intraday, open(d) to close(d).
- Costs: calculateNseFuturesCharges on both legs, dated, quantity ceil(500000/price), plus a roll
  round trip per month end crossed. Net %.
- A trade whose window contains a reset is dropped.

MEMBERS (Holm across all 10)
Published mean reversion:
  D1  Short-term reversal (Lehmann 1990, Jegadeesh 1990): each day, the universe stocks in the
      bottom 10% by 5-session return (close d-1 vs close d-6) -> long.
  D2  The same, the top 10% -> short.
  D3  Connors-style trend dip: close(d-1) > SMA200 AND it is the third lower close in a row -> long.
  D4  Internal bar strength: IBS = (close-low)/(high-low) of d-1 <= 0.2 AND close > SMA200 -> long.
  D5  Oversold in an uptrend: RSI(14) of d-1 < 35 AND SMA50 > SMA200 -> long (first day of the episode).
  D6  Gap-down fade: open(d) <= close(d-1) x 0.98 -> long at open(d), exit at close(d).
      The open is known when the trade is placed.
Our own (new; labelled OWN on any page):
  E1  OU dip: a stock whose own lag-1 autocorrelation of daily returns over the previous 120 bars
      is below -0.05 (it mean-reverts, like an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process), AND whose
      z = (close - SMA20) / sd20 <= -2 on d-1 -> long.
  E2  The same filter, z >= +2 -> short.
  E3  Walk-forward ridge regression (machine learning, linear).
      Features at d-1, each standardised across that day's universe:
        5-day return, 20-day return, 60-day return, RSI(14), distance from SMA50, ATR(14)%,
        volume / 20-day average volume, IBS, lag-1 autocorrelation (120 bars).
      Target: the 5-session forward net-of-nothing return (open d to close d+4), standardised
      per day.
      The model is refit on the first session of every calendar quarter, using ONLY samples whose
      target window ended before that session (all earlier history), ridge lambda = 10 (fixed).
      Each day: the top 10% of predicted -> long.
  E4  The same model, the bottom 10% -> short.
      The first fit needs 250 sessions of samples, so E3/E4 trade from about 2022 onwards.

WINDOWS
development: before 2024-09-19 | validation: 2024-09-19 .. 2025-09-17 | final: 2025-09-18 .. 2026-09-15

STATISTICS
The unit is the trade, clustered by calendar month of entry (api/_lib/panel.js clusterRobustMean).
p is one-sided Student-t with (months - 1) df.
PASS = validation mean > 0 AND final mean > 0 AND the final p survives Holm across all 10 at 5%.
Fewer than 30 final trades = NOT TESTABLE.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES
A PASS goes to the page only as "passed on re-used data; paper first" (OWN where it is ours), on
TRACK, after the owner sees the numbers. A FAIL is reported as a FAIL.

NOT TESTABLE HERE, AND WHY
- Shiller's CAPE (Nobel 2013) is a 10-year valuation signal: 6 years of data gives no test.
- Markowitz portfolio choice (Nobel 1990) is a way to size, not a signal.
- Fama-French value needs point-in-time fundamentals, which this desk does not have.
- Option-premium selling needs NSE option bhavcopies: a later study.

CODE CHECKS FIRST (synthetic only)
(1) A planted reversal must be found by D1.
(2) Noise must give at most one chance PASS.
(3) Changing future prices must leave earlier trades identical. This also proves the ML model
    never trains on the future.
