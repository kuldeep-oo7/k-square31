PRE-REGISTRATION -- THE COMBINED MODEL: EVERY NSE DATA FAMILY AND THE DESK'S EQUATIONS IN ONE WALK-FORWARD MODEL
Written 2026-10-13 ~23:30 IST, BEFORE any result of this test was computed. Branch claude/combined-model-2026-10-13.
Run ONCE on real data after the checks in section 7 pass. Judged by protocol v2 (studies/audit-2026-10-04/PROTOCOL.txt).

1. WHY, AND WHAT THIS SAMPLE HAS ALREADY SHOWN
Every single rule tested on this desk failed. Quant funds do not trade one signal: they combine many weak ones and
let the data set the weights (Gu, Kelly and Xiu, Review of Financial Studies 2020: trees and neural nets on 94
characteristics beat any single one). This test does that with every NSE daily data family this desk holds.
HONEST LIMIT: the same 2020-2026 NSE sample was already used to test each family one at a time (atlas, families,
orderflow, ta-atlas, dip-ml, loan-fee). The near misses (SLB borrowing surge t 2.23, loan fee t 1.90, pledge t 1.89)
were noticed on this sample. All families are included below by their earlier definitions, whatever their verdict,
so nothing is picked for having looked good. Even so, a PASS here is weaker than a fresh one: it goes to TRACK as a
paper test first, never to the tabs.
NOT INCLUDED, and why (decided now, not after a result):
- TimesFM / Chronos-2 / Kronos forecasts: they exist only for 100 names, every 10th session, from Jul 2024 (study
  forecast-models-2026-10-13, 0 PASS, rank IC 0.00-0.03). Too short to train on.
- Value and quality (earnings, book value, ROE, debt): needs company financials (NSE XBRL); no reader yet.
- Kalman filter trend: no fixed definition was registered before; trend is covered by P2-P10.

3. TIMING, UNIVERSE, TARGET
- Feature date d = an NSE session. Everything dated d, or earlier, may be used. Entry e = the open of the 2nd CM
  session after d (S[i+2]): one lag for every file, as the atlas and forward-v4 tests used. Exit = the close of the
  5th session counting e (S[i+6]).
- Universe on d: api/_lib/forward-v3.js context(): the top 200 EQ stocks by 60-session median traded value with >= 250
  bars after their last reset (|open/prev close - 1| >= 18%), for the month of e, AND a STK futures row on d (so the
  short side is a futures trade the owner can place), AND a CM bar on d. Price features use bars after the last reset
  only (NaN if too few).
- Target (training only): y = close(exit)/open(e) - 1 minus the equal-weight mean of the same over the universe;
  label = cross-sectional percentile rank of y on d, minus 0.5.

4. FEATURES (computed from data dated <= d; stock features are converted to their cross-sectional percentile rank
   among the universe on d; market features are left raw)
 PRICE AND VOLUME (CM bars; r_k = c_d / c_{d-k} - 1):
  P1 r1  P2 r5  P3 r20  P4 r60  P5 r120  P6 mom12_1 = c_{d-21}/c_{d-252} - 1  P7 dist52 = c_d / max(high, 250) - 1
  P8 c/SMA20 - 1  P9 c/SMA50 - 1  P10 c/SMA200 - 1  P11 RSI(14, Wilder)  P12 IBS = (c-l)/(h-l)  P13 gap = o_d/c_{d-1} - 1
  P14 rv20 = sd(log returns, 20) x sqrt(252)  P15 Parkinson vol 20  P16 rv5/rv60 (volatility clustering, the GARCH idea)
  P17 log(value_d / median value 60)  P18 Amihud 20 = mean(|r|/value)  P19 max daily return 20
  P20 lag-1 autocorrelation of daily log returns, 120 (the springy-dip measure)
  P21 OU z = (c - SMA20)/sd(close, 20)  P22 Hurst exponent, rescaled range on the last 128 log returns,
      slope of log(R/S) on log(n) for n = 8, 16, 32, 64, 128  P23 beta to NIFTY 50, 120 daily returns
 DELIVERY: D1 delivery %  D2 (delivery % - mean 60) / sd 60
 FUTURES: F1 OI_d/OI_{d-1} - 1 (all STK expiries summed)  F2 OI_d/OI_{d-5} - 1
  F3 carry = (nearest-expiry futures close / CM close - 1) x 365 / calendar days to that expiry (expiry >= d+1)
  F4 log(contracts_d / median contracts 60)
 OPTIONS: O1 put OI / call OI  O2 O1_d - O1_{d-5}
 SHORT SELLING: X1 short qty (trade date d) / CM traded qty d
 SLB: L1 open position qty_d / mean of the previous 20 files (NaN if < 10)  L2 open position qty_d / CM traded qty d
  L3 lending fee, annualised: per contract row (qty > 0, end >= d + 7 days) rate = (value/qty)/close_d x 365/days to end;
     qty-weighted mean over the symbol's rows (as the loan-fee study, without its size and universe filters)
 MWPL: W1 futures-equivalent OI / MWPL  W2 W1_d - W1_{d-5}
 MARGIN TRADING: M1 funded qty_d / funded qty 5 files earlier - 1  M2 funded qty_d / mean CM traded qty 20
 INSIDER FILINGS (broadcast date <= d, the last 28 calendar days): I1 log(1 + promoter market-purchase value Rs)
  I2 count of promoter pledge creations  I3 log(1 + director/KMP market-sale value Rs). NaN for a symbol-day when that
  month's pit file is missing or empty.
 MARKET (same for every stock on d; each as a z-score against its own previous 250 values, NaN under 120):
  K1 NIFTY r5  K2 NIFTY r20  K3 desk regime flag (NIFTY close < EMA20 < SMA50: 1, else 0; not z-scored)
  K4 India VIX close  K5 India VIX 5-day change  K6 FII index futures long - short  K7 PRO same  K8 CLIENT same
  K9 DII same  K10 FII index options (call long - call short) - (put long - put short)
  K11 FPI cash buy - sell  K12 mutual fund same  K13 retail same  K14 NIFTY P/E percentile of its previous 750 (raw)

5. MODELS (walk-forward; every setting fixed here)
- Rebalance feature dates: every 5th CM session, starting with the first session on or after 2022-10-03 (so at least
  ~12 months of training from Sep 2021). Entries do not overlap (e_k + 5 sessions = e_{k+1}).
- Training rows: EVERY session's universe rows (not only rebalances) with a finite label whose exit is strictly before
  the entry of the prediction (purge: feature index j <= i_k - 5). Retrained every 4th rebalance (~monthly); a block
  uses the purge of its FIRST rebalance.
- T (trees): sklearn HistGradientBoostingRegressor(max_iter=200, learning_rate=0.05, max_leaf_nodes=15,
  min_samples_leaf=200, l2_regularization=1.0, random_state=0), native NaN.
- R (ridge baseline): stock features only (ranks; NaN -> 0.5), market features dropped; closed-form ridge, alpha = 10,
  on the same rows and schedule.
- Predictions are appended to disk after every retrain block (the laptop has 8 GB; runs can be stopped and resumed;
  a resumed block is recomputed identically from the same seed).

6. TRADES, MEMBERS, VERDICT
- Each rebalance: rank the universe by prediction (n >= 50 names, else skipped); k = floor(n/10).
  Long the top k, short the bottom k, at NSE futures prices proxied by the CM open/close (as every stock study here),
  notional Rs 5 lakh a name, futures charges (api/_lib/shadow-broker.js, by date) both sides, a roll charged if the
  exit is in a later month. A name whose 5-session window breaks (missing bar or reset) is dropped from that rebalance.
- C1 trees long-short: R = 1/2 (mean net of longs + mean net of shorts).
- C2 ridge long-short: same book from R.
- C3 trees long-only: mean over the top k of (net - B), B = universe equal-weight open(e)->close(exit) return.
- Score: monthly mean of rebalance returns; Newey-West t (lag 3); PASS = mean > 0 and t >= 3; PROMISING = mean > 0 and
  t >= 2; FAIL otherwise; NOT TESTABLE under 24 months. Holm across C1-C3; Deflated Sharpe with N = 3.
- Reported, not judged: mean rank IC (Spearman of prediction vs y) per rebalance; turnover; months positive;
  permutation importance of the last trees model (20,000 rows, 3 repeats) to say which families carried weight.
- A PASS goes to TRACK as a forward paper test with these exact settings; nothing reaches the tabs from this run.

9. AMENDMENT A1 -- 2026-10-14, BEFORE any real-data result (only synthetic checks had run)
Prompted by an independent review (K2-Audit-Conclusion-2026-10-14.pdf), which found the desk's Deflated Sharpe omitted
the skewness/kurtosis term and that "PASS" did not require Holm or the DSR. The real-data verdict is tightened here.
One CHECK is loosened, and said so: check 7.1 no longer requires C2 (reason at the end of this section).
- Deflated Sharpe as Bailey and Lopez de Prado (2014), on the monthly series: sr = mean/sd (monthly, not annualised),
  skew g3 and kurtosis g4 (not excess) of the monthly returns, T months;
  SR0 = sqrt(Var[sr over the N = 3 members]) x ((1 - 0.5772) Phi^-1(1 - 1/N) + 0.5772 Phi^-1(1 - 1/(N e)));
  DSR = Phi( (sr - SR0) sqrt(T - 1) / sqrt(1 - g3 sr + (g4 - 1)/4 sr^2) ).
- PASS now needs ALL of: mean > 0, Newey-West t >= 3, Holm significant (alpha 0.05 over C1-C3), and DSR >= 0.95.
  PROMISING = mean > 0 and t >= 2 but not PASS. FAIL otherwise.
- Check 7.1 (synthetic planted edge): its first build let surges overlap, so the planted effect measured -0.48% instead of
  -0.8% (C1 t 3.82 PASS with L1 the top feature, C2 t 2.87). A second build blocked overlaps but cut surge frequency, and
  the top-decile effect measured -0.33% (C1 t 2.64, C2 t 1.11, L1 still the top feature). Both runs are recorded in
  RESULT.txt. Final design of the check, fixed now: surges on a fixed 10% of names per session, chosen so that no name
  surges twice within 6 sessions; the effect measured inside the panel (mean excess of surge names minus others) is
  reported beside the verdicts; the check passes if C1 is PASS by the amended rule and L1 is the top permutation
  feature. C2 (ridge) is reported, not required: a linear model on ranks cannot represent a threshold effect, which the
  first two runs showed.
