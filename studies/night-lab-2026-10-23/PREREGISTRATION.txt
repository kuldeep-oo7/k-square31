2. DESIGN (fixed now)
- DISCOVERY on NSE 2005-01 .. 2020-10 (private release data-oos: CM bhavcopy, F&O futures with OI, index closes).
  CONFIRMATION, once, on NSE 2020-10 .. 2026-10-01 (private release data-research snapshot + data-macro index closes).
  Every member's inputs exist in BOTH periods (CM OHLCV, futures OI, NIFTY), so none is "not confirmable" by design.
- NIFTY open/close: NSE's ind_close_all ("Nifty 50", "CNX Nifty" or "S&P CNX Nifty"); before the first index file in
  data-oos (about 2012), the near-month NIFTY futures open/close from NSE's F&O file (as studies/oos-replication).
- Universe each month: EQ stocks ranked by the median traded value of their last 60 bars before the month's first session,
  with >= 250 bars after their last reset (an open >= 18% from the previous close); the top 200. (SB members keep SB's own
  universe rule from oi-flow-forward.js.)
- A trade or book that crosses a reset is dropped. A stock must trade on every session of its window.
- Costs: calculateNseFuturesCharges (api/_lib/shadow-broker.js, dated) on both legs, quantity ceil(Rs 5 lakh / entry), plus a
  roll round trip per month end crossed. Stops fill at the stop price plus 0.1% slippage (worse for the trade).
- Holidays: a weekday that is not an NSE session in the CM files.

3. MEMBERS (17; "excess" = the trade's net minus the same-side equal-weighted universe return over the same window)
 GROUP S, SHORT BUILD-UP TWEAKS (SB signals exactly as oi-flow-forward.js):
  S1-S4 STOP-LOSS: short at the open; if the day's HIGH reaches open x (1 + y), buy back at that price + 0.1%; otherwise
        at the close. y = 1.0% (S1), 1.5% (S2), 2.0% (S3), 3.0% (S4). Judged on the PAIRED difference against base SB on
        the same trades (section 4).
  S5 MARKET FILTER: SB only when NIFTY's close on d-1 was below its close on d-2. Excess.
  S6 TWO SESSIONS: cover at the close of d+1 instead of d. Excess.
  S7 STRONG SIGNAL: SB only when the share fell >= 3% and futures OI rose >= 6% on d-1. Excess.
 GROUP C, CREATIVE:
  C1 SQUEEZE LONG: a stock shorted by SB on session d that closed ABOVE its open on d (the short lost): BUY at the open of
     d+1, sell at the close of d+1. Excess.
 GROUP P, PUBLISHED IDEAS NEW TO THIS DESK (definitions from the papers' own text where read tonight; daily-data
  adaptations labelled):
  P1 MAX, LONG-SHORT (Bali, Cakici and Whitelaw 2011, JFE 99: the maximum daily return over the past month predicts LOWER
     returns; note: a study of Indian stocks, Studies in Economics and Finance 2018, found no such effect). Each month:
     MAX = the highest close-to-close return over the previous month's sessions; LONG the lowest-MAX tenth, SHORT the
     highest-MAX tenth, open of the month's first session to the close of its last. Raw (market-neutral).
  P2 MAX, LONG ONLY: the lowest-MAX tenth, as P1. Excess. (A buy candidate.)
  P3 HIGH-VOLUME PREMIUM, LONG-SHORT (Gervais, Kaniel and Mingelgrin 2001, JF 56: 50-day intervals, a 1-day formation
     period compared with the 49-day reference period; "among the top (bottom) 10% daily volumes over the whole trading
     interval" = high (low) volume; portfolios formed at the end of the formation day, held 20 trading days).
     Non-overlapping 50-session intervals; LONG high-volume, SHORT low-volume, from the next open for 20 sessions. Raw.
  P4 HIGH-VOLUME, LONG ONLY: as P3, the high-volume stocks. Excess. (A buy candidate.)
  P5 SAME-MONTH SEASONALITY (Heston and Sadka 2008, JFE 87: same-calendar-month returns repeat at annual lags; adaptation:
     annual lags 1-5). Each month: the average of the stock's returns in the same calendar month over the previous 5
     years (all 5 needed); LONG the top tenth, SHORT the bottom tenth for the month. Raw.
  P6 TUG OF WAR, INTRADAY (Lou, Polk and Skouras 2019, JFE 134: "stocks with relatively high overnight returns over the
     last month have, on average, relatively high overnight returns as well as relatively low intraday returns in the
     subsequent month"). Each month: rank by the sum of last month's overnight log returns (open / previous close); every
     session of the month: SHORT the top tenth open-to-close, LONG the bottom tenth open-to-close. Raw.
  P7 TUG OF WAR, OVERNIGHT (same paper, the overnight persistence): the same ranking; every night of the month: LONG the
     top tenth close-to-next-open, SHORT the bottom tenth. Raw.
  P8 PRE-HOLIDAY (idea: Ariel 1990, JF; not re-read tonight): buy NIFTY at the close of the session before a pre-holiday
     session, sell at the pre-holiday session's close. Raw.
  P9 LEAD-LAG (idea: Lo and MacKinlay 1990, RFS; Hou 2007, RFS; not re-read tonight): after a session where NIFTY closed
     >= 1% above (below) its previous close, LONG (SHORT) the smallest third of the universe by traded value at the next
     open, out at that session's close (an equal-weighted basket). Raw.

4. SCORE AND VERDICT
- Each member's trades -> monthly mean of net returns (%), Newey-West t (lag 3). S1-S4: the monthly mean of the PAIRED
  difference (stop member's net minus base SB's net, trade by trade).
- Holm across all 17 at 5%. Full Deflated Sharpe (Bailey and Lopez de Prado 2014, skew and kurtosis) on the monthly series
  with N = 17 + 300 = 317 trials (the 300 counts every earlier test on this desk: the GM board's 186, the macro pod, the
  expiry study, the combined and forecast models and the rest, rounded up).
- DISCOVERY PASS = mean > 0, t >= 3, Holm, DSR >= 0.95 on 2005-2020. CONFIRMED = then, on 2020-2026, mean > 0 and t >= 2.
  A member that is a DISCOVERY PASS and CONFIRMED is a candidate; it goes to TRACK as a forward paper test first, never
  straight to FUTURES. PROMISING = discovery mean > 0 and t >= 2, not a pass. FAIL otherwise.
- POWER AND EXPECTATIONS, stated now: with 317 trials, a lucky t near 3 is expected somewhere; only the confirmation
  separates a real effect from luck. The most likely outcome is that nothing passes both periods.
- Reported, not judged: every member on 2020-2026 regardless of discovery; trades, win rate, worst month; for SB
  members, GM rupees at 20 units.
