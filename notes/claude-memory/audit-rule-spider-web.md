**Why:** on this desk, fixes kept breaking what depended on them, unseen:
- the NOT RECOMMENDED fold opened by itself on the laptop because only the phone was checked;
- short rows broke four simulation checks and would have broken the live NOW text and the health gate;
- a desk build silently undid the paper-test files.

**How to apply:**
- Every audit asks what, why, where from, when, who uses it, and is it right.
- Every fix lists its dependents and checks each one: page (phone AND laptop), TRACK/ledger, health
  gate, tests, simulations, workflows, live script, Sheet, memory.
- After each market close, audit that day's builds against NSE's own end-of-day files and report right
  vs wrong. Written into CLAUDE.md rule 9.
