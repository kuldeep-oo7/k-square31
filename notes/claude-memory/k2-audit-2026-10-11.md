Found and fixed on 11 Oct 2026, each one confirmed in the logs first:
- **Desk builds silently reverted the paper-test files.** desk.yml's "reset to main, then git add docs/"
  committed back its own older docs/paper-rules (9 Oct: the 14:35 build undid the 14:33 update). Now it
  takes main's copy. Commit 6e59f7c.
- **Ledger missed a stop.** Angel's day-low field lagged the last trade (MANKIND 2600 CE, 8 Oct), so the
  health gate failed a build. The last price now always counts. Commit 6ef6b32.
- **FINNIFTY/MIDCPNIFTY risk history was frozen at 1 Oct.** The NSE fill ran only when the copy was
  short; it now also runs when the copy is older than NSE's archive.
- **The paper job (springy-dip.yml) started 6-6.5 h late every day** on GitHub's schedule (14:07-14:32
  IST). desk.yml now starts it on weekday mornings when no run has started that IST day.
- **Night builds (after 00:00 IST) showed a close one day older**, from an old cache. Fixed by PR #2's
  per-run cache key; the 11 Oct rebuild showed the right close.

**PR #2 (cloud):** merged 11 Oct. It adds the out-of-date warning, the per-index reason a put is missing,
and the per-run cache. Its claim that the 6 Oct run was "dropped" was wrong: it ran late.

**Still open**
- The index puts dropped to 0 in some in-session builds on 6 and 8 Oct while all 45 chains were fetched.
  The builds whose fetch exceeded 90 s were the ones with 0. The reason now prints on PUTS; read it in
  Monday's builds.
- page-check's audit flagged 6 futures on 11 Oct as "NSE last" mismatches. The page equalled NSE's
  official close (F&O bhavcopy 9 Oct). NSE's quote answered without closePrice on the runner.
