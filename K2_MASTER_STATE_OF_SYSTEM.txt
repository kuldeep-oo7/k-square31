SECTION 1 — WHAT THE SYSTEM IS
================================================================================

[VERIFIED] 34 modules in api/_lib/, 36 test scripts wired into `npm run check`.
Full suite passes, exit 0.

[VERIFIED] The strategy, as the code actually defines it today:

    strategyId              K2_CONTINUOUS_NSE_STOCK_FUTURES
    strategyVersion         2.0.0
    stepPct                 2       ladder rungs 2% apart, compounding
    slots                   5       five rungs per weekly cohort
    targetPct               2
    stopLossPct             2
    maxHoldingSessions      20      finite risk ceiling
    maxConcurrentPositions  5
    fridayLiquidation       false   <-- positions CARRY across weeks
    fillRequiresTradeThrough true
    costPct                 0.08    round-trip, all-in
    adverseExitSlippagePct  0.02
    developmentRatio        0.60
    validationRatio         0.20

The mechanic: Monday open is the anchor. Five buy rungs are placed at 2%
compounding steps below it (above, for shorts). A filled rung exits on a 2%
target, a 2% stop, or a 20-session time stop, whichever comes first. Positions
carry across weeks; each Monday open rebases the weekly reference price for
P&L attribution.

[VERIFIED] Statutory costs are real and DATED, not a guess. Round-trip charges on
a Rs 7.5 lakh notional, computed from the actual schedule:

    2024-05-15   STT 0.013%    Rs 195.73   = 0.0261% of notional
    2025-01-15   STT 0.020%    Rs 252.73   = 0.0337%
    2026-09-08   STT 0.050%    Rs 482.73   = 0.0644%

    reproduce: node scripts/test-shadow-broker.js

The backtest assumption of costPct 0.08% is therefore CONSERVATIVE at every date
in the sample. This is one of the genuinely strong parts of the build — most
retail backtests use a flat guess and most of them are too low.

[VERIFIED] Portfolio risk enforcement — and a correction to an earlier finding.
`portfolio-risk.js` now defaults these to TRUE:

    requireCompleteMacro     true
    requireSector            true
    strictOrderValidation    true

An earlier audit of mine recorded these as shipping false-by-default. That was
true then and is NOT true now; commit 86467f8 flipped them. Flagged here because
the old finding is still sitting in three of the documents listed above.

SECTION 2 — WHAT IS MEASURED AND TRUE
================================================================================

All of Section 2 comes from one study, reproducible end to end:

    node scripts/panel-study.js --fetch     (populates a 5-year bar cache)
    node scripts/panel-study.js             (long side)
    node scripts/panel-study.js --side short

97 NSE large caps, 2021-09-15 to 2026-09-15, current continuous engine, splits
cut on shared calendar dates with labels straddling a boundary purged.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  THE LONG LADDER HAS NO CONFIRMED EDGE AT ANY GEOMETRY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[MEASURED] 24 stop/target geometries tested. ZERO survive validation. ALL 24
validation cells are negative.

    shipped 2.0% stop / 2.0% target :  dev +0.0674%   validation -0.1529% (t -1.14)
    best on development 3% / 4%     :  dev +0.2808%   validation -0.1742% (t -0.68)
                                       (t 2.18)

The development surface is smooth and monotone — wider stops look better — which
is exactly what makes it seductive. It does not survive contact with unseen data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2  THE SHORT LADDER HAS A MEASURED NEGATIVE EXPECTANCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[MEASURED] This is the single most statistically unambiguous result in five years
of this data, and it is bad news. All 24 short geometries lose in ALL THREE
splits:

    stop/target   development           validation          final test
    0.75 / 1.5    -0.2281%  t -11.12    -0.2165%  t -4.26   -0.1564%  t -3.15
    1.00 / 1.5    -0.2631%  t -10.28    -0.2192%  t -3.45   -0.1501%  t -2.46
    2.00 / 2.0    -0.3560%  t  -7.73    -0.2632%  t -2.12   -0.0932%  t -0.80
    3.00 / 4.0    -0.5558%  t  -5.58    -0.2303%  t -0.88   -0.0718%  t -0.31

The BEST short configuration on development still loses at t -9.79. A t of -11 is
not noise.

The cause is structural, not a tuning problem: the short ladder sells rallies
into a market with positive long-run drift. Widening the stop makes it worse
(-0.23% at 0.75% stop, -0.56% at 3% stop), because a wider stop simply holds the
losing short longer.

Note the asymmetry against the long side: long geometries improve as the stop
widens, short geometries deteriorate. Same drift term, opposite signs. That is
strong evidence the ladder is measuring market direction, not any property of
the entry rule.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3  NO CROSS-SECTIONAL CONDITIONER RESCUES IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[MEASURED] Six standard factors profiled by quintile, both sides. ZERO are stable.

    12-1 momentum, 5-day reversal, 21-day relative strength vs the cross-section,
    distance from the 52-week high, ATR%, 5d/60d volume ratio

Every one flips sign between development and validation. The two strongest on
development (5-day reversal t 3.21, distance-from-high t 3.42) are the two that
flip hardest. That is the textbook signature of overfitting: in a search over
correlated features, the biggest in-sample number is the most overfit one.

Ranking the names does not fix a rule with no expectancy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4  THE UNIVERSE IS SURVIVORSHIP-BIASED — AND SO IS EVERY BACKTEST IN THIS REPO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[MEASURED] This is the most consequential finding in the document, because it is
not about the ladder. It is about every historical number the system produces.

Buy-and-hold control, each name compounded on its own series, against the real
index over identical windows:

                      mean/name   median/name   NIFTY 500 (^CRSLDX)
    development        +169.9%      +105.9%          +59.1%
    validation           +1.7%        -1.1%           -1.7%
    final test           +0.4%        -2.5%           -3.4%

The medians track the index closely in the two later windows. Development does
not: +105.9% against +59.1%.

The reason is that `universe()` returns TODAY'S Nifty 500 constituent list, and
the backtest applies it backwards across five years. Names are in the sample
because they are large caps in 2026. The ones that fell out are simply absent.

That bias is present in weeklyCycle, in rule-study, in the ML training labels,
and in every dossier listed at the top of this file. Nobody has measured its
size. Fixing it needs point-in-time constituent history, which the repo does not
have and which is not free to obtain.

WHAT THIS MEANS PRACTICALLY: treat every development-window figure in every
document in this folder as optimistic by an unmeasured amount. Compare against a
real index, never against the internal universe.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5  PARTICIPATION RISES WITH VOLATILITY; P&L DOES NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[MEASURED] Fill rate by trailing-ATR decile, shipped config:

    decile  1  (ATR 0.86-1.69%)    7.9% of rungs filled   avg cohort net +0.0037%
    decile  5  (ATR 2.28-2.47%)   14.8%                          -0.0047%
    decile 10  (ATR 3.92%+)       29.5%                          -0.0016%

    overall 0.812 fills per cohort of 5 rungs

The fill-rate slope is steep and monotone. The P&L column is flat noise, and the
highest-participation decile is negative.

SECTION 3 — WHAT IS MEASURED AND FALSE (DEAD ENDS, DO NOT REVISIT)
================================================================================

[MEASURED] TUNING THE LADDER GEOMETRY. The surface is measured across 24 cells on
both sides. It is dead. Do not sweep it again.

[MEASURED] VOLATILITY-NORMALISED ENTRY GRID (previously queued as "A-4"). It would
work mechanically — it would flatten the 7.9%-to-29.5% participation slope in

fills x zero edge = zero, plus more cost and more tail exposure. Do not build it.

highest-value fix). Its thresholds were calibrated against a friday-flat engine
that no longer exists. Re-derived on the current engine, the premise it rested on
is false — see RETRACTION 2 below. Not restored, and should not be.

[MEASURED] CROSS-SECTIONAL RANKING ON THE SIX STANDARD FACTORS. See 2.3.

SECTION 4 — WHAT THIS BUILD GETS GENUINELY RIGHT
================================================================================

This section matters. The strategy has no measured edge, but the machinery
around it is better than most of what retail and a fair amount of what
institutions run. These are real and they should not be lost in a rewrite.

[VERIFIED] UNVALIDATED FEATURES WERE DELETED, NOT FLAGGED OFF. Commit 86467f8
removed STAGED_EXIT_3_TIER, depthProfile, exitPolicy and comparePolicies
outright, and scripts/verify-ci-flags.js now ASSERTS their absence:

    "Strategy-parity test passed: unvalidated staged exits and promised holding
     horizons are absent; continuous shadow-only policy remains canonical"

A shipped, user-visible feature was removed because it was not measured. In three
rounds of auditing this repo, that is the single rarest and best thing in it.
The general principle it embodies — every real fix changes a default, every fake
fix adds a flag — is the one to keep.

[VERIFIED] DATED STATUTORY COSTS. See Section 1. Conservative at every date.

[VERIFIED] PURGED THREE-WAY SPLITS. Development / validation / final test, cut on
input dates rather than on future-dependent label counts, with boundary-
straddling cohorts purged. The final test is explicitly marked "never used by
verdict, ranking, sizing or thresholds".

[VERIFIED] HONEST UNCERTAINTY LABELLING. The block standard error is named
NON_OVERLAPPING_FIVE_WEEK_BLOCK_MEANS_RESEARCH_APPROXIMATION rather than being
passed off as exact. `annualisedSharpe` is set to null with
`annualisationValid: false` and a comment explaining that overlapping cohort
labels make sqrt(52) a lie. Systems that fake this are the norm.

[VERIFIED] A FINITE RISK CEILING. maxHoldingSessions 20 and
maxConcurrentPositions 5 replaced an engine that could carry a losing position
indefinitely.

[VERIFIED] THE LIVE RULE AND THE MEASURED RULE NOW AGREE. An earlier audit of
mine found the portal deriving stops from ATR (observed 1.42%, 1.55%) while the
backtest measured a flat 2.0% — meaning every published statistic described a
different strategy from the one being traded. That divergence is CLOSED.
recommendation.js:260-262 and :443-445 now both read stopLossPct and targetPct
straight from config; atrPct is still computed but its only remaining use is
line 594, where it is passed through for display. No live path overrides the
config stops. This was the highest-ranked defect in the previous audit and it
has been fixed.

[VERIFIED] GAP-AWARE EXITS. Separate handling for stop-gap, target-gap and the
STOP_FIRST_DAILY_AMBIGUITY case where a daily bar touches both barriers — and it
resolves that ambiguity conservatively, against the strategy.

[MEASURED] THE SELECTION GATE REFUSES ALMOST EVERYTHING. Across 97 names x 2
sides it approves 3:

    SBILIFE    long L1   validation 23 fills +0.087%   final 21 fills +0.557%
    TORNTPHARM long L1   validation 25 fills +0.793%   final 18 fills +0.166%
    TVSMOTOR   long L2   validation 11 fills +0.102%   final  5 fills -1.284%

    ZERO short ladders approved.

Its default behaviour is "do not trade". For a rule with no measured edge that is
the correct behaviour, and it is the reason the absence of edge has not cost
money. Be clear about what this does and does not show: 3 approvals out of 194 is
roughly what chance predicts for a four-condition conjunction, so the gate has
NOT demonstrated skill at picking names. It has demonstrated appropriate
reluctance, which is worth more than it sounds.

SECTION 5 — WHAT IS STILL BROKEN, RANKED
================================================================================

RANK 1 [OPEN] SURVIVORSHIP BIAS IN THE UNIVERSE. Section 2.4. It affects every
    historical number this repo has ever produced, including every figure in
    every dossier listed at the top of this document. Nothing else on this list
    contaminates as much.

RANK 2 [OPEN] AN UNTRAINED FORMULA HOLDS A HARD VETO.
    metaLabelConfidence (advanced-quant.js:448) is a hand-written function of
    hurst, Kaufman ER, VPIN and trend state. Lopez de Prado's meta-labelling is a
    TRAINED SECONDARY CLASSIFIER fitted on primary-model outcomes. This is not
    that. It is a guess with veto power: recommendation.js:280 feeds it into
    enforceHardVetoes and returns early on a veto. An unfitted formula is
    currently able to kill any trade for reasons nobody has validated.

RANK 3 [OPEN] CASH BARS FOR A FUTURES STRATEGY. The research path prices on
    equity cash bars; the strategy trades single-stock futures. Basis, roll cost
    and the expiry calendar are absent from every backtest number.

RANK 4 [OPEN] NO LIQUIDITY OR CAPACITY MODEL. No ADV filter, no open-interest
    filter, no spread filter, no capacity curve. There is no answer to "how much
    size can this take before it stops working", which is the first question any
    desk asks.

RANK 5 [OPEN] KELLY IS COMPUTED AND THROWN AWAY. recommendation.js:289 computes
    calculateKellyFraction, then line 295 sizes off convictionPct instead:
    finalSizeMultiplier = clamp(0.20 + 0.20*clamp(conviction/70), 0.10, 0.40).
    Either use it or delete it; a computed-and-ignored number is how a system
    starts believing things it has not done.

RANK 6 [OPEN] THE 8-PILLAR SCORE IS DISPLAY-ONLY. It is rendered and it does not
    enter any decision.

RANK 7 [OPEN] VPIN IS NOT NORMALISED. Bulk-volume classification should normalise
    by the standard deviation of price change and be read as a percentile against
    its own history. It currently is not, so its absolute level is not meaningful
    across names or regimes — and it feeds the Rank 3 veto.

SECTION 6 — RETRACTIONS OF MY OWN EARLIER CLAIMS
================================================================================

These appear as findings in COUNTER_AUDIT_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW.txt and are wrong.

[RETRACTED] "1% stop / 3% target: +0.1894% per fill, cluster-robust t = 3.14,
    4/4 folds positive."
    Does not replicate. On the current engine 1%/3% gives development +0.0114%,
    validation -0.1504% (t -1.65), final test +0.0345%. The original came from
    the old friday-flat engine with unbounded holds. I carried a number across
    an engine change without re-deriving it.

[RETRACTED] "Hit rate and P&L move in OPPOSITE directions, monotonically, across
    every cell." (Appendix B-0.)
    False on the current engine. With a 20-session time stop they move in the
    SAME direction: wider stops raise both hit rate (27% -> 64%) and per-fill
    P&L. The inversion was an artifact of forced Friday liquidation, which booked
    losers before mean reversion could act. This retraction is what killed the
    CVaR rung gate.

[RETRACTED] "lowerPct >= 45 is a regression introduced in 86467f8."
    It is not. My tail-gate version never left my own working tree, so nothing
    was removed from the shipped line of development. This was parallel
    development, not a regression. Moot now in any case.

[RETRACTED] "portfolio-risk.js ships three safety flags defaulting false."
    True when written, false now. 86467f8 flipped all three to true.

[RETRACTED] "The live site derives stops from ATR while the backtest measures a
    flat 2.0% — the backtest describes a strategy nobody trades."
    True when observed, false now. Both code paths read the config values; the
    ATR-derived clamp formula no longer exists. I had this queued as the top
    open defect in this very document until I checked the source rather than
    trusting my own earlier finding. If the deployed portal still shows
    ATR-derived stops, that is a stale build, not a code divergence.

[RETRACTED] "The market rose and the strategy still lost."
    Validation and final test were slightly DOWN markets (-1.7%, -3.4% on the
    Nifty 500). The durable claim is the weaker one: no geometry shows a
    confirmed edge.

[RETRACTED] "Chance would approve about 10 of 194; the gate rejects more than
    chance." Wrong arithmetic, and tilted in my favour. The gate is a four-
    condition conjunction plus a validation confirmation, so its joint false-
    positive rate is nearer 1-2% and expected chance approvals are 2 to 4.
    Observing 3 is exactly chance.

Two defects were also found in code I wrote this session and fixed before commit:
deflatedTStat(null, N) returned -2.14 because Number(null) is 0; and
quintileProfile called a conditioner stable when its spreads merely shared a
sign, which let spreads of -0.020 and -0.011 read as a finding.

SECTION 7 — THE GAP BETWEEN THIS AND THE GOAL
================================================================================

The stated goal is a system built on: company fundamentals first, then deep
technical analysis, then mathematics and statistics, then quant finance, then
data science, machine learning and deep learning — working on any market, for
pure trading.

Here is the honest position against that.

WHAT EXISTS AND WORKS: the execution and accounting layer. Dated statutory costs,
a shadow broker with contract notes, a ledger that reconciles, portfolio risk
with sector caps and a kill switch, purged splits, honest uncertainty labelling,
and now a panel harness that can detect its own failure. This is the plumbing,
and it is good plumbing.

WHAT EXISTS BUT IS NOT LOAD-BEARING: fundamentals (fetched, displayed, not in the
decision), the 8-pillar score (display-only), Kelly (computed, discarded), chart
patterns and the ML upside model (present, but the gate that matters is a
hand-written formula).

WHAT IS MISSING, AND IS THE ACTUAL GAP: a reason to be long THIS name, THIS week.
The system currently has a price-mechanical entry rule and no view. Fundamentals,
cross-sectional ranking, regime conditioning and residualisation are all absent
from the decision. Section 2 is the proof that the mechanical rule cannot carry
the weight on its own, and Section 2.3 is the proof that bolting standard factors
onto it does not fix that either.

THE ONE UNTESTED STRUCTURAL IDEA LEFT: cross-sectional residualisation with a
beta hedge. Strip the market factor out of each name, ladder the residual, hedge
the beta with an index future. The argument for it is Section 2.2 — long and
short geometries move in opposite directions with the drift term, which says the
drift is what the rule is measuring. Remove the drift and you find out whether
anything idiosyncratic is underneath. It requires benchmark data in the research
path, which does not exist yet. It may well also fail. But it is the only
remaining idea that changes the structure rather than the parameters, and
parameters are measured dead.

A REALISTIC SEQUENCE FROM HERE:
    1. Quantify the survivorship bias. Even a rough correction tells you how
       much of the entire historical record to discount, and it gates the
       credibility of steps 2 and 3.
    2. Either train the meta-label properly or strip its veto. An unfitted
       formula holding kill authority is the most dangerous single line in the
       decision path.
    3. Build the residual + beta-hedge variant and run it through panel-study.
       Accept the answer either way.
    4. If it fails: the ladder is not the vehicle, and the right move is to keep
       the execution layer and replace the signal entirely — which is where
       fundamentals and ML belong, and where they have never actually been tried.

SECTION 8 — DECISIONS THAT ARE YOURS, NOT MINE
================================================================================

I have not made any of these. Each changes the trading rule, and the trading rule
is yours.

    1. DISABLE THE SHORT SIDE. Strongest recommendation here and the only one
       backed by significance rather than by absence of it (Section 2.2). The
       gate currently rejects every short candidate so nothing is bleeding, but
       weeklyCycle still evaluates the side and verdict() will select it if one
       ever passes.

    2. WHETHER THE PLAN SHOULD REFUSE TO EMIT while the panel reports
       NO_CONFIRMED_EDGE. I deliberately did NOT wire panelVerdict into the live
       path — it is computed and reported only. Wiring it in would probably stop
       all trading, and adding it switched-off-by-default would be exactly the
       fake-fix pattern this whole audit exists to catch. So it is a decision,
       not a default.

    3. stopLossPct. Stays at 2.0. No geometry has a confirmed edge, so there is
       no measured basis to move it in any direction.
