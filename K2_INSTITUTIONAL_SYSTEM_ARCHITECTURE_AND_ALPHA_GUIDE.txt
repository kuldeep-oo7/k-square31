3.1 WHY GUARANTEED 100% PROFIT IS MATHEMATICALLY IMPOSSIBLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Federal Reserve SR 11-7 and quantitative model governance mandate absolute honesty:
No algorithm, hedge fund, or mathematical model in existence can guarantee 100% 
profit certainty. Anyone promising a "sure profit" without risk is perpetrating 
a financial scam.

Market prices follow stochastic differential equations with Brownian motion:
  dS_t = mu * S_t * dt + sigma * S_t * dW_t

The random term (dW_t) represents unexpected Black Swan events (war, central bank 
rate shocks, natural disasters) that cannot be predicted in advance.
